# 2026-10-25 00:58 — постановка нового выхода считывателя: отклик по рамкам `z13_region_readout_v1`

↑ [[06 Журнал/Дни/2026-10-25|2026-10-25]]

*Действие.* Replace the internal readout output: use the existing target-response field to score actual detection regions, rather than overwrite the predicted box center with one peak. Test this zero-training prototype on exact one-step assignments before any closed-loop run.

*Метод.* All high-score detections are projected to the unchanged query retinal crop. A region is supported only if its center is in the existing hex field and its rectangle contains retinal columns. Its logit is the mean standardized target response inside the rectangle; NULL logit0; joint softmax over supported regions+NULL. New fused cost=(1-alpha)*old_cost+alpha*(1-det_conf*region_probability); unsupported costs unchanged. Exact alpha0 baseline. Same maps, support, calibration grid and association rules for C/D/R1. Geometry control uses only a fixed negative squared-distance field around the original prediction, replacing the response field in the identical decoder.

*Калибровка и критерии до результата.* For each arm independently, choose alpha from[0,.25,.5,1] on20fit only by mean-video(beneficial-harmful)/known, original alpha0 wins ties. Freeze choices before opening evaluate26 outcome rows. All four grids reported. No training of core or readout weights, no best seed or quota search. On all26 reused training evaluation videos, D net action frequency versus unchanged baseline must have mean>=0.02 and lower paired-video95%CI>0. D must also beat geometry-only, R1 and C region decoders with all lower95%CI>0. All conditions required; other comparisons cannot rescue. Unknown outcomes reported separately and never assigned invented labels.

*Отличие от испытанного.* The earlier detection3 gate replaced centers and evaluated center error, and learned-association/rankers selected existing response peaks. Neither tested this detection-region output or its effect on the complete assignment. The failed peak-center assignment reserve>=2% is preserved for that action; a cost-vector action has a different expressible output and is tested directly here. This is not a new detector or a claim that the surrounding matching algorithm improved.

*Контраргумент.* Cost calibration and proposal geometry may explain the entire effect without FlyVis. Region means may dilute the target, share response between overlapping boxes, or merely reject all detections. The no-FlyVis geometry/R1 controls are mandatory. Individual shadow actions do not capture simultaneous interactions, feedback or later matching stages, so a positive result is only a gate for separate closed-loop verification.
