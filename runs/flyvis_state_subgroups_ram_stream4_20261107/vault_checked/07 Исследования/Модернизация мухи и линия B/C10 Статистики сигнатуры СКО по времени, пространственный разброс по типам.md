# C10 Статистики сигнатуры: СКО по времени, пространственный разброс по типам

↑ [[07 Исследования/Модернизация мухи и линия B/Модернизация мухи и линия B|Модернизация мухи и линия B]]

## Суть
The current fly signature keeps only first-order statistics. For each of the 34 output types it stores the mean activity pooled over 19 macro-columns and averaged over the first/last K=5 frames of a track life, and over the integration sub-steps inside each frame (646 dims; EyeBank._by_type averages over steps, build_fly_signatures.mean_parts averages over K). The proposal adds second-order statistics per type: (a) the temporal SD of the response within the K-frame window and across sub-steps, which captures how strongly motion-sensitive T4/T5 and Tm/TmY types fluctuate rather than their level; (b) the spatial spread across columns, i.e. the SD or variation over the 721 columns or over the 19 macro-cells, which is translation-invariant within the eye and therefore tolerant to box misalignment after occlusion. This is exactly the descriptor form used by Kuklev 2026 for a frozen FlyVis (mean, temporal SD and spatial variation per type) and the classical recipe of texture statistics (Portilla-Simoncelli; Gram/second-order pooling).

## Источники
1) Kuklev D., arXiv 2609.24565 (Sep 2026), https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24565: descriptor of 57 types x {mean, temporal SD, spatial variation} x 8 motion trajectories = 1,368 dims on a frozen FlyVis. 2) Portilla J., Simoncelli E., 'A parametric texture model based on joint statistics of complex wavelet coefficients', IJCV 2000 (texture identity from marginal and second-order filter statistics). 3) Gatys L. et al., 'Texture synthesis using convolutional neural networks', NeurIPS 2015 (Gram statistics as a position-invariant texture code). 4) McLaughlin N. et al., 'Recurrent convolutional network for video-based person re-identification', CVPR 2016 (temporal pooling of per-frame features for video ReID). 5) FlyVis: Lappalainen J. et al., Nature 2024, https://github.com/TuragaLab/flyvis. Project code: scripts/build_fly_signatures.py, scripts/eval_fly_reid.py, tracking/foveated_eyes.py.

## Относимость к проекту
Targets N11 (the fly signature for re-identification after track loss) and the related IDSw. Measured so far: the signature carries identity (+0.14 AP over geometry), and topology matters for it (+0.044 AP versus rewired). Over the 721-facet input, however, it adds only +0.006 AP on test (+0.010 at night), and nothing at HOTA. The mean over K frames and sub-steps is the statistic least suited to a dynamical, motion-sensitive circuit, because it removes exactly the temporal structure that distinguishes processed from raw input. Spatial spread per type is also a cheap route to invariance to box drift, which is the typical failure after partial occlusion. It is complementary to C9 (active viewing) and C11 (trainable readout).

## Пересечение с чужими работами
Kuklev 2026 already uses mean + temporal SD + spatial variation per cell type of a frozen FlyVis as a descriptor, with matched receptor baselines and rewiring nulls. Second-order and texture statistics of filter banks are classical (Portilla-Simoncelli 2000, Gatys 2015). Temporal pooling in video ReID is standard. cannot_claim: the statistic set itself, and the claim that second-order statistics of FlyVis responses form a descriptor. At most, the measured contribution of these statistics to MOT re-identification with an information-matched input control and a connectome null can be reported, as a measurement rather than a method novelty.

Real sheets: fly 0.505 AP versus 0.415 for a 365,921-param CNN. Rewiring costs: -0.318 (degree-preserving), -0.271 (type/sign-preserving), -0.356 (Erdos-Renyi). ## Уровень измерения
Descriptor level only: matching accuracy and AP (Kuklev), texture synthesis or perception (Portilla-Simoncelli, Gatys), CMC rank for video ReID. Nothing at HOTA/IDF1.

## Согласованный контроль
Kuklev: a receptors-only descriptor from the same photoreceptor samples beats the fly on held-out data (0.894 vs 0.835). Whether the receptors got the identical three statistics is plausible but not confirmed from the retrieved text [uncertain]. Graph nulls show large wiring effects in his task. Classical texture work: no information-matched or graph controls (not applicable). In our project the control must apply the same statistics to the 721-facet eye input (hexin) and to the rewired-connectome signatures.

## Стоимость у нас
Level (a), zero-GPU from the existing cache. runs/fly_signatures (140 videos, 209,486 lives, first_fly/last_fly 34x19 float16) and runs/fly_signatures_null (same layout, rewired connectome) give per-type spatial SD and coefficient of variation across the 19 macro-cells (34 extra dims), and the same for hexin (721 facets pooled to the same 19 cells). Run through the existing ladder in scripts/eval_fly_reid.py (logistic regression on |a-b|, video bootstrap, dark/light strata). This takes CPU minutes. Temporal SD and within-macro-cell spatial spread are not in the cache, because only means over K frames and sub-steps are stored. Level (b), rebuild. Extend build_fly_signatures.py to also store the per-type temporal SD (over K frames x sub-steps) and the per-type spatial SD over the 721 columns (plus the matching hexin statistics). Then rebuild the intact signatures (previous cost 18.0 + 0.76 GPU-process-hours summed for 140 + 7 videos) and, for the null control, the rewired ones (20.0 + 1.09). This is about 40 GPU-hours summed, a few hours of wall time with parallel processes per the plan. Level (c): the C9 offline re-drive produces these statistics at no extra cost.

## Ожидаемый эффект
Level (a): about 0. The macro-cell SD is a deterministic function of the stored 646-dim mean vector. A linear head on |a-b| can use it only as a mild nonlinearity, so we expect less than +0.005 AP on fly-alone and about 0 over the input. Level (b): temporal SD is new information. Fly-alone AP (0.582 test) may rise noticeably. Over the input the gain is expected to stay small (0 to +0.01 AP) [uncertain: extrapolation, not measured], because the input also gets its own temporal SD, and in both Kuklev and our project the receptors or input win as a standalone descriptor (-0.052 AP here). The best chance is at night, where the fly's increment is already larger (+0.010). HOTA: no change is expected, since pair AP barely moves stitching HOTA (geometry-only 59.283 versus appearance 59.347, CI through zero).

## Риски
(1) Adding several statistics at once breaks the one-condition rule, so add them one at a time (mean -> +temporal SD -> +spatial SD), each with the matched input statistic. (2) Temporal SD over 5 frames (about 167 ms at 30 Hz) is dominated by ego-motion and box jitter. It can leak geometry and motion into the 'appearance' signature, so control for it with the input's temporal SD and keep geometry features in every head. (3) Dimensionality: the learned metric on full vectors already lost to cosine (test 0.6632 for the learned L_app_hexin_fly), so tune regularization on dev only. (4) float16 storage and a larger npz: the rebuild must keep the same tracker, retina and seed so that only the statistic changes. (5) The rebuild runs on the GPU next to possible training, so use Idle priority per plan section 9. (6) Overlap with Kuklev goes into cannot_claim. The detector stays frozen and is not affected.
