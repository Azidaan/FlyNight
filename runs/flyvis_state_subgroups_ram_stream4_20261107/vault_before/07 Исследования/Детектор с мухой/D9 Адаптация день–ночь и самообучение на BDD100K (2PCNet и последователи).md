# D9 Адаптация день–ночь и самообучение на BDD100K (2PCNet и последователи)

↑ [[07 Исследования/Детектор с мухой/Детектор с мухой|Детектор с мухой]]

> Доменная адаптация день→ночь и учитель–ученик на BDD100K 2023–2026 (2PCNet, DAI-Net и др.), приросты ночной AP; применимость при наличии ночной разметки (у нас она есть) — дают ли они что-то сверх обучения на размеченной ночи; роль биофронт-энда.

## Суть
Day-to-night unsupervised domain adaptive detection (DN-DAOD) trains on labelled daytime images plus unlabelled night images: a mean-teacher produces night pseudo-labels for a student, helped by physics-style day-to-night image synthesis (NightAug, ISP degradation, noise and headlight flare), class-balanced object mixing and adaptive pseudo-label thresholds. On the BDD100K day/night split the field moved from 41.1 AP50 (source-only Faster R-CNN) to 46.4 (2PCNet, CVPR 2023) and 50.9 (Debiased Teacher, ICCV 2025). The whole setting assumes night labels are absent; our project already trains on 564 labelled night videos (111,895 labelled night frames, 3.4-4x the unlabelled night pool of 27,971-32,998 images used in these papers), so the question is not adaptation but which of the ingredients survive when labelled night data are present. The evidence says the adaptation machinery itself adds roughly nothing over a fair supervised oracle; what may transfer is physics-based night synthesis for rare classes and confidence-aware handling of low-scored true positives.

## Место мухи
Adaptation as such has no slot for the fly, because the problem it solves (no night labels) is absent here. Three roles remain meaningful, in order of plausibility. (A) Physically grounded day-to-night generator for labelled day data, i.e. the fly analogue of 2PCNet NightAug, ISP-Teacher ISP-DTM, DAI-Net Dark-ISP and Debiased Teacher DNDT (all are hand-made physics of darkness: luminance scaling, sensor/photon noise, glare/flare, blur). The project's retina-lamina model already contains a photoreceptor stage with luminance adaptation; driving it with a day image scaled down to night luminance plus Poisson photon noise gives a night-like photoreceptor/lamina response with the day labels intact. This is useful only through what the detector sees, so the augmentation must be rendered back to image space (or applied to the fly input channel if a fly channel is ever added to the detector). It targets rare classes: day train has 9,246 motorcycle, 15,234 rider and 20,941 bicycle boxes versus 2,710 / 4,319 / 6,632 at night. (B) Domain-invariant front-end: L1/L2 lamina cells and the photoreceptor adaptation perform contrast normalisation, which is the biological version of Retinex-style reflectance (DAI-Net) and of illumination-invariant features; the measured fact that retina-lamina removes the fly's own night failure is the argument. Concretely, use the retina-lamina (not T4/T5) output as an extra input channel or as an auxiliary feature-alignment target for the YOLO neck (the DINO Teacher idea of aligning student features with a frozen, condition-robust encoder, with the frozen encoder being retina-lamina->FlyVis on foveae instead of DINOv2). (C) Teacher-side judge for night pseudo-labels on the unlabelled 30 Hz frames (see D12): the role of Debiased Teacher's ConCal and CoS's adaptive IoU-informed thresholding, i.e. deciding which low-confidence night boxes are real; T4/T5 motion evidence over the preceding frames plus lamina contrast form the judge's features. What from the fly: photoreceptor + L1/L2/L3 (role A, B), T4/T5 and type-wise readout of 34 types x 19 macrocolumns (role C). Why it is sensible: the fly's photoreceptor adaptation is the one component of the project with direct physiological grounding in light-level adaptation, and it has a measured night-specific effect; the T4/T5 signal is the only temporally grounded evidence of object presence available to a pseudo-labeller.

## Какую потерю закрывает
Mainly THRESHOLD, secondarily rare-class night recall, not BLINDNESS. Evidence from the project: blindness (4.5%) is equal day and night, so there is no extra night appearance gap at the candidate level for adaptation to close; threshold losses are 21.0% at night vs 15.5% by day with a +0.098 score deficit, i.e. a calibration/score-scale shift. The DA literature targets exactly this: CoS's adaptive IoU-informed thresholding aims to 'avoid overlooking potential true positives', Debiased Teacher's ConCal recalibrates per-class pseudo-label thresholds, 2PCNet's second phase lets low-confidence proposals shape training. In a supervised regime the threshold mechanism is addressed by training on more night-like positives (role A) rather than by pseudo-label logic. Rare classes: per-class AP50 (3 seeds, baseline_summary.csv) shows car at parity (day 0.8172, night 0.8163) and truck at parity (0.4225 vs 0.4310), while rider (0.4006 -> 0.2753), bus (0.5002 -> 0.3848), motorcycle (0.5433 -> 0.2726) and bicycle (0.4651 -> 0.2261) carry 97% of the summed per-class night gap (0.750 of 0.772; mean night mAP50 gap 0.0965). This pattern is what class-aware DA modules (Debiased Teacher CDRC class-balanced mixing, CAT inter-class augmentation) are built for, and in a labelled setting it is a class-imbalance-at-night problem. BIRTH (age 0: 31% vs 68%): not addressed by any DA method (all are still-image); only role C via temporal pseudo-labels touches it. SMALL: 2PCNet's student-scaling raised APs by about 6% relative (8.6 -> 9.1 within its ablation) - a curriculum on scale that is cheap to transfer. OCCLUSION at night and OVEREXPOSURE: only indirectly, via flare/glare synthesis (Debiased Teacher reports noise and flare modelling as the most useful parts on real night data).

## Пересечение с чужими работами
Day-to-night synthesis with physics priors is crowded: NightAug (2PCNet 2023), ISP-DTM (ISP-Teacher 2024), Dark-ISP + Retinex reflectance (DAI-Net 2024), DNDT noise/flare/darkening (Debiased Teacher 2025), GAN translation (ForkGAN, CoMoGAN, AAAI 2026 hallucination-suppressed translation), Fourier amplitude swap (FDA; Dutta 2022). Teacher-student with adaptive thresholds is standard (2PCNet, CoS, ConCal, CAT, ALDI). Feature alignment to a frozen robust encoder exists (DINO Teacher 2025 with DINOv2). Insect-inspired night enhancement exists outside detection (Warrant and colleagues' spatio-temporal summation models of nocturnal insect vision for low-light video [uncertain]). No work found that uses a connectome-constrained fly model (FlyVis) as a day-to-night generator, an invariant front-end, a frozen alignment target or a pseudo-label judge for detection. cannot_claim: 'first physics-based day-to-night augmentation', 'first teacher-student for night', 'first adaptive night threshold', 'domain adaptation result' (we have labels, so nothing here is UDA), and any gain over 2PCNet/Debiased Teacher numbers (different detector, data split and label regime - not comparable). Claimable only if it works: a fly photoreceptor/lamina model used as the night-rendering or alignment component, tested against a non-biological physics transform with the same inputs.

## Результаты у авторов
All BDD100K numbers below are AP at IoU 0.5 on the BDD100K night validation images (still images from the 100k detection set, not MOT video), Faster R-CNN ResNet-50 at shorter side 600 px, Detectron2, no seeds or spread reported. 2PCNet (Table 1; day 36,728 labelled, night 32,998 unlabelled, val 4,707): source-only lower bound 41.1, oracle trained on labelled night only 46.2, DA Faster 41.3, TDD 34.6, UMT 36.2, AT 38.5, 2PCNet 46.4 (+5.3 over source-only, +0.2 over night-only oracle). Per class 2PCNet vs oracle: person 54.4/52.1, rider 30.8/35.0, car 73.1/73.6, truck 53.8/53.5, bus 55.2/54.8, motorcycle 37.5/36.0, bicycle 44.5/41.8, traffic light 49.4/52.2, traffic sign 65.2/63.3. COCO-style AP: lower 22.1, upper 23.9, 2PCNet 23.5 (below oracle). Ablation (100k iterations): plain mean teacher 31.7 (below source-only, error propagation), + two-phase consistency 45.2, + NightAug 45.8, + student-scaling 46.4 (APs 8.2 -> 8.6 -> 9.1), + domain classifier 44.5 (adversarial alignment hurts). SHIFT (synthetic): lower 41.6, oracle 47.0, 2PCNet 49.1. Debiased Teacher (ICCV 2025, Table 1; re-split: 36,728 day, 27,971 night train, 3,929 night val): source 41.1, oracle 46.2 (copied from 2PCNet), SADA 39.4, MIC 39.8, 2PCNet 46.4 (orig) / 47.6 (re-run), CoS 49.4 / 48.4, SOCCER 44.0, ISP-Teacher 48.8 / 49.2, Debiased Teacher 50.9; ablation: DNDT +3.3, CDRC +3.2, ConCal +1.0 mAP averaged over three benchmarks; SHIFT 52.8, TDND 46.7. CoS (ICME 2024): source-only 35.1, oracle night-only 41.7, oracle day+night 47.1, 2PCNet 46.4, CoS 49.4 (so +2.3 over a day+night supervised model - but that oracle is a plain Faster R-CNN without the strong augmentation, EMA and enhancement CoS uses). Note the inconsistent baselines: the night-only oracle is 46.2 in 2PCNet and 41.7 in CoS on nominally the same task. ALDI (TMLR 2025, fair re-implementation with identical augmentation and EMA in all arms): Cityscapes->Foggy source-only 59.1, oracle 64.9, ALDI++ 64.5; Sim10k->Cityscapes source 70.8, oracle 88.0, ALDI++ 76.5; CFC source 65.8, oracle 76.7, ALDI++ 66.4; authors state that no existing method outperforms a fair oracle and many do not outperform a fair source-only model. DINO Teacher (CVPR 2025): Cityscapes->BDD100K 29.1 -> 47.8 vs oracle 51.6; Cityscapes->ACDC night 29.5 -> 36.4. YOLOF on BDD100K (Dutta 2022, 20k iterations): day->night 35.66 AP50, night->night (labelled night) 41.39, best DA method 39.32 (below the labelled-night model). DAI-Net (CVPR 2024, faces and ExDark): zero-shot WIDER FACE->DARK FACE DSFD 16.1 -> 28.0 mAP; with label fine-tuning on DARK FACE, DSFD 46.0 vs DAI-Net 52.9; COCO->DARK FACE with YOLOv3 and 5,500 labelled dark images for fine-tuning: plain YOLON 48.3, YOLOL (trained on synthetic low-light images) 54.0, MAET 55.8, DAI-Net 57.0 - i.e. synthetic-dark pretraining still adds +5.7 and DAI-Net +8.7 after supervised fine-tuning, in a small-label regime with an old detector. AAAI 2026 translation paper: +15.5% mAP (relative or absolute [uncertain]) on BDD100K day->night, +31.7% on traffic lights. Frame rate and camera motion are irrelevant for all of these: still images, no temporal component.

## Проверка против сильной базы
Weak. (1) Baselines are Faster R-CNN R50 at 600 px; no work tests a modern one-stage NMS-free detector at 960 px; ECCV 2024 uses YOLOv5/YOLOv7 but compares to other DA methods, not to a labelled-night YOLO with equal budget. (2) The relevant comparison for us, labelled day+night versus labelled day+night plus adaptation, is not run in any paper; the nearest is CoS's day+night oracle (47.1) which is not trained with the same augmentation/EMA as the method. (3) The fair-protocol study (ALDI) finds no DAOD method beats a fair oracle and many do not beat a fair source-only model, which implies that most reported day->night gains over 'oracle' come from extra augmentation, EMA and longer schedules rather than from adaptation. (4) Oracles are inconsistent across papers (46.2 vs 41.7) and copied rather than re-trained (Debiased Teacher). (5) No seeds, no confidence intervals; ablation steps of +0.6 AP (NightAug, student-scaling) are within the project's measured seed spread (0.0144 mAP on 12 epochs for DRB; night mAP50 SD 0.0031 for the control). (6) Independent reproduction: Debiased Teacher re-ran 2PCNet and got 47.6 vs 46.4 reported and CoS 48.4 vs 49.4 - reproductions within about +-1.2 AP, which is the honest noise floor of the field. The only result pointing to a gain beyond labels is DAI-Net/MAET synthetic-dark pretraining before label fine-tuning (+5.7 to +8.7 mAP on DARK FACE), but with 5,500 labelled images, YOLOv3/DSFD, and faces - far from our 112k labelled night frames.

## Разрез по условиям
BDD100K night per class in 2PCNet, Debiased Teacher and CoS (rider, motorcycle, bicycle benefit most from class-aware modules); 2PCNet gives APs/APm/APl in its ablation (APs 8.2-9.1); Debiased Teacher gives mAPs/mAPm/mAPl on SHIFT and states noise and flare modelling matter most on real night (BDD100K, TDND). No paper reports occlusion, first appearance of an object, overexposure separately, or any video/MOT metric. The project's own breakdown (baseline_summary.csv, 3 seeds): the night mAP50 gap is concentrated in rider, bus, motorcycle, bicycle (97% of the summed per-class gap), car and truck are at parity; val night has only 143 motorcycle, 737 rider and 867 bicycle boxes, so these class APs are noisy.

## Дешёвая проверка
No detector training in N0-N2; N3 only if they pass. N0 - what the night gap is made of (CPU, under 1 h, existing caches runs/det_cache_ctl_960, conf 0.01, 200 val videos). Split the night-vs-day threshold losses (21.0% vs 15.5%) and the score deficit (+0.098) by class and by size, with video bootstrap. Pre-registered decision: if car+pedestrian carry at least 70% of the extra night threshold losses (instance count), the lever is score calibration for common classes (and adaptive thresholds already gave only +0.096 MOTA at spread 0.095 - adaptive_threshold.csv), so DA-style synthesis is not prioritised; if rider/motorcycle/bicycle/bus carry at least 40% of the extra night losses in proportion to their share, go to N2 with rare-class oversampling. N1 - is the fly front-end more condition-invariant than raw pixels (GPU/CPU 2-3 h, the 7 local val videos with 30 Hz video plus train videos for fitting). On fovea crops of GT objects and of background (and separately on band candidates conf 0.01-0.25, TP vs FP), extract four feature sets with identical readout: raw gray fovea, retina-lamina output, full FlyVis type-wise readout (34 types x 19 macrocolumns), and YOLO26 neck feature at the box. Train a logistic probe on day, test on night, and the reverse; report transfer drop = AUC(night, trained on night) - AUC(night, trained on day). Pre-registered: retina-lamina is accepted as an invariant front-end/alignment target only if its transfer drop is at most 50% of the raw-gray drop and its within-night AUC is not lower than raw gray by more than 0.005; for band candidates the conf+fly head must beat conf-only by more than the 0.0081 AUC plank used in q_gate1. N2 - does a synthetic night look like real night to our detector (GPU about 1-2 h, frozen best.pt at 960). Apply to day val frames four transforms with matched mean luminance: NightAug-like (darken + gamma + noise + glare blobs), Debiased-Teacher-like DNDT (darken + shot/read noise + headlight flare), fly photoreceptor rendering (luminance scaled to night level, Poisson photon noise, photoreceptor adaptation, inverse-mapped to image), and simple gamma darkening as control. Run the frozen detector and compare with real night: mean score drop of matched TPs, threshold-loss share, blindness share. Pre-registered acceptance of a transform: it reproduces at least 50% of the real night score deficit (matched-TP score drop >= +0.05) while blindness rises by at most 2 pp above 4.5% (it should mimic the threshold mechanism, not erase objects), and it beats gamma darkening on this match; the fly rendering is preferred only if it beats the best non-biological transform by a margin larger than its bootstrap CI. N3 - short training (4-6 GPU h): fine-tune from weights/baseline_seed42/best.pt for 2 epochs at 640, 3 seeds per arm, arm A identical schedule and sampling, arm B with the accepted transform applied to day images with p = 0.3 and rare-class images oversampled x2 in both arms (so only the transform differs). Criterion: night mAP50 at 960 gain >= +0.010 (about 3x control SD 0.0031) with all 3 paired seeds positive, mean AP50 of rider+motorcycle+bicycle >= +0.02, day mAP50 not worse than -0.003; otherwise stop. Teacher-student on unlabelled night frames is gated in D12, not here.

## Стоимость у нас
Gates N0-N2: about 4-6 h total, mostly CPU, no training. N3: 2 epochs x 2 arms x 3 seeds from best.pt at 640, roughly 6 x 0.7 h = 4-5 GPU h on the RTX 5070 [uncertain about per-epoch time at 2 epochs]. Full confirmation: 12 epochs about 4.3 h per run, 3 seeds per arm = about 26 h; a 100-epoch confirmation (about 35 h per run) is needed before any claim because the DRB sign flipped between 12 and 100 epochs. Augmentation-only arms add no inference cost; image-space augmentation on CPU risks starving the dataloader (project experience), so the transform should run on GPU or be pre-rendered (pre-rendering 150k day frames once at 720p: a few hours, tens of GB [uncertain]). A full mean-teacher (2PCNet/Debiased Teacher style) on YOLO26s would roughly double forward passes per step (teacher on target, student on both), so about 1.6-2x time per epoch, i.e. 12 epochs about 7-9 h, and needs an Ultralytics training-loop rewrite; not recommended given the evidence.

## Ожидаемый эффект
Day-to-night adaptation proper (2PCNet/CoS/Debiased Teacher mechanism on top of labelled night): about 0 on night mAP50, within seed noise. Basis: 2PCNet exceeds a night-only oracle by only +0.2 AP50 with weaker data; ALDI's fair protocol shows no DA method above a fair oracle; our model is trained on 3.4-4x more labelled night frames than those papers' unlabelled pools. Physics/fly night synthesis for rare classes (N3 arm B): night mAP50 +0.003 to +0.010 (0.8% to 2.7% of 0.3772) [uncertain], concentrated in rider/motorcycle/bicycle where night train boxes are 3-3.5x fewer than day; car/pedestrian near 0. Basis: DAI-Net/MAET synthetic-dark gains survive label fine-tuning (+5.7 mAP) only in a small-label regime; 2PCNet NightAug adds +0.6 AP even without labels; our regime is label-rich. Tracker: DetA change at most +0.1 to +0.3 and FN change of a few hundred out of 15,763 [uncertain], because DetA and FN are instance-weighted and cars are 83% of night boxes (743,034 of 894,927 night train boxes), while the class-averaged mAP gap sits in rare classes; a rare-class gain moves mAP far more than DetA. Threshold-loss share at night: at most -0.5 to -1 pp from 21.0%, unless N0 shows rare classes carry a large share. Fly-specific increment over a non-biological physics transform: unknown; the honest prior is small.

## Риски
(1) Non-comparable baselines: published gains are over weak Faster R-CNN baselines and inconsistent oracles; transferring '+5 to +10 AP' to YOLO26s is unjustified. (2) The DRB lessons apply directly: +-0.6 AP ablation steps are inside our seed noise (0.0144 mAP spread at 12 epochs), and a sign seen at 2 or 12 epochs may flip at 100 epochs - require 3 paired seeds and a long confirmation. (3) More than one condition changing: augmentation arms must keep sampling, schedule and epochs identical; rare-class oversampling must be in both arms, otherwise the gain is from rebalancing, not from night rendering. (4) Synthetic artefacts: GAN-style translation hallucinates objects (traffic lights) and dark patches (Dutta 2022: ToDayGAN/CoMoGAN below the source-only baseline); physics transforms that erase small objects raise blindness and teach the detector to ignore faint objects - N2 checks blindness explicitly. (5) Confirmation bias for any teacher-student variant: plain mean teacher fell to 31.7 AP50, below source-only 41.1, in 2PCNet; on SHIFT UMT/AT were below source-only. (6) Adversarial domain alignment hurts (2PCNet: domain classifier 46.4 -> 44.5) - do not add a day/night discriminator. (7) Evaluation noise for rare night classes: 143 motorcycle and 737 rider boxes in val night, few distinct tracks; class APs can swing by 0.01-0.04 per seed (SD 0.010-0.016). (8) CPU dataloader slowdown from heavy augmentation; +time must be logged as with DRB (+17%). (9) Licenses: 2PCNet custom (NOASSERTION), several repos without a license file - re-implement transforms from the papers rather than copy code; DINO Teacher is Apache-2.0.

## Как подписать заимствование
Borrowed: the day-to-night UDA setting and teacher-student with two-phase consistency, NightAug and student-scaling (Kennerley et al., CVPR 2023); physics-based day-to-night transform with noise and flare, class-balanced cross-domain mixing, confidence calibration of pseudo-labels (Cui et al., ICCV 2025); adaptive IoU-informed thresholding (Yuan et al., ICME 2024); ISP-based degradation (Zhang et al., AAAI 2024); Dark-ISP synthesis and Retinex reflectance (Du et al., CVPR 2024); fair DAOD protocol and oracle reasoning (Kay et al., TMLR 2025); alignment to a frozen robust encoder (Lavoie et al., CVPR 2025); FlyVis (Lappalainen et al., Nature 2024). Ours: the observation that in a label-rich BDD100K MOT setting the night penalty is a threshold/score-scale shift plus a rare-class effect (97% of the class-averaged gap in four classes, car at parity) rather than a domain gap; the gate protocol (class split of threshold losses, day->night probe transfer of fly features, detector-matched night realism test); and, if it passes, the use of the fly photoreceptor/lamina model as night renderer or alignment target, always reported against a non-biological physics transform with the same inputs.
