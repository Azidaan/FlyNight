# 6.3.33. C8 Малые движущиеся цели (STMD, LC11) для рождения далёких объектов ночью

Small-target motion detectors respond to objects of about 1-3 deg moving relative to the background. Surround and lateral inhibition suppress wide-field motion and elongated features. The examples are STMD neurons in hoverflies and dragonflies, and LC11 in Drosophila with its columnar T2/T3 inputs. The transfer: compute an STMD/LC11-like saliency map over the whole frame from the fly's tiled activity (T2, T2a, T3 and T4/T5, with centre-surround inhibition, after the retina-lamina front-end). Use it as a feature for q_birth and deferred birth of small, distant, low-confidence YOLO26 candidates at night. A secondary, riskier use is class-agnostic motion proposals for objects the frozen detector misses entirely.
