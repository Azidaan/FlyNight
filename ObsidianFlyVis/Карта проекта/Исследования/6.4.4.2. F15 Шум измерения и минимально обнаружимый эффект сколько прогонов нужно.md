# 6.4.4.2. F15 Шум измерения и минимально обнаружимый эффект сколько прогонов нужно

> Bouthillier MLSys 2021, Picard 2021 (seed 3407), анализ мощности; разброс от сидов, данных, гиперпараметров; MDE до постановки; короткие прокси переворачивают знак.

## Механизм
A benchmark number is a random variable: it depends on the data sample (which videos are in the test split), weight initialisation, data order and augmentation, hyperparameter search, and numerical non-determinism. Bouthillier et al. (MLSys 2021) randomised each source 200 times on five case studies and found that data sampling (bootstrap of the data) is the largest source of variance; weight initialisation is generally below 50% of the bootstrap variance, on par with SGD data order. Their simulation of decision rules shows that comparing two single runs gives ≈10% false positives and ≈75% false negatives; comparing averages against a typical published improvement is over-conservative (≈90% false negatives even with k = 50); testing the probability of outperforming P(A>B) with a threshold γ = 0.75 and a bootstrap CI gives ≈5% false positives and ≈30% false negatives. They recommend randomising as many sources as possible (it also reduces the error of the mean, by decorrelating runs) and using resampled splits. Picard (2021, 'torch.manual_seed(3407) is all you need') scanned 10^4 seeds of a ResNet on CIFAR-10: final accuracy ranged 89.01–90.83% (1.82 points) with the distribution concentrated in 89.5–90.5; on ImageNet with pretrained backbones, SD ≈0.1% and max–min ≈0.5% over 50 seeds — i.e. a 0.5–2-point 'improvement' can be produced by seed choice alone. Dodge et al. (2020) showed the same for BERT fine-tuning: weight initialisation and data order each contribute variance comparable to published gains. Agarwal et al. (NeurIPS 2021) showed that with the 3–5 runs typical in deep RL, point estimates and even mean rankings are unreliable and proposed stratified-bootstrap CIs, IQM and performance profiles. The power-analysis consequence: for a two-arm comparison with between-run SD σ and n runs per arm, the minimum detectable effect at α = 0.05 (two-sided) and 80% power is MDE ≈ 2.8·σ·√(2/n); the required n to detect δ is n ≈ 2·(2.8·σ/δ)². The MDE must be computed BEFORE the experiment; if the expected effect is below it, the run cannot give a decision and is wasted. A second, separate problem is proxy validity: a short (12-epoch) run measures a different quantity than a full run; effects that interact with training length (regularisers, extra branches that slow early fitting) can change sign, so a short proxy answers 'does it break' but not 'how much', and its own variance can be much larger than that of the full run (early in training the loss surface is far from convergence, so seeds disagree more).

## Решения из литературы
(1) Measure variance first, then fix n by power analysis (Bouthillier 2021; Cohen): with σ known, n = 2·(2.8·σ/δ)² runs per arm for the smallest effect δ worth reporting. (2) Randomise all sources of variance in each run (seed of init, data order, augmentation, and the data split via out-of-bootstrap) — this both measures and reduces the error of the mean; Bouthillier's biased-but-cheap estimator approaches the ideal one with a 51× reduction in compute. (3) Decide with P(A>B) ≥ 0.75 plus a bootstrap CI rather than a single pair or a raw mean difference. (4) Report CIs from stratified bootstrap, IQM and performance profiles when runs are few (Agarwal 2021, rliable library). (5) Paired designs (common random numbers): run both arms with the same data-order seed and the same evaluation videos and test the paired difference; this removes the shared variance and lowers the MDE (the project's video bootstrap is already paired at the evaluation level). (6) Treat short runs as a filter for large effects only: proxies should be validated by checking rank correlation between short and full runs on a few known variants before trusting them [uncertain: no single canonical number; NAS/HPO literature reports imperfect, task-dependent short-vs-full rank correlations]. (7) Pre-register the threshold (plan rule 6.2) and keep the negative result when the CI includes zero (Picard: the max–min over seeds on CIFAR-10 is 1.82 points; anything smaller from one seed is not a result).

## Что сделать с мухой
For every fly experiment, write down before running: the effect δ that would matter (e.g. +0.005 pair AP, +0.5 pp night recall at equal FP, +0.3 HOTA), the variance components that apply (video bootstrap for frozen-fly heads; plus fly ensemble member (F13) and head seed for trained heads; plus training seed — passed manually into the Ultralytics dataloader generator, rule 6.12 — for any detector or fly fine-tuning), and the resulting n. Practical rules for the next fly steps: (a) Frozen fly + light heads (signature, residual probes): variance is dominated by videos; run heads with ≥5 head seeds and ≥3 fly members (F13), two-level bootstrap (members/seeds, then videos). (b) Fine-tuning the 734 fly parameters (K1/K2): ≥3 runs from different FlyVis members with different data-order seeds; the pilot criterion 'night error −30%' is large relative to likely noise and can be judged from 2–3 runs. (c) Fly inside or in front of the detector (K4/K5): do NOT use 12-epoch from-scratch probes for effects < 0.03 mAP (MDE at σ = 0.0144); use fine-tuning from best.pt (2 epochs) with seeds actually varied, or full 100-epoch runs where σ = 0.0007.

## Тест до постройки
Using numbers already in the project: (1) Compute the MDE table before proposing a run. Detector training, two-arm, α = 0.05, power 0.8: σ = 0.0144 (12 ep) → MDE 0.040 / 0.033 / 0.026 / 0.018 mAP for n = 2 / 3 / 5 / 10 per arm; if 0.0144 is a max–min range of 3 seeds rather than an SD, σ ≈ 0.0085 and MDE ≈ 0.019 at n = 3 [uncertain about the definition]. σ = 0.0031 (night mAP50 control, full runs) → MDE 0.0071 at n = 3; the DRB v2 first-seed gain +0.0066 was below it. σ = 0.0007 (100 ep) → MDE 0.0020 at n = 2, 0.0016 at n = 3. (2) Tracker and head gates with frozen models: paired video-bootstrap SE ≈ half-width/1.96 → test ΔHOTA SE ≈0.32, MDE ≈0.9 HOTA (≈1.5% of 58.47); val7 SE ≈0.17, MDE ≈0.47; recall at equal FP SE ≈0.09 pp, MDE ≈0.25 pp; pair AP SE ≈0.0028, MDE ≈0.008. Any proposed effect below these is not testable on the current splits without more videos. (3) Proxy validity check: for 2–3 already completed variants with both 12- and 100-epoch runs (base, DRB v1/v2), compute whether the 12-epoch ranking matches the 100-epoch ranking; the DRB sign flip already shows it does not for regulariser-like blocks. (4) Seed sanity check (rule 6.12): run two 'seeds' of a 1-epoch fine-tune and confirm the weights differ; if identical, the seed is not wired and variance is unmeasured.

## Потолок
Not a module, so no performance ceiling; the 'ceiling' is on what can be claimed. With 35 test videos, effects below ≈0.9 HOTA (≈1.5% relative) on test, ≈0.47 on val7, are not individually detectable; pooled dev+test+val7 or more videos lower this roughly as 1/√(videos). For detector training, the cheapest decisive design is full runs (σ = 0.0007): 3 seeds per arm give MDE 0.0016 mAP at a cost of 6 × 5.9 h ≈ 35 h, while 12-epoch runs would need n ≈ 2·(2.8·0.0144/δ)² — for δ = 0.005 that is ≈130 runs per arm (≈560 h at 4.3 h per run), i.e. short from-scratch probes cannot detect realistic effects at all.

## Где обязано помогать
Variance must be measured on the same subset the claim is about: night (the main deficit: threshold losses 21.0% vs 15.5% day), age-0 objects, glare, occluded; subset metrics have larger variance than aggregates because they contain fewer videos (27 dark dev+test videos vs 70 total), so the MDE on the night subset is ≈√(70/27) ≈ 1.6× the aggregate one [uncertain: assumes equal per-video variance]. No-harm check on the complement (day) must use the same n and its own CI.

## Отрицательный контроль
(1) A-vs-A null: run the baseline against itself with different seeds/members under the exact protocol; its 'effect' distribution is the empirical null and must be centred at zero with the claimed CI width. (2) Matched-compute control: the same number of extra parameters or training steps without the fly (rules out 'more training' effects). (3) For fly claims, the shuffled-connectome and matched-input controls (F13/F14) must be run with the same n and seeds as the real fly arm. (4) Permutation test on video labels between arms as a sanity check of the bootstrap.

## Вклад ветви
Record per run, not per experiment: seed (init, data order), FlyVis member, per-epoch validation metric (to see when arms cross — the 12 → 100 epoch sign flip), branch gate/weight norms. Plot the learning curves of both arms with seed bands; a difference that changes sign between epochs is a proxy-validity failure, not a result.

## Минимальный эффект и стоимость
Costs on the RTX 5070 12 GB: 12 epochs ≈ 4.3 h per run (task statement; plan 11.09 quoted 42 min for 12 epochs in a lighter setup), 100 epochs ≈ 5.9 h per seed (plan [§7.3↗](obsidian://open?vault=ObsidianFlyVis&file=05%20%D0%9F%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%BD%2F7.3.%20%D0%A7%D1%82%D0%BE%20%D0%BD%D0%B5%20%D0%B4%D0%B5%D0%BB%D0%B0%D1%82%D1%8C%20%D0%B4%D0%BE%20%D1%80%D0%B5%D0%B7%D1%83%D0%BB%D1%8C%D1%82%D0%B0%D1%82%D0%BE%D0%B2%20%D1%80%D0%B5%D0%BB%D1%8C%D1%81%D0%BE%D0%B2%20%D0%90%20%D0%B8%20%D0%91%2C%20%D1%81%D0%BE%D1%81%D1%82%D0%BE%D1%8F%D0%BD%D0%B8%D0%B5%20%D0%BD%D0%B0%2011.09)), 2-epoch fine-tune from best.pt ≈ 1–2 h [uncertain], frozen-model tracker gates = CPU minutes. MDE summary: detector from scratch at 12 ep — 0.033 mAP with 3 seeds/arm (26 h); at 100 ep — 0.0016 mAP with 3 seeds/arm (35 h); full-run night mAP50 with σ = 0.0031 — 0.007 with 3 seeds/arm, 0.005 needs 6 seeds/arm (≈71 h). Tracker/head gates on frozen caches — 0.9 HOTA on test, 0.25 pp recall at equal FP, 0.008 pair AP (free in GPU time). Recommendation: spend GPU on fewer, full or fine-tune runs with real seeds; spend CPU on bootstrap and pooled splits.

## Вердикт для проекта
Main methodological problem, already partly absorbed into plan rules 6.2–6.5 and 6.12, but still the reason most detector-side conclusions were unreliable: DRB 'gains' below 0.0144, a sign flip from 12 to 100 epochs, an adaptive threshold gain equal to its spread, and fine-tuning gates whose seed variance is literally unmeasured (rule 6.12). It does not explain why the fly does not help (frozen-fly results have proper video CIs and are clear nulls/negatives), but it determines which next experiments can give an answer at all. Priority: high and nearly free — compute the MDE table for every new proposal (K1, K3, K5) before running, wire the seed into the Ultralytics dataloader, and replace 12-epoch from-scratch probes by fine-tune or full runs.
