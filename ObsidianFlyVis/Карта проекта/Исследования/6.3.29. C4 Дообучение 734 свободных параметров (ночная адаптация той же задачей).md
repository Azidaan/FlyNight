# 6.3.29. C4 Дообучение 734 свободных параметров (ночная адаптация той же задачей)

## Суть
Train only the 734 free parameters of FlyVis (per-cell-type resting potentials and time constants for 65 types, plus per-type-pair synaptic strength scales for 604 edge types; topology, signs and synapse counts stay fixed) together with the decoder, so that the fly produces on artificially darkened / overexposed / noisy versions of bright frames the same output it (or DIS) produces on the clean bright frames. This is a connectome-constrained version of the day-to-night teacher-student / degradation-consistency adaptation that is standard in computer vision (2PCNet NightAug, DARTH, LTrack degradation suppression, DCPT/SAM-DA for night UAV tracking): the task stays the same, only the operating regime of the network is adapted. The same machinery can instead optimise a tracking loss (contrastive identity of the fly signature), which is plan item M2 / H7.

## Источники
Lappalainen et al., Connectome-constrained networks predict neural activity across the fly visual system, Nature 2024, https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07939-3, code https://github.com/TuragaLab/flyvis (NumberOfParams free=734, fixed=2959); Kennerley et al., 2PCNet: Two-Phase Consistency Training for Day-to-Night UDA Object Detection (NightAug), CVPR 2023, https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.13853, code https://github.com/mecarill/2pcnet; Segu et al., DARTH: Holistic Test-time Adaptation for MOT, ICCV 2023, https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.01926; Wang et al., Multi-Object Tracking in the Dark (LMOT, LTrack), CVPR 2024, https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.06600, code https://github.com/ying-fu/LMOT; Zhu et al., DCPT: Darkness Clue-Prompted Tracking in Nighttime UAVs, ICRA 2024, https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.10491; Yao et al., SAM-DA: UAV Tracks Anything at Night with SAM-Powered Domain Adaptation, ICARM 2024, https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.01024; TDA-Track: Prompt-Driven Temporal Domain Adaptation for Nighttime UAV Tracking, IROS 2024, https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.18533; Guo et al., TCEI: Dual-level Adaptation for MOT (test-time calibration), CVPR 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21629; Dhiman, Topological Sensitivity in Connectome-Constrained Neural Networks, arXiv 2604.04033 (2026); Range-Limited Scale Stability of Dynamic Direction Representations in a Connectome-Constrained Fly Visual Model, Neuroinformatics 2026, https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12021-026-09811-3.

## Относимость к проекту
Targets H7 (reformulated as M2: train the fly, not only the readout) and H9 (night input out of the trained distribution). The retina-lamina frontend already removed the night drop of the fly in the reliability head (stage B, dark videos, DIS+fly head, lamina minus no-retina +0.0094 [+0.0049; +0.0142]), but the fly still adds only +0.0028 [-0.0007; +0.0064] over DIS on dark videos and 0 on the test aggregate. In the re-identification role the fly is a worse descriptor than its own 721-facet input (test -0.052 AP) and adds only +0.006 AP on top of it (dark +0.0102). Training the free parameters is the only lever that changes what the fly computes rather than what it is fed; the relevant project problems are the dark-scene deficit, the matched-control gap (fly vs its input) and, indirectly, IDSw through better association signatures.

## Пересечение с чужими работами
[uncertain] Training the same 734 parameters is exactly the Lappalainen et al. 2024 procedure (task-optimisation on Sintel optic flow under a fixed connectome), so the training method itself is not ours. Day-to-night adaptation by teacher-student consistency on synthetically degraded images is established (2PCNet NightAug with random glare, blur and noise; DARTH photometric-consistency test-time adaptation for MOT on BDD100K; LTrack degradation-suppression learning on paired low-light video; DCPT and SAM-DA for frozen/adapted night UAV trackers). No published work was found that fine-tunes FlyVis free parameters for illumination invariance or for a tracking loss; the only 2026 reuse found (Kuklev, arXiv 2609.24565) keeps the fly frozen. cannot_claim: the teacher-student / synthetic-darkening scheme, connectome-constrained training as a method, biological fidelity of the adapted parameters, and any topology-driven benefit unless a degree- and sign-preserving shuffled connectome trained with the identical recipe and initialisation is worse (Dhiman 2026 reports that topology advantages largely disappear under shared initialisation and degree-preserving nulls).

## Результаты у авторов
[uncertain] 2PCNet (CVPR 2023): day-to-night UDA object detection, +8.9 AP on BDD100K and +10.2 AP on SHIFT over the previous state of the art (single images, dashcam, detection only, no tracking). DARTH (ICCV 2023): SHIFT to BDD100K MOT test-time adaptation, HOTA 17.3 to 20.6 (+3.3), IDF1 18.5 to 23.7, AssA 28.9 to 33.1, DetA 12.0 to 15.2, MOTA -66.4 to 8.3; BDD100K is 30 fps video with 5 Hz annotation from a moving car camera; teacher is an EMA (tau 0.998) of the student, detector and embedding head are adapted. LTrack (CVPR 2024): LMOT-real low-light MOT (32 sequences, 35K frames, 815K boxes, dual-camera aligned bright/dark pairs), HOTA 35.1, AssA 38.9, IDF1 40.4, MOTA 45.2, DetA 30.7. SAM-DA (ICARM 2024): +7.8% success, +5.8% normalised precision, +5.0% precision over baseline on DarkTrack2021 (UAV single-object tracking, moving camera). DCPT (ICRA 2024): prompts added to a frozen daytime tracker, reported state of the art on several dark UAV benchmarks (exact numbers not verified). Lappalainen 2024: 734 free parameters trained on Sintel flow, ensemble of 50 models; task-optimised connectome-constrained models predict T4/T5 direction selectivity. Neuroinformatics 2026: frozen FlyVis direction decoding transfers to interior aperture scales (0.986) but collapses at the upper boundary (0.167, chance), a regime limit that parameter adaptation could address.

## Уровень измерения
Literature: detection AP (2PCNet), HOTA/IDF1/AssA (DARTH, LTrack), single-object success/precision (SAM-DA, DCPT), neural tuning and flow error for FlyVis. Nothing at the level of a connectome model inside an MOT tracker. In the project the first test would be pair level (AP of the reliability head and of the re-identification head); HOTA/IDF1 after TrackEval only if the pair-level CI is above zero.

## Согласованный контроль
The CV adaptation papers have no same-information control and no graph null model. Dhiman 2026 uses degree-preserving rewired nulls and shared random initialisation and finds topology advantages mostly vanish. The project already has both control types for the frozen fly: [fly_matched_controls_v1↗](obsidian://open?vault=ObsidianFlyVis&file=01%20%D0%9C%D1%83%D1%85%D0%B0%2F%D0%AD%D0%BA%D1%81%D0%BF%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%B5%D0%BD%D1%82%D1%8B%2Ffly_matched_controls_v1) (fly vs its own 721-facet input; fly worse as a standalone descriptor, -0.052 AP test, +0.006 on top of the input) and [fly_connectome_null_v1↗](obsidian://open?vault=ObsidianFlyVis&file=01%20%D0%9C%D1%83%D1%85%D0%B0%2F%D0%AD%D0%BA%D1%81%D0%BF%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%B5%D0%BD%D1%82%D1%8B%2Ffly_connectome_null_v1) (degree- and sign-preserving permutation of receivers; +0.044 AP for the fly's own representation, +0.0032 [-0.0028; +0.0100] on top of the input on test). A fine-tuning study must repeat both with training: (1) shuffled connectome trained with the same recipe, steps and seed; (2) same-capacity head on the 721-facet input trained on the same degraded/clean pairs; (3) frozen fly with the retina-lamina frontend; (4) fly trained on clean frames only for the same number of steps (separates 'more training' from 'night adaptation').

## Разрез по условиям
2PCNet and DARTH are day-to-night by construction; LMOT is low-light only; SAM-DA/DCPT/TDA-Track are night-only benchmarks. None split by occlusion or density. The project has a dark (< 60/255, 27 dev+test videos) / bright (43) split with per-video bootstrap; overexposure and occlusion splits do not exist yet.

## Стоимость у нас
[uncertain] No FlyVis training code exists in the project (tracking/foveated_eyes.py runs inference only). The caches are not sufficient: runs/fly_signatures/*/signatures.npz stores per-life averages (first/last 5 steps) and runs/train_cache*/ stores decoded fovea flow, not the full per-eye hex-input sequences needed for BPTT, so foveal hex inputs must be re-rendered once for the train split (fast retina-lamina 67 ms/frame; signatures 75 ms/frame) and stored. Degradations (gamma, Poisson-Gaussian noise, clipping, blur, JPEG) are applied on the fly to bright train videos, so no new data download is needed. Training: BPTT windows of about 8 frames x 2 integration steps on foveal crops of the 130-video train split; the plan estimates 3-6 GPU hours for M2, and each trained control (shuffled connectome, input-only, clean-only) adds a comparable amount, i.e. roughly 10-20 GPU hours in total. Evaluation re-uses the existing heads and scripts (scripts/eval_fly_reid.py, the motion reliability ladder) on CPU.

## Ожидаемый эффект
[uncertain] Small. The dark-scene deficit that input normalisation can fix has already been fixed by the lamina (+0.0094 AP in the DIS+fly head on dark videos, +0.0003 on bright), and the frozen fly adds +0.0028 over DIS on dark videos and +0.0102 over its own input on dark videos in re-ID. A realistic target for night adaptation is +0.005 to +0.01 AP on dark pairs; the end-to-end reliability experiment gave dHOTA -0.01 [-0.04; +0.02], so an effect on HOTA is unlikely. The larger headroom is in the re-ID role (fly minus input -0.052 AP on test): a contrastive identity loss on the 734 parameters could close part of that gap, but the gain must then beat the trained input-only control.

## Риски
[uncertain] (1) Sim-to-real gap: synthetic darkening lacks headlight glare, colour noise, rolling-shutter and compression artefacts of real BDD100K night. (2) Teacher failure: DIS itself degrades at night (b1d22449: geometry 0.448 to 0.356 with DIS), so the teacher must only see bright frames. (3) Attribution: extra trainable capacity can explain any gain; without the trained shuffled connectome and trained input-only controls nothing can be attributed to the fly. (4) Rule compliance: the detector YOLO26 stays frozen; train only on the train split, choose hyperparameters on dev, report test and val7 once; do not combine frontend change and training in one comparison (one condition at a time); paired per-video bootstrap CIs; one seed of the shuffled connectome is insufficient. (5) Engineering: multi-process CUDA hangs seen before (use tracking/gpu_lock.py or a single process); long runs only via Start-Process with Idle priority (plan section 9), GPU runs over 1 h need user approval. (6) Training may push time constants towards the integration step and destabilise the dynamics.

## Как подписать заимствование
Borrowed: FlyVis model, connectome and the free-parameter training procedure (Lappalainen et al. 2024, TuragaLab/flyvis); teacher-student consistency on synthetically degraded images (2PCNet NightAug; DARTH; LTrack degradation suppression); degree-preserving null model (standard rewiring; Dhiman 2026 for connectome models). Ours: adapting the fly's free parameters for night operation of a per-track foveal eye inside an MOT tracker on BDD100K, the camera-model degradation for dashcam video, the paired control set (trained shuffled connectome, trained input-only head, clean-only training) and the pair-level and HOTA evaluation.
