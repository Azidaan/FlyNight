# 6.4.5.7. F8 Улучшение для глаза не равно улучшению для детектора задаче-ориентированная предобработка

> LIME-Eval 2024, Trash to Treasure 2023, Dark-ISP 2025: усиление слабого света под человека вредит детекции; обучение предобработки от потери детекции.

## Механизм
A preprocessing stage placed before a trained detector changes the input distribution the detector was fitted to, and its own objective (visual quality for a human, or a biological 'plausibility' of the transform) is not the detector's objective. Three effects combine. (1) Covariate shift for a frozen detector: the detector's filters, BN statistics and confidence calibration were fitted on raw camera frames (here H.264-compressed BDD100K frames). Any transform that changes local contrast, colour ratios, noise texture or temporal blur moves features off the training manifold; confidence drops and some objects stop being detected at all. The damage grows with the strength of the transform. (2) Information cannot be created by a deterministic transform of the same pixels (data processing inequality): enhancement can only redistribute or amplify what is already there, and it also amplifies noise and compression artefacts. A trained detector already performs its own internal normalisation, so the enhancer is mostly redundant at best. (3) Objective mismatch: low-light enhancers are trained/selected for PSNR/SSIM/NIQE or human preference; these correlate poorly with detection. LIME-Eval (Li, Zhao, Guo, arXiv 2410.08810, 2024) shows that common IQA metrics correlate weakly with human preference (Spearman NIMA 0.457, BRISQUE 0.321, NIQE -0.250) and that with retraining the detector on enhanced images, enhancement-first is worse than training on the dim input (ExDark, YOLOX-m: dim input 49.2 mAP vs Bread 45.9, IAT 47.6, RetinexFormer 46.1). Trash to Treasure (Cui et al., AAAI 2024, arXiv 2309.03548) states the disparity of task objectives between enhancer and detector as the reason enhancer+detector 'cannot shine': on ExDark with a fine-tuned SSD, low-light input gives 56.30 mAP and 5 of 7 enhancers are below it (EnGAN 55.57, KinD 54.33, DRBN 53.44, RUAS 54.32, SCI 55.77; only DeepUPE 56.66 and ZeroDCE 56.85 are marginally above), and their point is that the illumination component the enhancer throws away is useful to the detector. IA-YOLO (Liu et al., AAAI 2022) gives the cleanest frozen-detector example: Zero-DCE preprocessing in front of a YOLOv3 trained on normal VOC lowers mAP from 45.92 to 33.57 on VOC_Dark and from 36.42 to 34.41 on ExDark. The literature fix is always the same: make the preprocessing differentiable and train it with the detection loss (or remove it and learn illumination-invariant features inside the detector).

## Где видно у нас
(a) Г3 / [fly_eye_frontend_v1↗](obsidian://open?vault=ObsidianFlyVis&file=01%20%D0%9C%D1%83%D1%85%D0%B0%2F%D0%AD%D0%BA%D1%81%D0%BF%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%B5%D0%BD%D1%82%D1%8B%2Ffly_eye_frontend_v1) (completed_negative): frozen YOLO26 on processed frames, recall at equal FP minus raw, dev+test night (raw 0.7552): temporal average of 3 frames -1.97 pp [-2.79; -1.22], 5 frames -5.69 pp, fly photoreceptor -9.45 pp [-11.54; -7.68], photoreceptor with integration -15.13 pp [-17.20; -13.15], photoreceptor + tavg3 -8.31 pp; by day tavg3 -2.06 pp, photoreceptor -2.88 pp. Blindness at night (missed even at conf 0.01) rises from 0.1047 raw to 0.1222 (tavg3), 0.1585 (pr), 0.2167 (pr_int) - the same signature as IA-YOLO's Zero-DCE + frozen YOLOv3 case: harm grows with the size of the input change, and it is a distribution shift, not only noise. (b) Homography-based accumulation blurs exactly the moving objects (they do not move like the background): more frames is worse (tavg5 < tavg3). (c) No 'photon starvation' in the sense accumulation cures: H.264 already smooths noise temporally, so the enhancer adds no information (data-processing argument). (d) The retina-lamina front-end helps the fly's own pair proxy (+0.016 AP at night removes the fly's night dip) while DIS does not benefit (+0.001): the same transform is 'good' for a downstream module trained on such inputs (the fly was built around photoreceptor-like inputs) and 'bad' for one trained on raw frames (YOLO26) - preprocessing value is defined by the consumer, not by the transform. (e) DRB spatial/frequency branches inside the neck (a learned in-network 'enhancer') collapsed to identity (one branch weight = 0 in all 896 channels, frequency mask neutral 4 of 4): when the enhancement is trained with the detection loss but brings no new information, the loss switches it off.

## Решения из литературы
1) Differentiable preprocessing trained with the detection loss, with a tiny parameter budget: IA-YOLO (AAAI 2022) - CNN-predicted parameters of differentiable filters (WB, gamma, contrast, tone, sharpen, defog) trained end-to-end with YOLOv3; low-light: VOC_Dark 45.92 -> 59.40 mAP (+13.48 over YOLOv3 trained on normal data; +7.12 over YOLOv3 trained on hybrid data, 52.28), ExDark 36.42 -> 40.37 (+3.95; +3.34 over hybrid 37.03), while Zero-DCE preprocessing gives 33.57 / 34.41. Dark-ISP (ICCV 2025): RAW-domain ISP split into linear (sensor calibration) and nonlinear (tone mapping) differentiable sub-modules with physics priors, trained by the task loss, ~0.14 MB nonlinear + ~0.35 MB linear; RetinaNet-R50 on LOD 70.4 mAP vs 67.3 default ISP, 66.2 RAW-Adapter, 67.0 FeatEnHancer; NOD-Sony 31.5 vs 28.3 default ISP; SynCOCO 23.1 vs 21.5. 2) Keep the information the enhancer discards and fuse inside the detector: Trash to Treasure (AAAI 2024) - Retinex decomposition into illumination + reflectance, shared backbone, multiplicative FPN aggregation; ExDark SSD 59.74 mAP vs 56.30 low-light input (+3.44) and vs best enhancer ZeroDCE 56.85; initialising the decomposition with RUAS / SCI and training through the detector gives 67.36 / 68.33 vs 65.98 / 65.68 for 'enhance then fine-tune detector' (DSFD), while naive joint training of enhancer+detector is slightly worse (65.70 / 65.42). 3) Illumination-invariant features inside the network instead of image enhancement: YOLA (NeurIPS 2024) - zero-mean learnable kernels on neighbouring channels/pixels (Lambertian model), +1.5 mAP with YOLOv3 and up to +2.5 mAP on low-light sets, ExDark 72.7 mAP with YOLOv3 [baseline numbers per detector uncertain]. FeatEnHancer (ICCV 2023): task-loss-guided multi-scale feature enhancement, +5.7 mAP on ExDark, AP50 86.3 with Featurized Query R-CNN. 4) Restoration supervised by a detector-friendly target: Sun et al. NeurIPS 2022 generate pseudo-ground-truth by a targeted adversarial attack on the detector so the restorer learns images that are both close to sharp and better for detection [exact numbers not verified]. 5) Evaluate preprocessing by the frozen detector rather than by IQA (LIME-Eval: detection-based and energy-based scoring; Spearman with human preference 0.593 vs <= 0.457 for classic IQA). 6) Domain adaptation instead of input transformation on BDD100K itself: 2PCNet day->night Faster R-CNN AP50 46.4 vs source-only 41.1 and target-supervised 46.2; note several adaptation methods are below source-only (TDD 34.6, UMT 36.2, AT 38.5) and image-translation (ForkGAN, FDA) gives only +0.5..+0.8 AP over source-only - i.e. input-space translation is a weak lever on BDD100K night.

## Что сделать с мухой
Stop feeding fly-processed frames to a frozen detector; that design is closed by Г3 and by IA-YOLO's Zero-DCE result. Three task-oriented placements instead. (A) Fly as a learnable, residual, detector-trained input adapter (IA-YOLO / Dark-ISP logic): photoreceptor -> lamina (L1-L3) stage of FlyVis with its biophysical parameters (time constants, gains, adaptation) made trainable, output mixed into the RGB input as raw + g * fly_map with g initialised to 0 (zero-init residual), so the detector starts exactly at baseline; train adapter + detector for 2 epochs at 960 with the YOLO loss; arms: adapter trainable / adapter frozen at Sintel values / shuffled connectome / same-size non-fly differentiable ISP (IA-YOLO filters). (B) Fly as an extra channel, never a replacement (Trash-to-Treasure logic: keep what the enhancer would discard): a 4th input channel or a side branch into P3 of the FPN carrying lamina/medulla responses (temporal contrast, ON/OFF), with the first conv's new filter zero-initialised; frozen YOLO26 elsewhere in the first pilot, then full fine-tune. (C) Illumination-invariant inductive bias inside YOLO (YOLA logic): use the fly's photoreceptor adaptation (divisive normalisation over space/time) as a fixed or lightly-trainable module in the first layers, trained by the detection loss; this is the closest biological counterpart to YOLA's illumination-invariant module. In all three, the photoreceptor 'integration' variant (pr_int, -15.13 pp) must not be used for moving objects without object-motion compensation; and temporal accumulation should be replaced by the fly's own motion-selective pathway (T4/T5) as a channel, not by frame averaging.

## Тест до постройки
1) Frozen-detector shift test already done (Г3) - it tells us that any non-trainable fly input hurts; reuse it as the 'no-adaptation' anchor. 2) Information test before training (no detector training): on the cached night val frames, fit a light probe (logistic regression / 1-layer conv) on the YOLO26 neck features at the GT boxes that are lost at the threshold (21.0% of night GT) vs background, with and without concatenated fly-lamina features at the same locations; the gain in probe AUC with fly features over neck features (paired bootstrap over videos) estimates the information the fly channel can add. If DeltaAUC CI includes 0, a fly input channel will not raise recall at equal FP. 3) Cheap trainable-adapter pilot on a subset: train only the adapter (fly g and its biophysical parameters, < 1k params) with the detector frozen on 200 night videos for 1 epoch; if recall at equal FP does not reach >= raw - 0.25 pp (i.e. the adapter cannot even learn to be harmless), the placement is wrong. 4) Compare with the non-fly differentiable ISP of the same size trained identically: if the IA-YOLO filter set reaches the same recall, the fly topology adds nothing beyond 'a learnable tone curve'.

## Потолок
Upper bound for any preprocessing of the same pixels: the detector already sees the raw frame, so the ceiling is the share of night threshold losses (21.0% night vs 15.5% day = 5.5 pp gap) that is due to under-scoring recoverable by better features, not blindness (4.5% night and day). In literature terms, task-trained preprocessing on top of a fine-tuned detector buys +1..+4 mAP on ExDark/LOD/NOD (Dark-ISP +3.1 on LOD vs default ISP; T2 +3.44 on ExDark vs input; IA-YOLO +3.3 on ExDark vs hybrid-trained YOLOv3) - mostly against weak, old detectors (SSD, YOLOv3, RetinaNet). On BDD100K night with a strong detector trained on night data, translation-style input changes give +0.5..+0.8 AP (2PCNet table). Expected ceiling for a fly adapter here: night recall at equal FP +0.5..+2 pp, i.e. 10-35% of the 5.5 pp day-night threshold-loss gap, DetA +0.1..+0.6 [uncertain], HOTA +0.05..+0.3 [uncertain].

## Где обязано помогать
Must help: night frames (recall at equal FP, raw 0.7552 dev+test), night age-0 objects, over-exposure (headlight glare: crops containing saturated pixels > 5% of the box), and night rare classes (rider, bus, motorcycle, bicycle carry 97% of the night mAP gap). No-harm: day recall at equal FP within -0.2 pp of raw (Г3 showed even mild transforms cost -2 pp by day, so this is the binding constraint), night blindness at conf 0.01 not above raw 0.1047, overall mAP50 within seed spread, car AP night/day 0.817 / 0.816 unchanged.

## Отрицательный контроль
Negative: (1) same adapter architecture with shuffled connectome / randomised lamina parameters; (2) adapter trained with g forced to 0 (pure fine-tune of the detector for the same epochs) - controls for 'extra fine-tuning helps'; (3) non-biological differentiable ISP of matched parameter count (IA-YOLO filter set or YOLA zero-mean kernels) trained with the same loss and schedule. Matched: same start weights, same data order and augmentation, same 960 inference, same equal-FP operating point computed per arm, same videos (no train_cache videos in detection evaluation). Gain counts for the fly only if fly-adapter > g=0 fine-tune and > matched non-bio ISP on night recall at equal FP, with paired bootstrap CI over videos excluding 0, and no-harm on day.

## Вклад ветви
Track per epoch: residual gate g (and its gradient), mean |g * fly_map| / mean |raw| at the input, gradient-norm ratio adapter / first YOLO conv, and an epoch-wise ablation (g := 0) on a fixed night subset measuring recall at equal FP; a g that stays < 0.01 or an ablation gap < 0.1 pp after 2 epochs means the detector ignores the fly channel (the DRB failure mode).

## Минимальный эффект и стоимость
Primary metric: recall at equal FP, paired on the same frames. Г3 CIs (half-width ~0.8 pp for tavg3 at night) and conf_target_probe CIs (half-width ~0.17 pp) give MDE ~ 0.25-1.1 pp depending on subset size; with the 70 held-out + val7 videos, an effect of +0.5 pp at night is detectable, +0.2 pp is not. mAP at 12 epochs is useless as primary (seed spread 0.0144). Cost on RTX 5070 12 GB: adapter-only pilot (detector frozen, 1 epoch on 200 night videos) ~ 0.5-1 h; full 2-epoch fine-tune at 960 per arm ~ 2-3 h; 4 arms (fly trainable, fly frozen, shuffled, matched non-bio ISP) + g=0 control = 5 runs ~ 10-15 h; plus the evaluation on cached val ~ 1 h GPU per arm. Requires the user's permission for training (plan [§9↗](obsidian://open?vault=ObsidianFlyVis&file=05%20%D0%9F%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%BD%2F9.%20%D0%98%D0%BD%D1%84%D1%80%D0%B0%D1%81%D1%82%D1%80%D1%83%D0%BA%D1%82%D1%83%D1%80%D0%B0), Idle priority).

## Вердикт для проекта
Primary explanation of Г3 (frozen YOLO26 on fly/accumulated frames: -2..-15 pp, rising blindness) and of the 'retina helps the fly but not the detector' asymmetry; secondary for the overall HOTA ceiling (which is set by detection recall, F9). Priority: medium-high - it does not open a large ceiling, but it is the only correct way to put the fly in front of (or into) the detector, and it converts a closed negative into a controllable experiment (trainable fly adapter vs matched non-bio ISP). Novelty doubt: task-trained differentiable preprocessing is established (IA-YOLO 2022, Dark-ISP 2025, YOLA 2024); the possible contribution is only the connectome-constrained adapter with topology controls - record in cannot_claim that 'enhancement trained by detection loss' is not new.
