# 6.3.20. C10 Статистики сигнатуры СКО по времени, пространственный разброс по типам

The current fly signature keeps only first-order statistics. For each of the 34 output types it stores the mean activity pooled over 19 macro-columns and averaged over the first/last K=5 frames of a track life, and over the integration sub-steps inside each frame (646 dims; EyeBank._by_type averages over steps, build_fly_signatures.mean_parts averages over K). The proposal adds second-order statistics per type: (a) the temporal SD of the response within the K-frame window and across sub-steps, which captures how strongly motion-sensitive T4/T5 and Tm/TmY types fluctuate rather than their level; (b) the spatial spread across columns, i.e. the SD or variation over the 721 columns or over the 19 macro-cells, which is translation-invariant within the eye and therefore tolerant to box misalignment after occlusion. This is exactly the descriptor form used by Kuklev 2026 for a frozen FlyVis (mean, temporal SD and spatial variation per type) and the classical recipe of texture statistics (Portilla-Simoncelli; Gram/second-order pooling).
