# 6.3.30. C5 Модели сетчатки и ламины адаптация, шум и временная интеграция при слабом свете

Models of the first stages of fly vision that FlyVis does not contain: photoreceptor R1-R6 light adaptation (Weber / Naka-Rushton gain with an adapting half-saturation point, bandwidth that narrows in dim light), lamina LMC (L1-L3) centre-surround contrast normalisation, a separate luminance channel (L3), fast luminance gain control in the medulla (Tm1 divisive, Tm9 quadratic normalisation with Dm12 spatial pooling over about 8-20 degrees) and recurrent L1/L2 feedback that temporally sharpens intensity changes. In dim light such stages trade temporal and spatial resolution for signal-to-noise (neural summation). The project already has a simplified photoreceptor + lamina frontend in front of the fly (tracking/retina.py, stage B completed_positive); the question here is which missing pieces (light-dependent temporal integration, noise-aware gain, luminance channel, spatial pooling, highlight/glare handling) are worth adding and testing.
