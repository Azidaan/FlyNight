# 6.3.3.2.5. C8 Малые движущиеся цели (STMD, LC11) для рождения далёких объектов ночью

## Суть
Small-target motion detectors respond to objects of about 1-3 deg moving relative to the background. Surround and lateral inhibition suppress wide-field motion and elongated features. The examples are STMD neurons in hoverflies and dragonflies, and LC11 in Drosophila with its columnar T2/T3 inputs. The transfer: compute an STMD/LC11-like saliency map over the whole frame from the fly's tiled activity (T2, T2a, T3 and T4/T5, with centre-surround inhibition, after the retina-lamina front-end). Use it as a feature for q_birth and deferred birth of small, distant, low-confidence YOLO26 candidates at night. A secondary, riskier use is class-agnostic motion proposals for objects the frozen detector misses entirely.

## Относимость к проекту
Births are the largest measured gap in the project. The first-appearance (age-0) gap is 30.41 pp, of which 66% (20.20 pp) runs through underestimated confidence, and 17.92% of night tracks at 960 are never found at all (plan [§3↗](obsidian://open?vault=ObsidianFlyVis&file=05%20%D0%9F%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%BD%2F3.%20%D0%92%D1%81%D0%B5%20%D0%B8%D0%B7%D0%BC%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%B5%D0%BD%D0%BD%D1%8B%D0%B5%20%D1%87%D0%B8%D1%81%D0%BB%D0%B0)/[§5↗](obsidian://open?vault=ObsidianFlyVis&file=05%20%D0%9F%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%BD%2F5.%20%D0%A7%D1%82%D0%BE%20%D0%B6%D0%B8%D0%B2%D0%BE) item 4a). Line B already has q_birth heads and a deferred-birth policy (birth_deferred_train_v1): deferred birth alone reduces IDSw by 4.3 per video [-5.7; -3.0] and is better in 29 of 35 test videos; buffer plus deferred birth gives +0.72 HOTA [+0.21; +1.46] vs the stand. A better q_birth on small night candidates plugs straight into that policy. Registry: H9 (night out-of-distribution input, lamina front-end), H11 (functions where the fly is native: an STMD feature is listed in M4), H10 (scale). H3 closed only a generic tiled readout of the birth box for q_birth (AP 0.7003 vs 0.7014 control), not a small-target-specific, surround-inhibited readout. In tiled mode the column step of about 13.3 px is roughly the size of distant BDD objects, which is exactly the one-to-few-column regime where STMD/LC11 operate.

## Пересечение с чужими работами
STMD modelling is very active, especially in the Yue/Wang group: feedback STMD (2018-2021), STF (TIP 2024), vSTMD (2025), Frac-STMD (2023). There are LC11-based models (Tanaka & Clark 2020; Zhang et al. 2026), and deep moving-infrared-small-target detection is a large separate field. These works use STMDs as detectors or proposal generators. None was found that uses them as a birth-confidence feature in tracking-by-detection MOT or builds them on connectome-constrained FlyVis, but that absence is not evidence of novelty. cannot_claim: first bio-inspired small-target detector; LC11 derived from the connectome (FlyVis has T2/T3 but no LC11, so the pooling is ours); novelty of night small-object detection; 'the fly helps births' (H3 was negative) unless the new readout passes pre-registered controls.

## Результаты у авторов
vSTMD (arXiv 2025), on RIST: 19 real videos, 29,900 frames, 480x270 at 240 Hz, cluttered backgrounds, sunny and cloudy. Localization mF1: vSTMD 32.44%, vSTMD-F 39.33%, vs Frac-STMD 24.88%, FSTMD 24.51%, STMD+ 22.31%, FeedbackSTMD 18.70%, DSTMD 16.35%, ESTMD 14.93%, the LC11 model 0.63% and the LC18 model 0.18%. Orientation error AAE 0.50 rad for vSTMD-F vs 0.74 for DpFlow (CVPR 2025) and 1.25 for SEA-RAFT. Speed: 161-168 FPS on CPU, 567-608 FPS on GPU. On XS-VID, average recall for extremely tiny targets (<=8x8 px) is 52.86% for vSTMD-F vs 6.55% for yoloft-L and 30.63% for FracSTMD, at 132.9 FPS on GPU. STF (TIP 2024): evaluated on RIST GoPro 240 fps videos with detection rate / false-alarm metrics (a hit is within 5 px); claims to beat prior STMDs [uncertain: numbers not retrieved]. Zhang et al. 2026 (Biomimetics): synthetic 30 FPS 500x250 sequences with 5x5 px targets at 250 px/s over 12 real campus panoramas. Reports +28.24% accuracy over ESTMD/DVNPM and +77.82% over MOG/ViBe background subtraction, but runs 43.4% slower and degrades at 500 px/s. All results are daytime; none is night driving. Cameras are static, hand-held or panning synthetic. Frame rates are mostly 240 Hz; only Zhang uses 30 Hz, and that is synthetic.

## Уровень измерения
Pixel-level detection (DR/FA, F1 with a 5 px tolerance, mAUC/mAP) and proposal recall (AR). No tracker-level (HOTA/IDF1) or birth-decision (AP of true births) evaluation in any external work.

## Согласованный контроль
External: no same-information or graph-null controls; they compare against other STMDs, background subtraction and deep flow/detection models, and ablate components (vSTMD: Gaussian filter, cIDP, CDGC, feedback). Controls required here: (1) the existing q_birth head on geometry + score + DIS (event_heads_v1: AP 0.5480 -> 0.6931, +DIS 0.7014); (2) a classical non-fly STMD (ESTMD or vSTMD, code available) on the same frames and candidates, as the same-information control; (3) input-only: the same centre-surround temporal-contrast pooling on the 721-facet photoreceptor input after the lamina front-end; (4) FlyVis with the degree-preserving shuffled connectome (fly_connectome_null_v1 infrastructure); (5) H3's generic tiled readout, to show that the STMD-specific pooling adds information. Earlier project precedent: fly over its own input +0.006 AP (+0.010 at night); topology vs shuffled +0.044 AP for signatures.

## Разрез по условиям
External: breakdowns by target velocity and size (vSTMD) and luminance level (Zhang 2026, synthetic); sunny vs cloudy on RIST; nothing for real night, headlights, glare or rain. Needed here: night vs day (dark videos <60/255), candidate box size bins (<16, 16-32, >32 px), score tier (low 0.1-0.25 vs >=0.25), class (car vs pedestrian), and distance to the focus of expansion.

## Стоимость у нас
Candidates and labels already exist: runs/event_dataset_train (165 train videos; the q_birth head was trained on 156,193 candidates from 130 train videos) plus dev/test. Full-frame tiled FlyVis activity (34 types incl. T2, T2a, T3, T4a-d, T5a-d; 6 tiles x 721 columns) exists only for 8 videos (runs/flyvis_activity, about 20 ms/frame on RTX 5070). The night subset of train+dev+test is about 80 videos x about 1,200 frames, roughly 96k frames: about 32 min GPU plus init, under 1 h wall, launched with Idle priority. Store only per-candidate readouts or 4-6 types to keep a few GB instead of about 25 GB. The ESTMD/vSTMD control runs on CPU (vSTMD is about 160 FPS at 480x270, so an estimated 20-30 FPS at 1280x720), about 1 h CPU with parallelism. Heads and per-video bootstrap take CPU minutes. Tracker level: deferred birth with the new q_birth on cached detections, then TrackEval on dev/test, about 1 h CPU. A fovea per candidate would be much more expensive and is not needed.

## Ожидаемый эффект
Low to modest. H3 found no gain from a generic tiled readout for q_birth (0.7003 vs 0.7014 control). DIS added only +0.0052 AP [+0.0011; +0.0108] to q_birth, and the fly added +0.006 AP over its own input (+0.010 at night). Expected: an STMD-specific readout on night small candidates gives at most +0.01 AP over geometry+score+DIS, probably not above the classical ESTMD/vSTMD control [uncertain]. Tracker level: small distant objects weigh little in HOTA (poor IoU localization for tiny boxes), so ΔHOTA is about 0 to +0.1 [uncertain]. The plausible benefit is fewer false or late night births and fewer IDSw through the deferred-birth policy. A kinematic caveat: distant vehicles near the focus of expansion move below 1 px/frame at 30 Hz, where STMD responses are weak. Objects that move fast in the image are usually near and already detected.

## Риски
(1) Night distant targets are mostly bright lamps (ON polarity), whereas STMD/LC11 models are tuned to small dark targets; glare, rain streaks and reflections produce false small-target responses. (2) Ego-motion moves the whole background, so wide-field inhibition must be effective; combine with C6 compensation. (3) Resolution mismatch: the tiled column step is about 13.3 px, while RIST targets are 1-3 px at 240 Hz, so STMD temporal filters must be retuned for 30 Hz. (4) Labels: BDD100K may not label very small objects, labels are 5 Hz, and the birth horizon is 3 reference frames, so negatives may be unlabeled true objects. (5) Detector rule: q_birth re-scoring of YOLO26 candidates is compliant. Motion-only proposals that add boxes introduce a new detection source; report them separately, because they change DetA and box extents from STMD are poor. (6) Project rules: one condition per comparison; no gains without per-video bootstrap CI; no sign transfer from short runs; H3 was negative, so the criteria must be written before the run; negatives are not novelty.
