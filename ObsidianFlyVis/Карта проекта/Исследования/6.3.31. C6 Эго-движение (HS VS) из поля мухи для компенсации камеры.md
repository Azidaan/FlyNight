# 6.3.31. C6 Эго-движение (HS VS) из поля мухи для компенсации камеры

Estimate the camera's ego-motion (pan/yaw, tilt/pitch, roll, forward-translation expansion) for every frame from the fly's direction-selective lobula-plate outputs, then use it for camera motion compensation (CMC/GMC) in the tracker. The Kalman predictions get warped before association, the same way BoT-SORT's sparseOptFlow GMC does it. FlyVis has T4a-d/T5a-d but no HS/VS tangential cells, so the 'HS/VS' stage would be our own matched-filter readout (Krapp/Franz-style flow templates or a ridge decoder) over full-frame tiled T4/T5 activity. The promising variant is not a fly GMC in place of CV GMC. It is a reliability-gated GMC: the fly estimate, or its agreement with DIS/feature GMC, decides when compensation is trustworthy, especially in dark scenes where feature-based GMC fails.
