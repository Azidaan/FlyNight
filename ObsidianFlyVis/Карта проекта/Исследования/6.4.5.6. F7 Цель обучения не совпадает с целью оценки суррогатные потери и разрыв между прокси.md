# 6.4.5.6. F7 Цель обучения не совпадает с целью оценки суррогатные потери и разрыв между прокси и итоговой метрикой

> Муха обучена на потоке Sintel, применялась к опознанию; AP пар не доходит до HOTA; обучение от итоговой метрики, дифференцируемые метрики трекинга, задаче-ориентированная предобработка.

## Механизм
Every module in the project was optimised (or selected) against a proxy that is several causal steps away from HOTA, and the gradient (or the selection signal) of that proxy is only weakly aligned with the gradient of HOTA. Three distinct mechanisms produce the gap. (1) Task mismatch of the pretrained module. FlyVis (Lappalainen et al., Nature 2024) was fitted end-to-end to decode optical flow on Sintel; its 734 free parameters encode whatever helps a flow decoder on rendered film scenes, not what separates 'same object / different object' or 'object / background' in night dash-cam video. A module optimised for task A is only useful for task B to the extent that the sufficient statistics of A and B overlap. Sevilla-Lara et al. (GCPR 2018) showed this directly for flow: end-point error of a flow method is only weakly correlated with the action-recognition accuracy of a network that consumes it; what matters is accuracy at motion boundaries and small displacements, and training flow on the recognition loss improves recognition. Xue et al. (TOFlow, IJCV 2019) showed that a flow network trained jointly with the downstream task (interpolation, denoising, super-resolution) beats a fixed, more accurate pretrained flow: 'precise flow' is a sub-optimal representation for a given task. (2) Surrogate-to-metric gap inside one stage. Detection is trained with per-anchor classification + box losses, but evaluated by ranking-based AP and, downstream, by thresholded recall at a fixed FP budget. Ranking-based losses (AP-Loss 2019, aLRP 2020, Rank & Sort loss, Oksuz et al. ICCV 2021) exist precisely because the classification surrogate does not optimise the ranking that AP measures; RS loss gives about +1 to +2.2 AP over focal loss on ATSS and +3 box AP on Faster R-CNN on COCO. Similarly, pair-verification AP (the fly-signature probe) is a surrogate for association, and association is only one of the two factors of HOTA (HOTA = sqrt(DetA x AssA), Luiten et al. IJCV 2021). (3) Stage-wise composition gap. Even if every stage is optimised for its own metric, the final metric is a non-differentiable composition (thresholds, Hungarian matching, track birth/death rules). A gain at stage k reaches the final metric only through the 'transfer coefficient' of the downstream stages; if a downstream stage is saturated (association by geometry already near its accessible ceiling) the coefficient is about zero and any stage-k gain is invisible. DeepMOT (Xu et al., CVPR 2020) is the canonical attempt to close this: a Deep Hungarian Net makes MOTA/MOTP differentiable and lets the tracker be trained on them. Its lesson is double: it helps weak, mis-trained components a lot (GOTURN 38.3 -> 48.1 MOTA, SiamRPN 47.8 -> 52.1 on MOT17) but gives almost nothing on a strong, well-tuned pipeline (Tracktor 53.5 -> 53.7 MOTA, IDF1 52.3 -> 53.8). In plain words: optimising the final metric matters most where the current component was trained for the wrong thing - which is exactly FlyVis's situation, but not YOLO26's. Musgrave et al. ('A Metric Learning Reality Check', ECCV 2020) add the warning that surrogate-loss improvements in metric learning mostly vanish under fair tuning, so changing the loss without changing the information or the bottleneck rarely moves the final number.

## Где видно у нас
(a) FlyVis trained on Sintel flow is a poor flow source on BDD100K: motion error 52.77 px vs 4.87 px for DIS, and in the tracker DeltaHOTA ~ 0 - the module's own training proxy (Sintel flow decoding) does not transfer to the target proxy (dash-cam displacement), let alone to HOTA. (b) As an identity signature the fly gives +0.14 AP over geometry on the pair-verification proxy, but only +0.006 AP [+0.001; +0.012] over its own 721-facet input and is worse than the input alone (-0.052 AP): the untrained-for-this-task network destroys part of the information it receives - a classic signature of a module optimised for a different objective. (c) Proxy does not reach the final metric: pair AP gains do not translate into HOTA; the final tracker goes 58.47 -> 59.27 HOTA, but all of that comes from geometric stitching (+0.79 HOTA) and appearance/fly over geometry are not significant; DetA is unchanged (58.21 -> 58.17) while FN 15,763 >> IDSw 2,221, so the stage the fly was tuned to help (association) is not the stage that limits HOTA. (d) Detector surrogate vs. thresholded recall: the confidence target in Ultralytics is multiplied by IoU of the best box (plan, ultralytics/utils/tal.py:140-142); Г0а found an imprecise box co-occurs with ~40% of the night under-scoring (40.4% [32.6; 51.5]), yet fine-tuning without the IoU factor changed night recall at equal FP by -0.15 pp [-0.32; +0.03] and mAP50 by -0.0006 (conf_target_probe_v1) - removing a surrogate artefact does not help when the shared cause is the hard object itself. (e) DRB: training loss is minimised by switching a branch off (branch weight = 0 in all 896 channels, frequency mask converged to neutral 4 of 4) - the detection loss saw no use for the branch; seed spread 0.0144 mAP at 12 epochs and the sign flip at 100 epochs show that the short-run proxy (12-epoch mAP) is not a proxy for the long-run metric. (f) Retina-lamina front-end removes the fly's night dip (+0.016 AP) - the pair proxy improves - but the frozen YOLO26 on photoreceptor/accumulated frames loses 2-15 pp recall at equal FP (fly_eye_frontend_v1): a front-end that is good by one proxy is harmful for a downstream module trained on a different input distribution.

## Решения из литературы
1) Train the module on the downstream task loss, not on its original pretext (task-oriented training). TOFlow (IJCV 2019): flow trained jointly with the task outperforms fixed accurate flow (EpicFlow / pretrained SpyNet) on all three video tasks on Vimeo-90K [exact dB margins uncertain]. Sevilla-Lara et al. 2018: flow fine-tuned on the action-recognition loss improves recognition accuracy although its EPE does not improve. Liu et al. IJCAI 2018: a denoiser trained with a joint denoising + segmentation loss gives higher mIoU than a separately trained denoiser and generalises across tasks. 2) Differentiable or learned surrogates of the final metric. DeepMOT (CVPR 2020): differentiable MOTA/MOTP via Deep Hungarian Net; MOT17 test: Tracktor 53.5 -> 53.7 MOTA, 52.3 -> 53.8 IDF1, IDS 2072 -> 1947; for weak trainable components much more (GOTURN 38.3 -> 48.1 MOTA, SiamRPN 47.8 -> 52.1). Ranking losses for detection: RS loss +1.8/+2.2 AP on ATSS vs focal loss, +3 box AP on Faster R-CNN (COCO). No published differentiable-HOTA loss was found; HOTA itself uses a surrogate matching objective [uncertain whether a 2024-2026 differentiable HOTA loss exists]. 3) End-to-end association learned against identity labels inside the tracker rather than via a pair-AP probe: MOTIP (CVPR 2025) casts association as in-context ID prediction and reaches 69.6 HOTA on DanceTrack without extra data, +4.3 HOTA / +6.9 AssA over CO-MOT; MOTRv2 shows the counterpart warning: end-to-end MOTR lost to tracking-by-detection 'mainly due to poor detection'; with YOLOX proposals as anchors DanceTrack test HOTA goes 54.2 -> 69.9 (DetA 73.5 -> 83.0, AssA 40.2 -> 59.0) and BDD100K 43.6 mMOTA / 56.5 mIDF1 vs Unicorn 41.2 / 54.0. 4) Online target-domain adaptation of an appearance cue instead of offline proxy training: GHOST (CVPR 2023) shows reID trained on reID datasets is inconsistent on MOT sequences; on-the-fly domain adaptation + separate policies for active/inactive tracks give +2.6 IDF1 / +1.3 HOTA over SOTA on MOT20 public detections. 5) Methodological fix (cheapest): choose the training objective as the closest differentiable proxy of the stage that actually limits HOTA (here: detection recall at fixed FP on night/age-0 objects), and verify proxy-to-final transfer on cached outputs before training (rank correlation between proxy deltas and HOTA deltas across already-run variants).

## Что сделать с мухой
Concrete recipe that removes the Sintel-flow objective mismatch while keeping the fly as the subject. (A) Keep the connectome topology and the 734 parameters, but re-train them on a target-task loss instead of Sintel flow: the pair 'same object / different object' contrastive loss computed on BDD100K track crops (cached tracks exist, plan K2, 2-4 h GPU + 1 day code), with the facet input and retina-lamina front-end in front, and a small trainable read-out after the output cells. Two training arms: (A1) all 734 parameters free, (A2) only the read-out free (fly frozen) - A1 minus A2 is the effect of task-oriented fly training, which is the claim the dissertation can own. (B) If the target is detection (where HOTA is limited), the fly must be trained through the detector loss, not through a separate proxy: put a FlyVis-derived map as an extra input channel / FPN side input to YOLO26 initialised as a zero-init residual (so the detector starts at baseline), and back-propagate the YOLO detection loss into the fly parameters (A1) vs fly frozen (A2). Use a ranking-aware or recall-at-fixed-FP oriented loss on the fly branch only if (A) shows a significant signal. (C) Motion: if the fly is used as a motion source again, train its decoder on BDD100K displacement targets from DIS or GT box motion (task-oriented flow, TOFlow logic), and evaluate the decoder by center-error inside tracked boxes, not by Sintel EPE. (D) What stays frozen: YOLO26 weights in the first pilot (to isolate the fly), the tracker hyper-parameters (XYWHByteTrack + cascade + deferred birth + stitching) and the evaluation protocol. (E) Report the proxy and the final metric side by side for every variant (pair AP, recall at equal FP, DetA, AssA, HOTA) so the transfer coefficient proxy->HOTA is measured, not assumed.

## Тест до постройки
1) Proxy-to-final transfer check on already-run variants (no new training): for all tracker variants with cached outputs (stand, cascade, deferred birth, stitching, appearance, fly signature, combined), regress DeltaHOTA (and DeltaAssA) on Delta pair-AP of the cue used; if the slope CI includes 0 or Spearman < 0.5, a better pair-AP fly cannot be expected to move HOTA. Already known: fly +0.006 AP over input, appearance over geometry not significant in HOTA -> slope ~ 0. 2) Oracle of the proxy: replace the fly signature by a perfect same/different oracle restricted to the cases where geometry-only stitching is ambiguous; the resulting HOTA gain is the ceiling for any re-trained fly signature. If it is < +0.3 HOTA, skip K2 for HOTA and keep it only as a fly-science result. 3) Linear probe / CKA at the fly output for the target label: train a linear probe on frozen fly features to predict (a) same/different, (b) object vs background at night, (c) GT displacement; compare with the same probe on the 721-facet input. If fly < input (as now: -0.052 AP), task-oriented re-training has room; if fly ~ input after a cheap 1-epoch fine-tune of the 734 parameters, the topology itself carries no task advantage. 4) Gradient alignment test before full training: on 200 mini-batches compute the cosine between the gradient of the surrogate loss (pair contrastive or detection loss) w.r.t. fly parameters and the gradient of a smoothed recall-at-fixed-FP proxy; median cosine near 0 predicts that the surrogate will not improve the final metric.

## Потолок
For association-side fly use: geometry-only stitching already gives +0.79 HOTA (58.47 -> 59.27 combined), and appearance/fly beyond it is not significant; the measured association oracle is AssA 49.79 -> 73.40 (+47%) on the older ByteTrack stand, but the accessible spread among real association algorithms is only 5.78 mMOTA vs 24.13 for swapping the detector (plan [§5↗](obsidian://open?vault=ObsidianFlyVis&file=05%20%D0%9F%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%BD%2F5.%20%D0%A7%D1%82%D0%BE%20%D0%B6%D0%B8%D0%B2%D0%BE) items 10-11). So a perfectly task-trained fly signature can plausibly add at most a fraction of +1 HOTA [uncertain; to be fixed by diagnostic 2]. For detection-side fly use: HOTA ~ sqrt(DetA x AssA), so dHOTA/dDetA ~ 0.5 x sqrt(AssA/DetA) ~ 0.51 at DetA 58.2 / AssA 61.3; returning all night threshold losses (21.0% of night GT vs 15.5% by day) is the large ceiling, but the literature-based expectation for a bio-inspired detector add-on is DetA +0.1..+0.6 (research_fly_detector summary), i.e. +0.05..+0.3 HOTA. DeepMOT-style metric training on an already strong pipeline gives ~ +0.2 MOTA / +1.5 IDF1 (MOT17), which is the realistic ceiling of 'optimise the final metric' when the components are already good.

## Где обязано помогать
Must help: (1) night videos and night age-0 objects (first appearance), where night recall at equal FP is 0.65 and age-0 recall 0.39 (conf_target_probe_v1 baseline); (2) occluded objects at night (Г0а: 30.0% of the age gap explained by IoU among occluded); (3) for association use - geometry-ambiguous stitching cases (gaps > N frames, crossing tracks). No-harm checks: day recall at equal FP (0.71) within -0.2 pp; overall mAP50 at 640 val (0.4286) within seed spread; HOTA/IDF1 on val7 and the 70 held-out videos not below the stand by more than the bootstrap CI; car class separately (83% of night boxes weigh DetA) and rare classes rider/bus/motorcycle/bicycle separately (97% of the night mAP gap).

## Отрицательный контроль
Negative controls: (1) same training objective and schedule with the shuffled connectome (fly_connectome_null_v1 protocol) - a task-trained gain must exceed the shuffled-connectome gain; (2) same capacity non-fly module (a 734-parameter conv stack or MLP on the same 721-facet input) trained on the same loss - controls for 'any small trainable module trained on the task'; (3) fly frozen at Sintel weights with the same trainable read-out (A2) - isolates the effect of task-oriented fly training. Matched controls: identical input (facet sampling + retina-lamina), identical crops/tracks, identical tracker and thresholds, equal FP operating point; DIS with the same retina input for any motion claim (dis_retina_control_v1). A gain counts for the fly only if A1 > A2, A1 > shuffled, A1 > equal-capacity non-fly, each with bootstrap CI over videos excluding 0.

## Вклад ветви
Log per epoch: (i) L2 norm of the fly branch output relative to the main path (zero-init residual gate value or the scale of the injected channel), (ii) ratio of gradient norms ||dL/dtheta_fly|| / ||dL/dtheta_backbone||, (iii) cosine between surrogate-loss gradient and the recall-proxy gradient w.r.t. fly parameters, (iv) epoch-wise ablation: evaluate with the fly branch zeroed every 2 epochs on a fixed 200-frame night subset; the contribution is the gap (with-fly minus zeroed). A gate collapsing to 0 (as DRB's branch did in all 896 channels) or a gradient ratio < 1e-3 for 3 consecutive checks is a stop criterion.

## Минимальный эффект и стоимость
From project CIs: HOTA paired bootstrap over test videos - the combined tracker +0.80 HOTA had CI [+0.08; +1.44], SE ~ 0.35, so MDE (alpha 0.05 two-sided, power 0.8, ~2.8 SE) ~ 1.0 HOTA: any fly effect expected < 0.5 HOTA is not detectable on HOTA directly and must be shown on a closer proxy. Recall at equal FP (paired, same frames): conf_target_probe CI half-width ~ 0.17 pp, SE ~ 0.09 pp, MDE ~ 0.25 pp at night - this is the right primary metric for a detection-side fly. Pair AP: +0.006 [+0.001; +0.012] -> SE ~ 0.003, MDE ~ 0.008 AP. Detector seeds: short-run spread 0.0144 mAP at 12 epochs (MDE in mAP unusable at 12 epochs; the Ultralytics seed flag when fine-tuning from the same weights changes nothing, so replicate variance has to be created explicitly, e.g. by different data order or subsets [uncertain how much variance that produces]). Cost on RTX 5070 12 GB: fly re-training on pairs 2-4 h GPU + 1 day code; fly-as-input-to-YOLO fine-tune 2 epochs at 960 ~ 2-3 h per arm, 3 arms (A1, A2, shuffled) + equal-capacity control = 4 runs ~ 8-12 h; a 12-epoch run ~ 4.3 h, so a 3-seed x 4-arm 12-epoch design ~ 52 h (too expensive; use 2-epoch fine-tunes with paired recall-at-equal-FP as primary).

## Вердикт для проекта
Primary for the fly as a module (the 734 parameters were never trained for any task in this project; '-0.052 AP below its own input' and '52.77 px vs 4.87 px' are textbook objective-mismatch symptoms), secondary for HOTA (even a perfectly re-trained fly in the association role is capped by the saturated association stage; the HOTA limit is detection, see F9). Priority: high for the fly-science line (K2-style task-oriented re-training, A1 vs A2 vs shuffled vs equal-capacity), and the same principle - train through the loss of the limiting stage - should be applied when the fly is attached to the detector. Doubt about novelty: task-oriented training of a fixed module is standard (TOFlow, DeepMOT, IA-YOLO); what is not standard is doing it with a connectome-constrained network and controlling for topology - record in cannot_claim that the training principle itself is not new.

*Неуверенные поля: solutions (exact TOFlow dB margins; existence of a 2024-2026 differentiable HOTA loss), ceiling (fraction of +1 HOTA for a perfectly task-trained fly signature; DetA +0.1..+0.6 is a literature-based expectation, not a measurement)*
