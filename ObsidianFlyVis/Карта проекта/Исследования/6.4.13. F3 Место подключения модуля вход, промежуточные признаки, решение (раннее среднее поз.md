# 6.4.13. F3 Место подключения модуля вход, промежуточные признаки, решение (раннее среднее позднее слияние)

> Где в сети модуль может помочь; почему пересчёт после решения и подмена входа проигрывают; промежуточное слияние с обучаемым мостом; данные по RGB-T, RGB-событиям 2022–2025.

## Механизм
A module can enter a detector at three places, and each has a different failure mode. (1) Input (early fusion / input substitution): the module replaces or is concatenated to pixels. If the detector is frozen, any change of input statistics is a covariate shift for every layer - accuracy falls even when the new input is more informative. If the detector is retrained, early fusion mixes low-level statistics of very different signals; Liu et al. (BMVC 2016) argue that early fusion of low-level features fuses task-irrelevant detail and undermines the fusion. (2) Decision (late / score fusion, post-hoc rescoring): the module only re-ranks candidates the detector already produced. It is bounded twice: it cannot recover objects with no candidate (recall ceiling = candidate recall), and it only helps if its evidence is conditionally independent of the detector score; with a strong detector the score already summarises box precision and short-term consistency, so the residual is small. Late fusion also misses cross-modal interactions at the spatial level. (3) Intermediate (halfway / mid-level fusion with a learned bridge): features of the second stream are injected into the backbone/neck at a level where they are 'semantic enough but still spatial'; the host can learn how to use them and they can affect proposals, so they can reduce misses, not just re-rank. This is consistently the best placement in RGB-thermal and RGB-event detection. But placement is necessary, not sufficient: a mid-level branch computed from the same pixels (no new information, see F1) collapses, and even a branch with new information can be starved by the dominant stream (modality competition: Wang et al. CVPR 2020 - A+RGB late fusion 71.4 % vs RGB-only 72.6 % on Kinetics; Peng et al. CVPR 2022 OGM-GE). The bridge must therefore (a) start as an identity so it cannot hurt the host (zero-initialised convolutions, as in ControlNet, Zhang et al. ICCV 2023), (b) be kept alive by an auxiliary loss or gradient modulation, and (c) carry a signal the host lacks (another sensor, or time for a single-frame detector).

## Где видно у нас
The project has tried all three placements and each failed for the predicted reason. Input: frozen YOLO26 on frames after the fly photoreceptor and temporal accumulation: -2...-15 pp (tavg3 -2.0 pp at night, photoreceptor -9.5 pp), blindness grows - covariate shift of a frozen host (G3). Decision: rescoring weak candidates by 5 Hz tubelets (G0b: rules -12...-47 %, learned head night +1.6 % [-0.6; +3.9], day -4.6 %) and 30 Hz tubelets with background-relative motion (G0b-30: dev+test -0.1 %, val7 +1.5 %, CI through 0) - ~0 at equal FP; blind objects (4.5 %) are unreachable from this place by construction; IoU-free confidence target -0.15 pp at night (G2). Tracker-level decision fusion: HOTA 58.47 -> 59.27 but DetA 58.21 -> 58.17, FN 15 763 >> IDSw 2 221 - modules after the detector improve linking, not detection; fly and appearance beyond geometry stitching (+0.79 HOTA) not significant; fly as motion source Delta HOTA ~ 0 (error 52.77 vs 4.87 px for DIS). Intermediate: DRB in the YOLO neck - correct place, wrong content: spatial/frequency branches from the same feature map, one branch weight = 0 in all 896 channels, frequency mask neutral 4/4, seed spread 0.0144 mAP at 12 epochs, sign flip at 100 epochs, +17 % time. So the intermediate placement has never been tested with a signal the detector lacks (multi-frame fly responses).

## Решения из литературы
1) Mid-level fusion beats early and late fusion when the second stream carries new information. KAIST (Liu et al. 2016), log-average miss rate, all-day: color-only Faster R-CNN 48.59 %, thermal-only 47.59 %, Early Fusion 40.44 %, Halfway Fusion 36.99 %, Late Fusion 40.46 %, Score Fusion 40.06 %; night: color-only 64.39 %, Early 40.81 %, Halfway 35.49 %, Late 41.84 %, Score 38.50 %. Halfway fusion was ~3.5 points better than the other fusion stages and ~11 points better than single-modality. 2) Learned cross-modal bridges at several backbone stages: CFT on a two-stream YOLOv5, FLIR mAP 37.4 -> 40.2 (+2.8; mAP50 +5.7), LLVIP mAP 62.3 -> 63.6 (+1.3); single-stream RGB-only YOLOv5 on FLIR 31.8 mAP, thermal-only 39.5. Note the base here is the two-stream mid-fusion network itself, i.e. the bridge design adds +1.3...+2.8 mAP over naive mid fusion. 3) RGB + events (time as new information): DAGr (Nature 2024), image-only 33.8 mAP vs hybrid 37.2 mAP on DSEC-Det, ResNet-50 variant 41.9 mAP, and in inter-frame detection 44.2 mAP, +6.7 mAP over image-based extrapolation; a 20 fps camera plus events matches the latency of a 5000 fps camera at the bandwidth of a 45 fps camera [uncertain: exact pairing of the 33.8/37.2 numbers with backbone and setting]. RENet (ICRA 2023) fuses event frames from several temporal scales with bi-directional attention at feature level, 38.38 mAP on DSEC [uncertain]; MCFNet (2025) reports +7.4 % mAP50 / +1.7 % mAP on DSEC-Det over prior fusion methods with temporal alignment of events to frames before cross-modal fusion. 4) Feature-level enhancement trained by the detection loss instead of pixel-level enhancement: FeatEnHancer +5.7 mAP on ExDark (vs fixed pre-enhancement that does not help, LIME-Eval 2024). 5) Bridge engineering to avoid collapse and harm: zero-initialised residual injection so training starts exactly at the host (ControlNet zero convolutions); auxiliary loss on the branch alone or gradient blending / OGM-GE (CREMA-D concat 51.7 % -> 61.9 %) to keep the weaker stream learning; modality dropout.

## Что сделать с мухой
1) Place: a parallel fly stream into the YOLO26 neck at P3 (stride 8, small objects) and P4 (stride 16), not at the input and not after NMS. 2) Content: fly responses over the last k = 3...10 frames at 30 Hz (lamina adaptation, T4/T5 motion, T2/T3 small-object channels), resampled from the hexagonal ommatidia grid to the feature grid (column scale ~13 px is fixed, so resample features, not the fly). This is the one signal the single-frame detector does not have (analogous to events in DAGr/RENet). 3) Bridge: 1x1 conv + norm -> zero-initialised gated residual added to the neck feature (ControlNet-style), optionally cross-attention as in CFT later. 4) Training in two stages: (a) backbone and fly frozen, train bridge + neck for 2 epochs from best.pt (cheap pilot, infrastructure of scripts/conf_target_probe.py), (b) unfreeze the fly's 734 parameters + retina at a 10-100x lower learning rate (after the K1 self-supervised fine-tuning on BDD). 5) Keep the branch alive: auxiliary objectness head on the fly stream alone (deep supervision) and OGM-style down-weighting of the dominant RGB gradient if the branch/main gradient ratio collapses. 6) Identical-shape matched control: the same bridge fed with frame differences or DIS flow and with the shuffled-connectome fly. 7) Do not resume input substitution or post-hoc rescoring: both are closed by measurement (G3; rule 6.14).

## Тест до постройки
1) Placement-specific conditional probe (no detector training): at the P3/P4 grid cells of GT objects missed at 0.25 and of true negatives, fit probes Y ~ [neck features] vs Y ~ [neck features, fly features over k frames]; Delta in held-out bits/candidate with video bootstrap, per slice (night, age 0, occluded), plus the same with DIS / frame-difference features instead of the fly and conditioned on them. This answers 'is there usable information at the place where the bridge will inject it'. 2) Candidate-recall ceiling for late fusion (already implied by data): fraction of night GT with any candidate above 0.01 at IoU >= 0.5; objects outside it can only be recovered by early/mid placement. 3) Frozen-host shift check (already done in G3): if the frozen detector degrades on processed input, input substitution is ruled out unless the host is retrained; use K5 (2-epoch retrain on processed frames) only as a shift control, not as the main route. 4) Zero-init sanity test: after inserting the bridge with zero init, mAP must equal best.pt to all digits; after 2 epochs with the branch input replaced by zeros, the change measures the cost of the extra training alone.

## Потолок
Late fusion / rescoring: at most the night threshold losses (21.0 % of night GT vs 15.5 % by day), and in practice ~0 % of it was reached (G0b, G0b-30); 0 % of blind objects (4.5 %) by construction. Input substitution on a frozen host: negative (-2...-15 pp). Mid fusion with a new-information stream: literature range against single-modality bases is large with a new sensor (KAIST night miss rate 64.39 % -> 35.49 %, i.e. ~45 % relative; DSEC +3.4 mAP, ~10 % relative) but only +1.3...+2.8 mAP for bridge design over naive mid fusion (CFT). For the fly as a temporal stream from the same camera the expectation is much lower: plan estimate 0...+1 pp for retina/dense-fly input channels (K4/K9); a realistic target is recovering 10-20 % of the night-specific threshold gap of 5.5 pp, i.e. +0.5...+1.1 pp night recall at equal FP [uncertain: no same-camera temporal-bio-branch result against a strong detector exists in the literature].

## Где обязано помогать
Night recall at equal FP at the 0.25 threshold; age-0 (newborn) objects; small objects (P3 path); partially occluded objects; rare night classes (rider, bus, motorcycle, bicycle carry 97 % of the night mAP gap) for mAP, cars (83 % of night boxes) for DetA/FN. No-harm: day subset and overexposure subset within the seed-spread CI, FP per frame unchanged, and the 70 held-out videos; with zero-init the starting point equals best.pt, so any day drop is attributable to training.

## Отрицательный контроль
Same bridge, same epochs, branch fed with zeros (cost of extra training/capacity); same bridge fed with frame differences, DIS flow and the mean of 3-5 homography-aligned frames (non-biological temporal controls, matched in shape and in number of frames); shuffled-connectome fly; time-shuffled frames for the fly stream; mid-fusion placement vs input concatenation of the same fly features (tests placement holding content fixed - the one-condition comparison the plan requires). Seeds injected into the dataloader generator (rule 6.12).

## Вклад ветви
Per epoch: norm of the zero-init bridge weights and gate value (DRB-style collapse to 0 = stop); ratio of gradient norms at the fusion point (branch/main, OGM-GE discrepancy ratio from per-stream auxiliary heads); dev mAP and night recall with the branch zeroed at evaluation; auxiliary head loss of the fly stream alone.

## Минимальный эффект и стоимость
Pilot: 2 epochs from best.pt with the bridge, ~0.7 h per run (12 epochs ~ 4.3 h on RTX 5070 12 GB), 4 arms (fly, zeros, frame-difference/DIS, shuffled fly) x 3 seeds = 12 runs ~ 9 h, plus fly feature extraction on train (1.2 ms per step at 1/16 density, 15.8 ms full; caching required, disk heavy). Full comparison at 12 epochs: sigma = 0.0144 mAP gives MDE ~ 0.033 mAP for 3 seeds per arm (6 runs, 26 h), 0.018 for 10 seeds per arm (20 runs, 86 h). Since the expected effect (0...+1 pp) is below the 3-seed MDE, use the paired night-recall-at-equal-FP metric with video bootstrap (smaller variance than mAP across seeds) and pre-register the probe outcome as the go/no-go.

## Вердикт для проекта
Secondary but decisive for design (priority 2, after F1/F2). 'The module is in the wrong place' explains G3 (input on a frozen host) and G0b/G0b-30/G2 (decision level, cannot touch blind objects, redundant with the score); DRB shows that the right place without new information also fails. The only untested combination is the one the literature supports: mid-level fusion of a stream with information the detector lacks (multi-frame fly responses) through a zero-init, kept-alive bridge, with the fly fine-tuned on BDD. Run the placement-specific probe first. Novelty doubt, briefly: mid-level fusion and zero-init bridges are standard; only the connectome temporal stream is new, and it can be claimed only if it beats the matched frame-difference/DIS stream.
