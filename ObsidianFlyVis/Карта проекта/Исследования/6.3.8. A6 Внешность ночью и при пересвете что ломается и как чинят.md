# 6.3.8. A6 Внешность ночью и при пересвете что ломается и как чинят

At night, appearance cues for association degrade in four ways: sensor noise swamps shallow features, deep features respond weakly to objects, colour becomes unreliable under artificial lighting, and headlights create glare and blooming. Under overexposure, clipped pixels carry no texture at all, so that information is physically lost. Work since 2024 fixes this in four ways: (a) train for degradation-invariant features instead of enhancing images first (LTrack); (b) suppress glare with a mask of saturated pixels (DNDM vehicle re-ID); (c) distil illumination-invariant features from paired or enhanced images (nighttime person re-ID); (d) lean on motion and geometry when appearance fails. The fly-side analogue is already in the project: photoreceptor adaptation plus lamina contrast normalisation in front of FlyVis. Two cheap transfers remain: masking saturated facets in the fly input, and illumination-normalised variants of the stored appearance controls.
