# 6.2.5. D13 Биологические фронт-энды и нейронные данные как регуляризатор (VOneNet и последователи)

> Работы, где биологически ограниченный фронт-энд (VOneNet, модели сетчатки, EVNets, retina-inspired) или нейронные данные улучшают устойчивость или детекцию; есть ли прирост на детекции, а не только на классификации с порчей; дистилляция представлений биомодели в детектор как вспомогательная цель.

## Суть
A fixed, biologically constrained front-end (VOneNet: Gabor filter bank + simple/complex nonlinearities + Poisson-like noise; RetinaBlock/SubcorticalBlock: DoG centre-surround, light adaptation, contrast normalization; EVNets = both) is placed before a trainable CNN, or neural recordings are used as an auxiliary similarity/regression target (neural regularization, IT alignment). The consistent finding 2020-2025 is a gain in robustness to corruptions, adversarial noise and domain shift on image CLASSIFICATION, paid for with 2.5-5 points of clean accuracy. No primary paper found reports a gain on object DETECTION against a modern detector from such a front-end or neural regularizer; the detection-side analogue that does work is training-only feature distillation from a strong teacher (RT-DETRv4 with a DINOv3 teacher), and even that gives only +0.3-0.5 COCO AP. For the project this means: the fly is defensible as a training-time auxiliary signal or as a candidate-rescoring input, and least defensible as a fixed front-end replacing the detector input.

## Место мухи
Four ways to place the fly, ranked by what the literature supports. (a) Candidate rescoring input (best supported, cheapest): for every candidate in the conf band 0.01..threshold, run the foveal eye (721 columns, retina-lamina front-end that already removes the night dip) on the candidate box over the last k frames and feed a compact readout (L1/L2/Mi1/Tm3 contrast and adaptation state, T4/T5 motion-opponency inside box vs annulus) to a small head that raises or lowers the score. This attacks the measured failure (candidate exists but is underconfident) without touching the detector and mirrors the project's q_det/q_birth heads. (b) Training-time auxiliary target / distillation (the RT-DETRv4 pattern): a frozen fly map (full-frame, run as a shared-weight hex-conv recurrent net resampled to a square grid, since parameters are shared per cell type) is the teacher; a linear projector from a YOLO26 neck level (P3, stride 8, where small objects live) is trained with a patch-wise cosine loss to predict it; zero inference cost. Biologically meaningful targets are the lamina/medulla contrast-normalized channels (night: light adaptation) and T4/T5 direction maps (temporal evidence for objects at age 0 that move differently from ego-flow). (c) Extra input channel(s) (VOneNet/EVNet pattern): concatenate 2-4 fly channels (lamina ON/OFF contrast, T4/T5 opponent motion energy) to RGB with a zero-initialized first conv. Evidence from classification says this buys robustness at a clean-accuracy price; for detection it is unproven and needs a full-frame fly pass, which is the expensive part. (d) Replacing the input with a fixed bio front-end (pure VOneNet-style) - not recommended: EVNets lose 5.0 points clean ImageNet accuracy (75.4 -> 70.4), and the project's headroom is in recall of faint candidates, which a lossy fixed front-end can only reduce.

## Какую потерю закрывает
Primary: THRESHOLD (21.0% night vs 15.5% day, night score deficit +0.098). Rescoring (a) and distillation (b) both act on the score of candidates that already exist; the bio-front-end literature's actual benefit is invariance to illumination/contrast and noise (light adaptation, divisive normalization), which is exactly the night score deficit, not missing proposals. Secondary: BIRTH (age 0 found 31% vs 68% at age 10+, 2/3 through threshold) - only the motion part of the fly (T4/T5) carries evidence available at first appearance that a single-frame detector lacks; static front-ends (VOneNet, RetinaBlock) do not address it. Occlusion at night (penalty 5.4x): weak link - no bio-front-end paper evaluates occlusion. BLINDNESS (4.5%, equal day and night) is not targeted: front-ends do not create proposals the detector never forms, and the equality day/night says blindness is not an illumination problem. Small objects (56.7% found): only route (b)/(c) at stride 8 could help; the fly's native 721-column foveal resolution is too coarse for full-frame small objects unless run on tiles.

## Пересечение с чужими работами
Bio front-ends: VOneNet (2020), RetinaNets/EVNets (2024-2025), photoreceptor-adaptation front-ends (Idrees 2024) - all classification or neural-response prediction, mammalian (primate/mouse) models. Neural-data regularization: Li 2019, Safarani 2021, Dapello ICLR 2023, Attias 2024 - classification/adversarial. Bio-inspired detection: Bio-YOLO (X/Y-cell and V1 receptive-field pre-network, aerial small objects, 2026), dark-adaptation detection (Hung & Lin 2024, gamma/fusion, weak baseline), insect STMD models (dragonfly/hoverfly small-target motion detectors, mostly hand-crafted, synthetic or small datasets), event-camera (silicon retina) fusion on DSEC-Det. Fly connectome as a computer-vision component: Kuklev 2026 (frozen FlyVis, documents, matched baselines) and Dhiman 2026 (topology advantage vanishes under degree-preserving null). Not found: any connectome-constrained insect visual model used as a front-end, teacher or rescoring input for an object DETECTOR on driving video. Consequence for cannot_claim: cannot claim 'first biologically constrained front-end for detection' (Bio-YOLO, dark-adaptation detectors exist), cannot claim that biology per se causes robustness (Su & Kempe 2023 show a wavelet scattering front-end with plain Gaussian noise beats VOneBlock; Dhiman 2026 shows topology effects are fragile to the null model), and cannot claim novelty from absence of publications. What can be claimed if gates pass: a connectome fly model (with retina-lamina) as a source of training-time or rescoring evidence for an NMS-free detector on BDD100K MOT, with same-input and shuffled-connectome controls.

## Результаты у авторов
VOneNet (Dapello 2020, ImageNet, ResNet50/AlexNet/CORnet-S back-ends): +18% relative on a conglomerate perturbation benchmark vs base CNN and +3% vs the then state-of-the-art defense; clean accuracy retained but lower. EVNets (Piper 2025, ImageNet-1k, ResNet50): clean 75.4 (ResNet50) / 72.9 (VOneResNet50) / 70.4 (EVResNet50); aggregate robustness 26.8 / 34.5 / 36.1; ImageNet-C mean 38.8 / 40.4 / 41.9; domain shift 33.4 / 37.1 / 38.1; PGD 8.3 / 26.1 / 28.3; with PRIME augmentation 36.5 / 42.1 / 42.7 - i.e. on corruptions alone the gain over the plain CNN is only +3.1 points, the large gains are adversarial. RetinaNets (Piper 2024, Tiny-ImageNet-C, ResNet18): clean 58.00 -> 57.60 (Retina) / 55.70 (EV); relative robustness +12.7% (Retina), +18.1% (EV), +10.3% (VOne). Photoreceptor front-end (Idrees 2024): generalization to untrained light levels 54% +/- 11% vs 24% +/- 15% variance explained (retinal ganglion cell prediction, not detection). Neural alignment (Dapello ICLR 2023): IT-likeness correlates with adversarial robustness; classification only. Detection-side analogues: RT-DETRv4 (COCO, frozen DINOv3-ViT-B teacher, cosine loss on the top encoder feature): RT-DETRv2-L 52.1 +0.5, D-FINE-L 53.1 +0.3, DEIM-L 53.8 +0.5 AP; no YOLO results. Dark adaptation (Hung & Lin 2024): +1.5 to +6% mAP vs 'traditional models' on a simulated 0-lux-to-bright set [uncertain: baseline detector and dataset are not modern/standard]. Bio-YOLO: 53.9 mAP50 / 23.7 mAP50-95 on AI-TOD with 1.8M params [uncertain: gain over a matched YOLO baseline not verified]. MCFNet (RGB+events, DSEC-Det): +7.4% mAP50 and +1.7% mAP over prior fusion methods - requires a real event sensor. None of these use 30 Hz video from a moving car with 5 Hz labels except DSEC (moving car, but event camera).

## Проверка против сильной базы
No: none of the bio front-end papers compares against a modern detector, and the classification baselines are standard-trained ResNets, not robustness-trained strong models. The gains shrink sharply under stronger controls: (1) augmentation - with PRIME the EVNet margin over the plain CNN drops from +9.3 to +6.2 points; (2) same-information non-biological control - Su & Kempe 2023 show a wavelet scattering front-end with simple Gaussian noise exceeds VOneBlock robustness, i.e. the active ingredients are low-pass/multiscale filtering plus stochasticity, not biological fidelity; (3) topology control - Dhiman 2026 shows the apparent advantage of the flyvis connectome over random wiring disappears with shared random initialization and degree-preserving rewiring. Independent reproduction: VOneNet robustness has been reproduced and extended by the same group (Marques lab) and benchmarked by others (Su & Kempe), but not on detection. The only detection result with a strong base (RT-DETRv4 on DEIM/D-FINE) is from feature distillation from a 86M-parameter ViT teacher and is +0.3-0.5 AP - a realistic ceiling estimate for a much weaker fly teacher. The project's own controls already match this standard (shuffled connectome +0.044 AP, 'same input for DIS' control), and any detector claim must carry them.

## Разрез по условиям
Bio front-end papers: breakdown by corruption type (noise, blur, weather, digital - 75 corruption types in RetinaNets) and by adversarial strength; brightness/contrast corruptions are the closest proxy for night, but there is no real-night, glare, occlusion, small-object or first-appearance breakdown and no BDD100K. Idrees 2024: breakdown by light level (photopic to scotopic) - for neural responses only. Hung & Lin 2024: simulated illuminance levels 0 lux to bright [uncertain]. MCFNet/DSEC-Det: low-light and fast-motion traffic scenes. RT-DETRv4: COCO only. BDD100K: none.

## Дешёвая проверка
Gate D13-A (rescoring, 2-4 h, no detector training). Data: existing conf-0.01 detection caches on the 130/35/35 train/dev/test videos (runs/train_cache) and val7, the q_det event dataset (20 507 candidates, 11 987 positive), foveal fly flow with retina-lamina (runs/train_cache_retina_lamina4). Restrict to candidates in the threshold band (0.01 <= conf < operating threshold) that matter for the 21.0% / 15.5% losses. Heads (same logistic spec as [event_heads_v1↗](obsidian://open?vault=ObsidianFlyVis&file=02%20%D0%AD%D0%BA%D1%81%D0%BF%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%B5%D0%BD%D1%82%D1%8B%2Fevent_heads_v1)): base (conf, class, geometry, brightness), base+fly(retina-lamina readout), base+shuffled-fly, base+receptor-only (photoreceptor input of the same crop, no connectome = same-information control), base+DoG/wavelet control (non-biological front-end of the same crop, per Su & Kempe). Pre-registered pass rule: on test and val7, base+fly beats base, receptor-only and DoG controls by AUC >= +0.005 with paired bootstrap 95% CI excluding 0, and on night candidates raises recall at the precision of the current threshold by >= 2 points; otherwise the fly is not a detector-score signal and routes (b)/(c) are not opened. Note the prior negative: class/geometry/scene rescoring moved AUC 0.9025 -> 0.9005, so the gate tests information beyond that. Gate D13-B (distillability, 3-5 h): freeze best.pt, dump P3/P4 neck features on ~2 000 val frames (night-enriched), run the fly full-frame (hex-conv recurrent implementation, quarter resolution) to get lamina and T4/T5 maps; fit a linear probe neck->fly map. If R^2 is already high (> 0.8), the detector already contains the fly information and distillation cannot add anything; if low, fit the reverse probe fly->'threshold-lost object present' heatmap at GT locations of lost objects and require it to beat a probe from the same-resolution RGB/DoG control by AUC >= +0.01 (CI excludes 0). Only if B passes run a 2-epoch fine-tune from best.pt with the auxiliary cosine loss, 3 seeds, vs 3 seeds of 2-epoch fine-tune without it.

## Стоимость у нас
Gate A: CPU/GPU hours, all inputs cached except new control features (receptor-only and DoG per crop) - about 1-2 h extraction, minutes to fit. Gate B: neck feature dump ~0.5 h; full-frame fly on 2 000 frames is the unknown - foveal eyes cost ~214 s per video including detection and DIS, full-frame needs either many 721-column tiles or a re-implementation as a dense conv-recurrent net [uncertain: 1-4 h on RTX 5070]. Full test of route (b): fly maps for all training frames must be precomputed (BDD100K MOT train, ~278k labeled frames at 5 Hz plus temporal context) - likely tens of hours of fly inference [uncertain], then training 12 epochs ~4.3 h x 3 seeds x 2 arms ~26 h, and confirmation at 100 epochs (~35 h per run). Route (c) adds the same precompute plus a 4th-channel model trained from scratch or with a zero-initialized first conv from best.pt; inference cost at deployment includes the full-frame fly (route b has zero inference cost). Route (a) at inference: per-candidate foveal eye - the current per-track eye cost scaled to candidates in the threshold band (more candidates than tracks) [uncertain].

## Ожидаемый эффект
Route (a) rescoring: plausible but modest. The project's T4T5 readout did NOT improve q_match over base+DIS (AP 0.9432 vs 0.9454, CI fully negative), and scene/geometry rescoring did not move AUC; the fly has proved informative for identity (signature) and for motion reliability, not yet for 'is this an object'. Expected: AUC change 0 to +0.01, mostly on night candidates once retina-lamina is in [uncertain]. If it passes, translating into tracker FN: if night threshold losses fell from 21.0% toward the day level 15.5% by one quarter of the gap (~1.4 points), FN 15 763 would drop by a few hundred at fixed FP - DetA +0.2-0.5 [uncertain]. Route (b) distillation: literature prior +0.3-0.5 AP with a far stronger teacher on COCO; expected for the fly at 12 epochs is below the seed spread 0.0144 mAP, i.e. undetectable without >= 5 seeds per arm; night mAP50 change within +/-0.005 of 0.3772 [uncertain]. Route (c) input channel: classification analogues show robustness gains with clean losses; expected small net change at 12 epochs and risk of a sign flip at 100 epochs (as with DRB).

## Риски
1) Seed noise: 0.0144 mAP spread at 12 epochs exceeds every literature gain for detection-side bio signals (+0.3-0.5 AP); a one-seed result is uninterpretable - minimum 3, preferably 5 seeds per arm, paired by seed, CI on the difference. 2) Sign flip at 100 epochs (DRB lesson): auxiliary/front-end benefits often act as early regularizers; Yang et al. 2026 (arXiv 2608.04442) report robustness that emerges early in training and fades at convergence - confirm at the long schedule before claiming. 3) Branch without gradient: with a zero-initialized extra input channel or a small auxiliary loss the detector may ignore the fly; log gradient norm and weight magnitude of the fly path and the auxiliary loss value per epoch; a path with ~0 gradient is a failed experiment, not a negative result about the fly. 4) +17% time lesson: full-frame fly maps can cost more than the detector; route (b) keeps inference unchanged, route (c) does not. 5) Clean-accuracy tax: every bio front-end paper loses 0.4-5 points of clean accuracy; day mAP must be reported, not only night. 6) Same-information confound: any gain must beat the receptor-only and DoG/wavelet controls and the shuffled-connectome fly, otherwise it is preprocessing, not the fly. 7) Night proxy mismatch: ImageNet-C brightness is not BDD100K night (glare, headlights, noise, motion blur). 8) Licenses: VOneNet repo (check license before reuse), EVNet code CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (non-commercial, share-alike), flyvis package license must be checked [uncertain], Ultralytics AGPL-3.0 for the detector; BDD100K non-commercial research use.

## Как подписать заимствование
Borrowed: the hybrid 'fixed bio front-end + trainable back-end' design and robustness framing (Dapello et al. 2020; Piper, Oliveira, Marques 2024/2025), light-adaptation front-end idea (Idrees et al. 2024), neural-representation alignment as regularizer (Li 2019; Dapello 2023), training-time feature distillation with a frozen teacher and cosine loss (RT-DETRv4, Liao et al. 2025), non-biological control logic (Su & Kempe 2023), topology null-model caution (Dhiman 2026), and the fly model itself (Lappalainen et al. 2024). Ours: using the connectome fly with retina-lamina as a rescoring signal or distillation teacher for an NMS-free detector on BDD100K MOT; the threshold-band evaluation tied to the measured loss decomposition (threshold vs blindness vs birth); the same-input, DoG and shuffled-connectome controls for detection; and any measured result. Negative outcomes are reported as results of the gates, not as novelty.
