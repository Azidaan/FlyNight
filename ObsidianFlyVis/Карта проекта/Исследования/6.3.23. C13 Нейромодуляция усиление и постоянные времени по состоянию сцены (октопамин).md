# 6.3.23. C13 Нейромодуляция усиление и постоянные времени по состоянию сцены (октопамин)

Add a small modulator that reads a causal scene-state vector (mean luminance, contrast, glare fraction, ego-motion speed of the camera) and outputs global per-cell-type multipliers for FlyVis: a gain and a time-constant factor for each of the 65 types (about 130 numbers), applied on top of the fixed connectome and the trained parameters. Biological analogue: during flight octopaminergic neurons raise the gain of motion-sensitive neurons and speed up the inputs of T4/T5 (Mi1, Tm3, Mi4, Mi9), shifting temporal tuning towards higher frequencies. Engineering analogues are FiLM-style conditioning and neuromodulated networks, image-adaptive parameter prediction (IA-YOLO) and darkness prompts for a frozen tracker (DCPT). A zero-training variant sets the multipliers by hand from the biology (faster time constants at high ego-speed, higher gain in the dark).
