# 6.2.4.2. D5 Каналы движения на входе детектора поток, разность кадров, CenterTrack, псевдособытия

> Детекторы, получающие на вход движение (оптический поток, разность кадров, предыдущий кадр и тепловая карта прошлых детекций как в CenterTrack и PermaTrack), RGB и события для вождения ночью (DSEC-Det, PKU-DAVIS-SOD, псевдособытия v2e); прирост на мелких, ночных и рождающихся объектах; контроль «тот же вход без биомодели» — ключ к сравнению мухи с потоком DIS.

## Суть
Give the single-frame detector an explicit motion signal as extra input: a (motion-compensated) frame-difference map, optical flow, the previous frame plus a heatmap of previous detections (CenterTrack, PermaTrack), or events from an event camera (or pseudo-events simulated from video with v2e). Motion makes small, dim or camouflaged moving objects stand out from the background, and a prior-detection heatmap lets the detector keep objects it saw a moment ago. Evidence 2020-2026: large gains where the background is static or compensable and targets are tiny (drones: AP 0.53 -> 0.78), moderate gains with real event cameras against a strong RGB YOLO (DSEC-Det +1.7 mAP50-95; low-light PKU-DAVIS-SOD +0.023 mAP50), small-object gains from flow-guided temporal necks (XS-VID +3.2 AP), and fewer false negatives but more false positives from prior heatmaps (CenterTrack). A key negative control already exists in the literature: feeding two raw RGB frames without an explicit motion map was much worse than the motion map (and worse than single RGB) - i.e. the explicit motion transform matters, which is exactly the 'same input without the bio-model' question for the fly.

## Место мухи
Role: input channel (early fusion) or P3-level side channel of the detector, i.e. the fly as a learned-by-evolution motion and temporal-contrast transform of the same 30 Hz video, placed exactly where the literature puts frame-difference maps, flow or events. What to take from the fly: (a) lamina L1/L2 (and L3) after the retina-lamina front-end - transient ON/OFF temporal-contrast cells, functionally the closest biological analogue of DVS events (log-intensity change with adaptation), i.e. a 'biological pseudo-event' channel that already fixed the fly's night failure; (b) T4a-d/T5a-d - ON/OFF direction-selective motion maps (8 channels), analogous to optical flow direction but sparse and contrast-normalised; (c) optionally Tm/TmY types as mid-level spatio-temporal features. How: run FlyVis full-frame as a hex-lattice recurrent convolution over the 6-10 frames preceding each labelled frame (the unlabelled 30 Hz frames between 5 Hz labels are available), resample to input resolution (or stride 4-8), and append as extra input channels of YOLO26s whose new first-conv weights are zero-initialised (exact baseline at init), or inject at P3 via zero-conv (see D4). Ego-motion is the central issue for BDD: YOLOMG compensates camera motion with a homography before differencing, which is valid for distant/planar backgrounds but not for the strong parallax of a forward-moving car; the fly has no compensation either, so the channel should be the residual of T4/T5 relative to the expected ego-flow (e.g. local ring subtraction or a global flow model from DIS). The CenterTrack/PermaTrack view gives a second role: the fly's recurrent state is a ConvGRU-like memory; PermaTrack found that a recurrent ConvGRU over video made the explicit prior-detection heatmap redundant (Track mAP 59.6 with heatmap vs 60.6 without, 62.4 with video-length training), so the fly's recurrence could play the memory role at the input. The decisive comparison is fly vs the same frames through non-biological transforms: 2- and 3-frame differences (YOLOMG), v2e pseudo-events from the same video, DIS flow magnitude/direction, a ConvGRU of equal size, and topology-shuffled fly.

## Какую потерю закрывает
Primary: BIRTH (age 0 found 31% at night vs 68% at age 10+, 2/3 of the gap through the threshold). A newly appearing object has no track and no prior heatmap (CenterTrack's prior helps only already-seen objects), but if it moves relative to the background it produces motion/temporal-contrast evidence in its first frames; YOLOMG-type motion maps convert exactly such weak-appearance moving targets into detections (recall 0.31 with two raw RGB frames vs 0.69-0.71 with motion difference maps on ARD100). Secondary: THRESHOLD (21.0% night vs 15.5% day): a motion channel raises confidence of real moving candidates whose appearance is weak at night; real events help low-light most clearly (PKU-DAVIS-SOD low-light mAP50 0.351 -> 0.374). SMALL objects (56.7% found): the strongest literature gains are for tiny objects (XS-VID AP_es +3.7 for YOLOFT-S over YOLOv8-S) [uncertain: column mapping]; distant vehicles and pedestrians in BDD are the analogue, but their image motion at 30 Hz is small under forward ego-motion. BLINDNESS (4.5%): only an input-level motion channel can create new candidates; possible for moving objects, not for static ones. Night occlusion: CenterTrack-style prior heatmaps reduce FN of partially occluded already-tracked objects (KITTI FN 9.8% -> 5.8%), but the project tracker already carries buffers, so this overlaps with the tracker side. Not targeted: static parked objects (no motion signal), overexposure (events/lamina adaptation may help, unverified).

## Пересечение с чужими работами
Motion inputs to detectors are standard: frame-difference fusion (YOLOMG 2025 and earlier drone/satellite small-moving-object works), flow-guided aggregation (FGFA 2017, YOLOFT 2024), previous frame + prior heatmap (CenterTrack 2020, PermaTrack 2021), RGB-event fusion (RENet 2023, SODFormer 2023, DAGr 2024, CAFR 2024, MCFNet 2025), simulated events (v2e 2021). Insect-inspired motion processing for small-target detection exists as standalone models (Hassenstein-Reichardt EMD variants, STMD/LC-type networks, e.g. MDDC-STMD 2026; bio-inspired background subtraction, Bioinspir. Biomim. 2024), not as a channel of a modern trained detector in what was found. No published use of the connectome-constrained FlyVis (T4/T5, lamina) maps as an input channel to a YOLO/DETR detector was found [uncertain: search not exhaustive]. cannot_claim: motion/temporal-contrast input channels, event-style fusion, the use of a prior detection heatmap, ego-motion compensation, and the zero-init injection are all borrowed. A claim is possible only for 'a connectome-constrained fly front-end as the motion/temporal-contrast transform that beats equal-information non-biological transforms (frame difference, v2e, DIS, equal-size ConvGRU) on BDD100K night birth/threshold losses' - and only if the control comparison passes.

## Результаты у авторов
YOLOMG (ARD100 drone videos, 202,467 frames, tiny single-class targets, moving camera with homography compensation; YOLOv5s base): AP 0.53 (YOLOv5) / 0.55 (YOLOv11) / 0.64 (YOLOv9) -> YOLOMG-640 0.78, YOLOMG-1280 0.85; ablation: 3-frame difference k=2 P/R/AP 0.83/0.71/0.78, 2-frame k=1 0.79/0.67/0.73, 'RGB + RGB' (two raw frames, no difference map) 0.53/0.31/0.33; NPS-Drones 0.93 (YOLOv5) vs 0.92-0.95 (no gain on the easier set); night generalisation (trained without night): AP 0.84 vs 0.62 (YOLOv5) and 0.64 (YOLOv9); failure: hovering/slow drones missed. XS-VID (extremely small objects, drone-view video): YOLOv8-S AP 30.0 / AP_es 17.8 -> YOLOFT-S 33.2 / 21.5; YOLOv8-L 33.6 / 21.3 -> YOLOFT-L 36.4 / 24.7; StreamYOLO 33.4 / 18.7; 10 repeated runs: fusing motion from the start gave std 0.4 AP, training the static detector first then adding motion halved the std and added +0.3 AP. CenterTrack (validation; prior frame + prior heatmap vs detection-only with two images): MOT17 MOTA 63.6 -> 66.1, FN 30.3% -> 28.4%, FP 3.5% -> 4.5%; KITTI MOTA 84.3 -> 88.7, FN 9.8% -> 5.8%, FP 4.3% -> 5.4%. PermaTrack (synthetic PD, Track mAP): CenterTrack 60.3; ConvGRU video model 62.4 (visible-only supervision), 67.9 with invisible supervision + propagation. Real events, strong RGB base (MCFNet, DSEC-Det class-balanced): YOLOv11 RGB 84.5 mAP50 / 59.2 mAP -> MCFNet 90.7 / 60.9; CAFR 87.4 / 54.8 (mAP below the RGB baseline); events-only RVT 51.1 / 26.6. SODFormer (PKU-DAVIS-SOD, DAVIS346 346x260): mAP50 frames vs frames+events: normal 0.557 -> 0.569, motion blur 0.419 -> 0.432, low-light 0.351 -> 0.374, all 0.489 -> 0.504 [uncertain: column alignment in the extracted table]. DAGr (DSEC-Detection, YOLOX-style image branch): main benefit is in the blind time between 20 Hz frames - image-only degrades by 6.4-10.8 mAP after 50 ms while DAGr stays flat; 20 fps + events beats a 45 fps camera by 2.6 mAP [uncertain: exact values from secondary summary of the article]. v2e: detector pre-trained on v2e synthetic events improved night car detection on real DVS data by ~40% over YOLOv3 on intensity frames (test input = real events, not pseudo-events) [uncertain].

## Проверка против сильной базы
Mixed. Strongest check: MCFNet compares to YOLOv11 RGB on DSEC-Det and gains +6.2 mAP50 / +1.7 mAP50-95, but CAFR (ECCV 2024) is below the RGB YOLOv11 on mAP50-95 (54.8 vs 59.2) - real events do not automatically beat a modern RGB detector. YOLOMG compares to YOLOv5/v8/v9/v11 but on a narrow domain (tiny drones, sky/static backgrounds) where single-frame appearance is almost absent; on NPS-Drones the gain vanishes. YOLOFT compares to same-size YOLOv8 with 10-run variance for ablations - the best-controlled small-object result. CenterTrack's comparison is MOTA, not detection AP, and its gain trades FN for FP. Equal-information control: YOLOMG's 'RGB+RGB' row is the only explicit 'same frames, no motion transform' control found, and it is strongly negative for raw frames; no work compares a learned motion transform against hand-crafted ones at equal information on driving video. All real-event results add a new sensor; pseudo-events from 30 Hz RGB carry no new information beyond the frames, and no driving-domain paper was found showing pseudo-events beating RGB-only at test time [uncertain]. No independent reproductions found.

## Разрез по условиям
SODFormer: normal / motion blur / low-light split (PKU-DAVIS-SOD). YOLOMG: small (<12x12 px) vs larger, complex background, night generalisation test. XS-VID: extremely small / relatively small / generally small AP. DSEC-Det includes night sequences but MCFNet reports no night split; DAGr gives qualitative night examples only. CenterTrack/PermaTrack: occlusion (PermaTrack, full occlusion supervision) but no night split. No first-appearance (age 0) breakdown anywhere; no BDD100K results found for motion-input detectors.

## Дешёвая проверка
Gate 0a (hours, no training): motion evidence at ground truth vs candidates, on runs/det_cache_ctl_960 (baseline seed42, 960, all 200 val videos, conf>=0.01) restricted to a night-heavy subset (e.g. val7 + 20 dark val videos). For each GT object and each cached candidate, compute per-box features from five channels over the preceding 6-10 frames at 30 Hz: fly T4/T5 residual (box minus ring), fly lamina L1/L2 transient energy, 3-frame difference (YOLOMG, k=2), v2e pseudo-events count (default thresholds), DIS flow residual vs ring. Measures, all with video-level bootstrap 95% CI: (1) for candidates, AUC of logistic [score + channel] vs [score] (TP vs FP); (2) for the 'blind' 4.5% objects with no candidate, AUC of channel energy at the GT box vs size-matched random background boxes in the same frame (can motion create proposals?); (3) for age-0 night objects, recall at the operating threshold after re-scoring at fixed FP. Pre-registered pass: fly beats score-only by dAUC >= +0.005 (CI > 0) AND beats the best non-biological channel by dAUC >= +0.003 (CI > 0) or by >= +3 pp age-0 night recall at fixed FP; shuffled-topology fly loses >= half of the gain. If a non-biological channel wins, adopt it and report the fly result as negative (completed_negative). Gate 0b (motion compensation check, 1 h): repeat (1) with homography-compensated differences; if compensation changes nothing on BDD, ego-motion parallax dominates and only relative (ring-subtracted) features are usable.

## Стоимость у нас
Channel computation: frame difference and v2e are cheap (ms per frame, CPU) [uncertain: v2e at 960 with its interpolation step can be slower]; DIS is already in the project pipeline; full-frame FlyVis is the bottleneck - the per-track foveal fly runs at ~150-390 ms/frame per process, and a full-frame tiling has ~5-11x more columns than one 721-facet eye, so precomputing train-set maps is days of GPU time unless the fly is re-implemented as a batched hex-conv RNN [uncertain]. Gate 0 on a 27-video subset: a few hours. Training: early fusion with zero-initialised new input channels from best.pt, 12 epochs ~4.3 h per seed plus data-loading overhead for 8-16 extra channels (disk-cached float16 maps, I/O-bound) [uncertain: ~+10-30% time]; 3 seeds ~13-17 h; the same for each control channel (at least frame difference and DIS) - a fair comparison is 3 channels x 3 seeds ~40-50 h. 100-epoch confirmation ~35 h per run only for the winner. Inference: the motion channel itself is cheap except for the fly; first-conv change is negligible.

## Ожидаемый эффект
Realistic range: small for the overall val mAP, possibly visible for night births and small moving objects. Priors: real-event fusion vs strong RGB YOLO +1.7 mAP50-95 (DSEC-Det), low-light +0.023 mAP50 (PKU-DAVIS-SOD); flow neck for tiny objects +3.2 AP (XS-VID); drone motion maps +0.25 AP (narrow domain, not transferable). Pseudo-motion from the same 30 Hz RGB with forward ego-motion should be below real events. For us: night mAP50 0 to +0.01 over 0.3772 +- 0.0031 [uncertain]; the more likely measurable effect is a few percentage points on age-0 night recall and on the 21.0% night threshold loss, which would translate into a small FN reduction (of 15,763) and a DetA change of +0.2 to +0.5 at most [uncertain]. Since the fly's retina-lamina already showed a fly-specific night benefit that DIS did not get, there is a plausible but unproven route for the fly to beat DIS on night births; whether it beats plain frame difference is open.

## Риски
1) Raw previous-frame input can hurt badly (YOLOMG 'RGB+RGB' AP 0.33 vs 0.53 single-frame): never compare fly maps against 'no motion' only; compare against raw frames, frame difference, DIS and v2e at equal channels. 2) Static objects lose: motion-guided detectors miss hovering/slow targets (YOLOMG failure case); parked cars and standing pedestrians dominate BDD, so a non-residual motion channel could reduce their scores - zero-init residual injection and per-class/per-motion breakdowns are required. 3) Ego-motion and parallax flood T4/T5 and frame differences; homography compensation (YOLOMG) does not hold for road scenes. 4) More FP: CenterTrack's prior heatmap raised FP (MOT17 3.5% -> 4.5%), and the tracker already has FP 8,242. 5) DRB lessons: seed spread 0.0144 at 12 epochs; sign flip at 100 epochs; a branch without gradient - monitor first-conv weight norms of new channels; +17% time. XS-VID supports staged training (static detector first, then motion) to halve variance. 6) Temporal leakage/mismatch: channels need frames before each labelled frame; consistent alignment between 5 Hz labels and 30 Hz video is required; first frames of a clip have no history. 7) Licences: Ultralytics AGPL-3.0; YOLOMG code/data CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (non-commercial, share-alike); CenterTrack MIT; flyvis MIT.
