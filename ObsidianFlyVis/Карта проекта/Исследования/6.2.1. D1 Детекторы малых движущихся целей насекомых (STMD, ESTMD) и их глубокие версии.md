# 6.2.1. D1 Детекторы малых движущихся целей насекомых (STMD, ESTMD) и их глубокие версии

> Модели нейронов малых движущихся целей (стрекоза, журчалка), их глубокие/обучаемые версии 2022–2026, применение к ИК малым целям и к видео с движущейся камерой; работает ли при собственном движении камеры; что дают мелким объектам и почему у мухи есть аналоги (LC11, вход T4/T5).

## Суть
Insects (dragonflies, hoverflies) have small target motion detector neurons (STMD) in the lobula that fire for small (about 1-3 degrees) dark objects moving against clutter and ignore large objects and wide-field motion. Computational models copy the chain photoreceptor adaptation -> lamina temporal high-pass (LMC) -> rectifying transient cell (RTC, OFF-then-ON correlation, i.e. ESTMD) -> lateral/surround inhibition -> spatial facilitation along a trajectory, with variants that add direction selectivity (DSTMD), feedback (Feedback-STMD, ST-feedback), attention/prediction (apg-STMD), velocity-agnostic cross-inhibition (vSTMD, 2025) and ego-motion inhibition from wide-field (LPTC/EMD) motion. In Drosophila the closest analogue is LC11 (lobula columnar), fed mainly by T2/T3 (not T4/T5); T3 is an ON-OFF, tightly size-tuned small-object cell. 'Deep versions' 2022-2026 are almost always hybrids: a hand-tuned insect/retina motion front-end whose map is concatenated with the RGB/IR frame and fed to a trained CNN/YOLO/DETR (BIV + YOLO for drones AP 0.83 -> 0.92; magnocellular SNN + YOLOv5-s AP 81.9 -> 84.3; retina-motivated motion pathway in MI-DETR on moving IR small targets mAP50 88.9 -> 98.0 on DAUB-R). Gains are large only for tiny, low-contrast targets on mostly static or slowly rotating backgrounds (sky, IR surveillance); pure STMD models remain weak in absolute terms (vSTMD-F mF1 39.3% on RIST) and are never compared against a modern detector on a driving dataset.

## Место мухи
Correction to the framing first: in Drosophila the small-object pathway LC11 is driven mainly by T2 and T3 (ON-OFF, fast-adapting, tightly size-tuned columnar cells), with GABAergic surround inhibition; T4/T5 are thought not to feed LC11 (Keles 2020, Tanaka & Clark 2020). T4/T5 belong to the wide-field / direction pathway that STMD models use for ego-motion inhibition (the LPTC/EMD branch in Melville-Smith 2022 and the LPTC population coding in recent Yue/Wang-group models). FlyVis contains T2, T2a, T3 and the Tm/TmY types, but no LC/LPLC neurons (checked in the project runtime: output_cell_types of fib25-fib19_v2.2), so an LC11 stage has to be added on top. Proposed roles, from cheapest to most expensive: (a) Candidate rescoring head (the main role): for each low-confidence candidate of the frozen YOLO26s (conf 0.01 cache), run a foveal eye (721 columns, retina-lamina front-end on) on a crop around the candidate over the 6-10 preceding 30 Hz frames and read an 'LC11 proxy' = spatial pooling of rectified, baseline-subtracted T2/T2a/T3 (optionally Tm5 types) in the box centre divided by the same quantity in a surround ring (size selectivity), plus a T4/T5 wide-field inhibition term = local T4/T5 energy minus the ring energy (residual relative to ego-motion). This is a learned-by-evolution, connectome-constrained STMD evaluated exactly where the detector loses objects: candidates that exist but are scored below threshold. (b) Learnable LC11 layer ('deep version'): a small trainable pooling layer over FlyVis T2/T3/Tm features with a learned divisive surround and short temporal facilitation (dragonfly CSTMD1-like predictive gain along the track), trained on BDD candidates while FlyVis stays frozen or is fine-tuned (the user allows modifying the fly). (c) Detector input channel: a full-frame STMD map (LC11 proxy at P3 stride 8) appended as an extra input channel or a zero-initialised P3 side branch of YOLO26s, as in BIV+YOLO and MG-SNN+YOLO. (d) Proposal source for blind misses: peaks of the STMD map that have no YOLO candidate within IoU 0.3 become extra proposals for the tracker's delayed-birth buffer. The retina-lamina front-end is required in all roles: the bio-inspired IR work (Uzair 2021) shows that the photoreceptor/LMC adaptation alone delivers most of the gain (AUROC +75.4%), and in this project it is what removes the fly's night failure.

## Какую потерю закрывает
Primary: THRESHOLD losses (21.0% night vs 15.5% day: candidate exists but its confidence is too low; night under-scoring +0.098) - STMD/LC11 evidence is an appearance-independent, temporal signal that can raise the score of real but dim moving candidates, which is exactly the role STMD preprocessing plays in IR small-target work. Secondary: SMALL objects (56.7% found) - STMD is by construction tuned to targets of a few columns and suppresses large objects; but only for objects with image motion relative to the background. Partial: BIRTH (age 0: 31% night vs 68% at age 10+, two thirds through the threshold) - objects crossing into view or emerging from behind an occluder generate transient ON/OFF edges that T2/T3 and ESTMD react to; however distant objects near the focus of expansion and vehicles moving with the ego car have almost zero image motion and give no STMD response. Weak/none: BLINDNESS (4.5%, equal night and day) - a proposal-source role (d) is possible but STMD models have extremely low precision in clutter (RFSTMD precision 0.065 at FA=3), so blind-miss recovery would flood the tracker with false births. Night occlusion (penalty 5.4x): STMD inhibition by larger objects makes partially occluded small parts weakly responsive at best; no evidence. Overexposure/glare: moving headlights are small bright moving targets; LC11/ESTMD prefer dark targets (OFF-first) and bright lamps will be strong false positives at night unless polarity is handled - a risk rather than a gain.

## Пересечение с чужими работами
Bio-inspired small-target motion front-ends feeding trained detectors already exist: insect BIV (photoreceptor/LMC/ESTMD) frames concatenated with still frames for YOLO drone detection (Luesutthiviboon et al. 2023), insect photoreceptor preprocessing for IR moving-target detectors (Uzair et al. TIP 2021), magnocellular/retina models as a motion-guidance channel of YOLO (Magno-VTOD 2024; MG-SNN + YOLOv5/v6 2025), and a retina-motivated recurrent motion pathway in a DETR (MI-DETR 2026). STMD-to-detector fusion, 'bio-inspired motion channel for small objects', and 'insect-inspired detection from a moving platform' (Melville-Smith 2022) are therefore not new. Cowley et al. (Nature 2024) used an LC-type bottleneck inside a DNN, but to model behaviour, not detection. No work was found that (i) uses a connectome-constrained whole-optic-lobe model (FlyVis) as the STMD stage, (ii) reads T2/T3 as an LC11 proxy, or (iii) applies STMD evidence to rescoring low-confidence candidates of a modern detector on driving video with forward ego-motion [uncertain: absence of evidence from searches on 02.10.2026, not proof]. cannot_claim: 'first bio-inspired / insect STMD channel in a detector'; 'STMD works under ego-motion in driving' (the literature shows it degrades when background motion exceeds target motion); 'LC11 is fed by T4/T5'; any gain from the fly beyond what the retina-lamina front-end or a plain ESTMD on the same crops gives, unless the control in cheap_gate is passed.

## Результаты у авторов
Pure STMD models (no learning, compared only with other STMD models): vSTMD (2025) on RIST (real natural scenes, handheld/moving camera, ego-motion backgrounds): mF1 32.44%, vSTMD-F 39.33% vs FracSTMD 24.88% and STMD+ 22.31%; >560 FPS; orientation error 0.50 rad vs deep optical-flow DpFlow 0.74 rad. RFSTMD (2026) on RIST at FA=3: detection rate 0.81 vs <=0.59 for ESTMD/DSTMD/FSTMD/Feedback-STMD/Frac-STMD/ST-STMD, but precision only 0.0648 and F1 0.12 (baselines F1 <=0.015) - absolute precision is very low. ml-SOD (2026, T4/T5 + LC11-motivated motion x luminance): keeps F-measure >0.5 across simulated background velocities where ESTMD fails beyond -400 / +1100 px/s; better than STMD baselines on RIST and 22 IR aircraft sequences (Pd at Fa 1e-4), >160 fps; no deep baseline. ST-STMD (TIP 2024): best detection rate vs earlier STMDs on synthetic and RIST [uncertain: exact numbers not extracted]. Moving platform: Melville-Smith et al. 2022 (20 HDR panoramas, 1000x200 px, 500 inserted 1.2-px targets, simulated rotational ego-motion 10-50 px/s): nonlinear EMD/MLI motion inhibition gives 2.33x AUROC over the 2008 BIV model, +25% over linear inhibition, 104x over LCM and 10x over FKRW; >250 true positives before the first false alarm vs 100-130 without inhibition; underperforms spatial-only baselines when background motion exceeds target speed. Hybrids with trained detectors: Uzair et al. TIP 2021, IR long-range video: insect photoreceptor temporal preprocessing alone improved AUROC of the best moving-object detector by 75.4% (classical detectors, not deep). Luesutthiviboon et al. 2023, IR drone videos, YOLO [uncertain: version]: AP 0.83 (still frames) -> 0.92 (BIV-processed frames) / 0.88 (BIV + still concatenated) at long range; fixed sensor [uncertain]. MG-SNN + YOLO (2025, Anti-UAV derived VMD, 120x100 px, 650 videos): YOLOv5-s AP 81.9 -> 84.3, YOLOv5-x 82.8 -> 86.1, YOLOv6-l 82.4 -> 85.0; negligible GFLOPs (203.8 -> 204.5); no frame-difference control. MI-DETR (2026, moving IR small targets, RT-DETR base): mAP50 98.0 on DAUB-R vs RT-DETR appearance-only 88.9 (ablation 90.2 -> 98.0 with the motion pathway), ITSDT-15K 88.3 vs 80.67 and IRDST-H 70.3 vs 43.95 for the previous best iMoPKL. Baseline strength: STMD papers compare only with STMD models; hybrid papers use YOLOv5/v6/RT-DETR on tiny-target IR/drone sets; none uses a 2025-2026 detector of YOLO26 class, none uses forward-driving video; frame rates are native video rates, camera mostly static or slowly panning.

## Проверка против сильной базы
Weak. (1) No STMD or STMD-hybrid paper evaluates against a modern RGB detector on a driving benchmark; the hybrid gains (+2.4 to +9 AP) come from tiny-target IR/drone datasets with near-static backgrounds where motion is almost the only cue. (2) The 'same information without the bio-model' control is mostly missing: MG-SNN and BIV+YOLO do not compare with a plain frame-difference or flow channel of equal size; MI-DETR's motion pathway is learned, not biological, and its own ablation shows that explicit motion is what matters, not biology. Melville-Smith 2022 shows that adding the same inhibition to a classical LCM detector also gives a 22x median gain - i.e. the mechanism transfers to non-bio pipelines. (3) Equal training budget is not reported in the hybrids. (4) Independent reproduction: STMD models are reproduced inside the same groups (Yue/Wang/Peng lineage; Adelaide Wiederman/O'Carroll/Brinkworth lineage) on RIST and synthetic Vision Egg data; no independent reproduction of hybrid detector gains found [uncertain]. (5) Project evidence points the same way: post-hoc score correction with class/geometry/scene state did not raise AUC (0.9025 -> 0.9005), and tiled FlyVis readouts including T2/T3 (all34) gave nothing for birth safety (q_birth ΔAP -0.0004, CI [-0.0014, +0.0007]); a positive STMD result would have to beat both a plain ESTMD and a frame-difference/DIS control on the same crops.

## Разрез по условиям
No BDD100K or driving evaluation exists for STMD models or STMD-hybrid detectors. Available breakdowns: target velocity vs background velocity (vSTMD, ml-SOD, Melville-Smith: degradation when background motion exceeds target motion), target size (1-15 px in RIST/synthetic), target luminance/contrast (apg-STMD for low-contrast; Neural Networks 2026 direction-decoding STMD across luminance), sky/urban/forest backgrounds (MG-SNN), IR vs visible. No breakdown for night, overexposure, occlusion or first appearance of the object. Low light is addressed only indirectly (low-contrast targets; Applied Sciences 2025 low-light STMD on synthetic sequences [uncertain]).

## Дешёвая проверка
Gate written before any detector training, all on existing artefacts (frozen weights/baseline_seed42/best.pt, conf 0.01 caches in runs/det_cache_ctl_960 and runs/train_cache/<video>/detections/raw_video.npz, val7 / test videos). G0 (30-60 min, synthetic, decides whether the premise holds): drive FlyVis (with and without the retina-lamina front-end) with the classic STMD stimulus set - small dark square of 1-5 columns, long vertical bar, wide-field grating, each moving on a static and on a translating/expanding textured background - and measure size tuning of T2, T2a, T3 and of the LC11 proxy (centre/surround divisive pooling). Pre-recorded pass: LC11-proxy response to a 1-2 column target >= 2x the response to a bar of the same contrast and >= 2x the response to wide-field motion; if FlyVis T3 is not size-tuned (it was trained only on optic flow), the 'STMD inside the fly' premise is dropped and only role (b) with an explicitly trained layer remains. G1 (2-4 h GPU, the decisive test; differs from the failed tests in target and scale): unit = YOLO candidate in the threshold band (conf from 0.01 to the operating threshold) on val7 and dev/test videos; label = matched to GT (IoU >= 0.5) or not; strata: night/day, small (GT area < 32^2 at 960) / larger, age 0 / age >= 10, global ego-flow magnitude (DIS median) tertiles. Features: base = conf, class, geometry, scene state (the 0.9025 head); + fly LC11 proxy and T4/T5 residual from a foveal eye centred on the candidate, run over the 8 preceding 30 Hz frames with the lamina front-end (new run: existing fovea caches exist only for tracked tracks, and the tiled cache has 13.3 px column step, too coarse for small boxes - this is why the earlier tiled and track-fovea readouts are not a test of this hypothesis). Controls on the same crops and frames: (c1) plain ESTMD/vSTMD-style operator (numpy, no connectome); (c2) frame-difference energy centre/surround and DIS residual magnitude; (c3) shuffled-connectome fly (fly_connectome_null_v1 weights); (c4) lamina-only output without the fly. Model: logistic regression and HGB, fit on train videos, paired bootstrap over held-out videos. Pre-recorded pass criteria: ΔAUC(base + fly) - AUC(base) >= +0.005 with 95% CI > 0; fly > c1, c2 and c3 each with CI > 0; night ΔAUC >= day ΔAUC; at a fixed number of false positives, recovered threshold-lost GT >= +5% relative (night) and recovered small-object recall >= +3 points; effect not concentrated in the lowest ego-flow tertile only (otherwise the result is 'stationary-camera only' and does not transfer to driving). Fail on any of the first two criteria closes the STMD rescoring route. G2 (only if G1 passes, about 1 h): tracker replay - feed rescored candidates to the final tracker (cascade buffers + delayed birth + stitching) on val7 and record DetA, FN and FP vs 58.21 / 15 763 / 8 242.

## Стоимость у нас
G0: <1 h (synthetic stimuli, FlyVis forward only). G1: foveal eye on band candidates - with an assumed subsample of about 20-50k candidates x 8 frames and batched eyes on the RTX 5070, about 0.5-2 h GPU plus 1 h CPU for features and bootstrap [uncertain: depends on candidate density in the band]; ESTMD and frame-difference controls are cheap (numpy). Rescoring head in production: one foveal eye per band candidate per frame, i.e. inference cost grows with the candidate count; the cap is about 50-100 eyes per frame (comparable to the existing per-track eyes). Full detector integration (role c): a full-frame STMD map at P3 stride 8 at 960 (~120x68, about 8k columns, roughly 2x the current tiled 4326 columns, ~40 ms/frame by scaling the measured ~19 ms/frame tiled) streamed through the 30 Hz video: about 2.7 h for the 202 videos in runs/train_cache, about 19 h for all 1400 training videos (~1.68 M frames) plus storage of a single-channel map; then YOLO26s fine-tune from best.pt with a zero-initialised extra input channel/P3 branch, 12 epochs about 4.3 h per seed, at least 3 seeds for the variant and 3 for the equal-information frame-difference control = about 26 h; a 100-epoch confirmation about 35 h per run. Total for the detector route about 2-3 days of GPU, justified only after G1 passes.

## Ожидаемый эффект
Modest at best. Arguments for a small effect: STMD evidence is a temporal, appearance-independent cue aimed at small moving low-contrast objects, the same population where the detector loses candidates at the threshold; hybrids report +2.4 to +9 AP on tiny-target sets. Arguments against: (1) in forward-driving video most objects (vehicles ahead in the same direction, distant objects near the focus of expansion, parked cars) have small or purely ego-induced image motion, and the literature shows STMD degrades when background motion exceeds target motion; (2) at night the strongest small moving targets are lamps and reflections, which will become false positives; (3) project priors - post-hoc rescoring did not move AUC (0.9025 -> 0.9005), T2/T3 were already inside the all34 readout that failed for q_birth, and the fly readouts that did carry information (fovea T4T5 vs shuffled +0.0021 AP) did not beat geometry + DIS. Estimate for G1: ΔAUC in [0, +0.005], i.e. probably below the pre-recorded threshold [uncertain]; if it passes, the plausible tracker effect is a few hundred FN fewer out of 15 763 (night small/threshold strata) with DetA +0.1-0.3, which is within the regime where the cascade tracker already moved HOTA but not DetA. For the detector route (role c), expected night mAP50 change of order +0.000 to +0.005, comparable to the night control spread (±0.0031) and well below the 12-epoch seed spread of 0.0144 mAP [uncertain].

## Риски
DRB lessons apply directly: seed noise 0.0144 mAP at 12 epochs dwarfs the expected effect, so any detector-level claim needs >= 3 seeds per arm and an equal-information control (zero-initialised frame-difference/DIS channel of the same shape), and the sign must be confirmed at 100 epochs (DRB flipped sign). A zero-initialised extra channel can stay unused (DRB had a branch that received no gradient) - log the gradient norm and the weight norm of the new first-conv slice per epoch. Time overhead: a full-frame fly map adds roughly the cost of a second tiled pass per frame; DRB's +17% time was already an argument against it. Specific risks: (a) FlyVis T2/T3 may not be size-tuned because the model was trained only for optic flow - G0 exists to catch this; (b) ego-motion and parallax produce STMD responses on clutter (poles, signs, lane markings), and night lamps are high-contrast small movers; (c) polarity: LC11/ESTMD prefer dark targets, while night targets are often bright; (d) incomparable comparisons in the literature (STMD vs STMD only; hybrids on IR/drone data, YOLOv5-class bases, no frame-difference control) - do not transfer their percentages; (e) the per-candidate fovea scales with candidate count; (f) licences: vSTMD repository has no licence file (treat as all rights reserved, re-implement from the paper), ml-SOD code is GPL-3.0 and MI-DETR AGPL-3.0 (do not copy into project code; re-implement), FlyVis is MIT; (g) do not present a negative G1 as novelty.
