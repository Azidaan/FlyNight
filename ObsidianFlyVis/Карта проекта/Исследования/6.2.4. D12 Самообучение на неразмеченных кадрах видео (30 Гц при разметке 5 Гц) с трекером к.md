# 6.2.4. D12 Самообучение на неразмеченных кадрах видео (30 Гц при разметке 5 Гц) с трекером как разметчиком

> Псевдоразметка промежуточных кадров по согласованности трека (temporal pseudo-labels, video self-training, consistent teacher), интерполяция разметки по трекам; приросты детекции и трекинга; у нас — 5× больше кадров и готовый трекер линии B как разметчик; может ли муха отбирать надёжные псевдометки (надёжность, ночь).

## Суть
Use the 25 unlabelled frames per second between the 5 Hz labelled frames as extra training data, labelled either by interpolating ground-truth boxes along their IDs (no detector involved), or by the project's line-B tracker (cascade buffers + deferred birth + stitching), whose temporal persistence recovers objects the detector scores below threshold - so-called hard positives. The literature shows that tracker-derived hard examples and motion-consistent pseudo-labels help mostly when labels are sparse or the domain shifts (SSVOD, PseudoProp, RoyChowdhury et al. on BDD100K), and that with full labels plus extra unlabelled images a strong YOLO gains about +1.45 mAP (Efficient Teacher). It is also documented that naive teacher-student self-training on BDD100K night images can fall below the no-adaptation baseline because noisy pseudo-labels propagate (2PCNet). Our setting is dense labels (5 Hz) and highly correlated extra frames, so the value lies less in more data and more in targeted hard positives at night, at birth and under occlusion, provided their precision is verified; the fly can be tested as a reliability judge for which pseudo-labels to keep.

## Место мухи
Teacher-side reliability judge, not part of the student detector. For each pseudo-label candidate (a track box on an unlabelled frame, especially a tracker-rescued box whose detector score is below threshold, and boxes propagated backwards to the first appearance of a track), place a fovea (721 facets) on it, run retina-lamina -> FlyVis over the preceding 5-10 frames and take the type-wise readout (34 types x 19 macrocolumns, as in build_fly_signatures.py). A small head predicts whether the pseudo-label is correct (IoU >= 0.5 with GT, evaluated on held-out labelled frames). What from the fly: (1) T4/T5 direction-selective channels - a real object moving coherently against the ego-flow gives consistent local motion, while a ghost track drifting on background (or a box interpolated through an occluder) does not; (2) retina-lamina - the part that removes the fly's own night deficit (+0.009 [+0.005; +0.014] on dark videos), relevant because pseudo-label reliability is lowest at night; (3) identity continuity - the fly signature carries identity beyond geometry (+0.14 AP re-ID), so a sudden change of signature along a track flags a swap that would produce a wrong pseudo-label. Secondary role: weight of each pseudo-label in the loss (soft label = calibrated reliability), as in the soft-label/distillation variants that worked best for RoyChowdhury et al. The detector trained on these labels stays a plain YOLO26s, so inference cost is unchanged - this is the cheapest way for the fly to influence detection.

## Какую потерю закрывает
THRESHOLD primarily: tracker-rescued boxes are exactly real candidates whose score is under-estimated (21.0% night vs 15.5% day threshold losses, +0.098 night score deficit); training on them as positives directly pushes their scores up, unlike post-hoc rescoring which failed (AUC 0.9025 -> 0.9005). BIRTH: backward propagation of a confirmed track to its first frames provides labels for age-0..age-5 objects, the stratum with 31% recall at night vs 68% at age 10+, 2/3 of the gap through threshold. OCCLUSION at night: GT interpolation and track persistence keep labels for partially occluded objects between labelled frames; but interpolated boxes through a full occlusion would be wrong for modal BDD labels and must be filtered. SMALL: limited - small objects are the ones trackers lose first (SSVOD reports its smallest gain on small objects, +2.1). Not BLINDNESS: if no candidate ever exists the tracker cannot rescue it (except via GT interpolation).

## Пересечение с чужими работами
Tracker-based pseudo-labels and hard-positive mining from video are well established: Misra et al. 2015, Jin et al. 2018, RoyChowdhury et al. 2019 (on BDD100K itself, including night sub-domains), PseudoProp 2022 (bidirectional propagation in driving video), SSVOD 2024 (flow-warped cross-IoU selection), plus teacher-student SSOD (Consistent-Teacher, Efficient Teacher for YOLO). Linear interpolation of keyframe annotations is a standard annotation-tool practice. Learned pseudo-label quality estimators also exist in SSOD. No work was found that uses a connectome-constrained visual model to judge pseudo-label reliability [uncertain; absence of evidence is not a novelty argument]. cannot_claim: tracker-as-labeller, bidirectional propagation, GT interpolation, soft-label distillation, temporal-consistency filtering, self-training on BDD100K. Potentially claimable only if measured: fly readout improves pseudo-label reliability prediction over the same raw input and over track statistics, at night.

## Проверка против сильной базы
Weak for our regime. Most gains are reported against sparse-label or domain-shift baselines (1 labelled frame out of 15; clear-day source -> night target), where any extra signal helps; our detector is already trained on all 5 Hz labels of the same domain. The only full-label analogue on a YOLO is Efficient Teacher (+1.45 mAP), and its unlabelled set consists of distinct images, not near-duplicate neighbouring frames. Detectors are Faster R-CNN/SELSA/YOLOv5 - not NMS-free YOLO26. Seeds: RoyChowdhury (5 rounds) and SSVOD (3 runs) report spread - a strength. Iteration-matched controls (same number of gradient steps without the extra frames) are generally absent, so part of the gain may be longer training. Independent reproduction: SSVOD re-implements prior baselines; the tracker-hard-positive effect is reproduced across 2015-2024 papers qualitatively. The 2PCNet failure analysis on BDD100K night is an independent warning that night pseudo-labels are risky.

## Разрез по условиям
RoyChowdhury et al. give BDD100K sub-domain results (rainy, foggy, night, dusk; night degrades all models, HP-cons best) - figure only, numbers [uncertain]. SSVOD: object size and motion speed (small +2.1, fast +2.3, large +9.2, slow +10.1). 2PCNet: BDD100K night per class. No paper breaks down by occlusion or by first appearance of an object; none uses the 30 Hz between 5 Hz labels setting.

## Дешёвая проверка
All gates on validation videos with existing caches, no detector training. Gate 1 - GT interpolation quality (CPU, < 1 h): for every GT ID present at labelled frames k-1 and k+1 (0.4 s apart), linearly interpolate the box to frame k and compare with the real GT at k; report IoU distribution by night/day, size, occlusion flag and ego-speed. This is a pessimistic bound (actual unlabelled frames are at most 0.1 s from a label, inside a 0.2 s gap). Pre-registered: GT interpolation is accepted as a clean label source if >= 90% of interpolated boxes reach IoU >= 0.7 at night and >= 95% reach IoU >= 0.5; objects appearing or disappearing between keyframes are excluded. Gate 2 - tracker pseudo-label precision (GPU/CPU, 1-2 h): run the line-B tracker on 30 Hz val video from det_cache_ctl_960-style detections, treat the labelled frames as held-out, and measure precision/recall of (a) all published track boxes, (b) the rescued subset (box present in a confirmed track, detector score < operating threshold), (c) backward-propagated birth boxes (first 5 frames before a track's first confident detection). Pre-registered: rescued and birth subsets are usable only if their night precision >= 0.80 at IoU 0.5 and they cover >= 20% of night threshold-lost GT; otherwise only GT interpolation is used. Gate 3 - fly as reliability judge (3-5 h, fly compute): on the rescued/birth candidates from Gate 2, fit nested correctness classifiers with video-level CV: J0 = detector score + track statistics (age, hits, IoU-to-prediction, buffer state), J1 = J0 + raw 721-facet retina-lamina input, J2 = J0 + fly readout, J3 = J0 + shuffled-connectome fly. Pre-registered: J2 - max(J0, J1) >= +0.01 AUC with video-bootstrap 95% CI > 0 on night videos; else the fly is dropped and J0 (or J1) is used as the filter.

## Стоимость у нас
Frame extraction: the 30 Hz frames come from the BDD100K videos (runs/bdd100k_videos_zip_index.pkl exists); about 282k unlabelled frames for 1400 train videos, decoding/storage of a few hundred GB as JPEG at 720p [uncertain] - better to sample 1 extra frame per 0.2 s gap (doubling the set to ~113k) or only frames around rescued/birth events. Tracker labelling: the line-B tracker on cached detections is CPU-cheap, but detection caches for train videos at 30 Hz must first be produced (~282k frames at ~10-15 ms each ~1-1.5 h GPU [uncertain]). Fly judge: only on candidate boxes, several hours. Training: with a doubled set, an epoch takes ~2x; the fair comparison is iteration-matched: arm A = labelled only for N iterations, arm B = labelled + pseudo for the same N iterations (12-epoch-equivalent ~4.3 h each), 3 seeds per arm ~26 h; fine-tune from best.pt for gates (~1-2 h per arm). Ultralytics has no ignore-region or soft-label support out of the box: soft labels and 'ignore unlabelled objects' need a custom loss/dataset (engineering cost). Inference cost of the final detector: unchanged.

## Ожидаемый эффект
Moderate at best, concentrated on threshold losses. Overall val mAP50: +0.003 to +0.010; night mAP50: +0.005 to +0.015 if rescued/birth pseudo-labels pass Gate 2 (about +1.3 to +4% of 0.3772). Reasoning: full-label + unlabelled YOLO gain is +1.45 mAP with distinct images (Efficient Teacher); our extra frames are near-duplicates, so the effective new information is far less than 5x; the specific hard-positive mechanism gave +0.95 AP over high-confidence pseudo-labels and +1.5 more with soft labels in RoyChowdhury et al., but under strong domain shift. On recall: if training lifts even one third of night threshold-lost rescued objects above threshold, night threshold losses would fall from 21.0% towards ~19% (~ -2 pp), birth recall at age 0 by a few pp, DetA +0.3 to +0.6 and FN -2 to -4% (FN 15 763 -> ~15 200) [uncertain]. Fly judge: +0.005 to +0.01 AUC in reliability on night candidates, which would change the pseudo-label set by a few percent - second-order on detector metrics.

## Риски
Confirmation bias: the tracker's own errors (ghost tracks, ID swaps across occluders) become training labels; 2PCNet shows teacher-student methods dropping below source-only on BDD100K night (UMT 36.2, AT 38.5 vs 41.1). Missing objects in pseudo-labelled frames act as negatives and teach the detector to suppress them - use frames where all objects are covered, or implement ignore regions. Frame correlation: 30 Hz neighbours inflate the dataset without new information and can over-fit; iteration-matched controls are mandatory, otherwise longer training is mistaken for a pseudo-label effect. DRB lessons: seed noise 0.0144 at 12 epochs, sign flip at 100 epochs, so 3 seeds and a long-schedule check of the winner; a fly judge that ends up ignored is the analogue of a branch without gradient - report its share of decisions changed. Leakage: never use val/test videos for pseudo-labels of the training set and keep tracker hyper-parameters frozen from dev. Evaluation consistency: pseudo-labels must be modal like BDD GT.
