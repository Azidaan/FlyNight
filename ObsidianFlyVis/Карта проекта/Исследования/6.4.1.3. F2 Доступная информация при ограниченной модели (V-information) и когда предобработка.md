# 6.4.1.3. F2 Доступная информация при ограниченной модели (V-information) и когда предобработка всё же помогает

> Xu et al. ICLR 2020, Ethayarajh ICML 2022: предобработка повышает доступную информацию для ограниченного ученика; при каком бюджете и в каком режиме данных; линейная проба «база» против «база + муха», CKA.

## Механизм
Shannon information obeys the data processing inequality, so preprocessing cannot add information. Xu et al. (ICLR 2020) define predictive V-information I_V(X -> Y) = H_V(Y) - H_V(Y | X), where H_V is the best cross-entropy achievable by a predictor from a restricted family V (e.g. linear heads, a fixed-size MLP, a detector trained for 12 epochs on 1400 videos). Two properties matter here: (1) V-information can be created by computation - for a bounded V, I_V(f(X) -> Y) can exceed I_V(X -> Y) because f does work V cannot do (the paper states this explicitly as a violation of DPI, consistent with deep networks building progressively more usable features); (2) the gain from f shrinks as V grows (more capacity, more data, longer training), because the learner can then implement f itself. Hence preprocessing helps exactly when the student is bounded relative to the computation f performs: small capacity, small data, short schedule, or an input distribution it was not trained on. Ethayarajh, Choi & Swayamdipta (ICML 2022, outstanding paper) turn this into a measurement tool: V-usable information of a dataset and pointwise V-information (PVI) per instance, which can be compared across slices (night vs day) and across input transformations (keep only some attributes, shuffle, add a feature) to see which inputs carry usable information for a given model family. Hewitt et al. (EMNLP 2021) add conditioning: I_V(F -> Y | B) = H_V(Y | B) - H_V(Y | B, F), the usable information of a feature F beyond a baseline B, estimated by two probes. Kornblith et al. (ICML 2019) CKA measures representational similarity, not information about Y; Davari et al. (ICLR 2023) show linear CKA is dominated by outliers and can be manipulated without changing function, so CKA is a redundancy screen only. Analogous evidence outside information theory: fixed or pretrained priors help mainly at low data / short schedules - scattering front-ends beat end-to-end CNNs in the small-sample regime on CIFAR-10 subsets and STL-10 but not at full data (Oyallon et al., TPAMI 2018); ImageNet pretraining mostly speeds convergence and helps with little data, while from-scratch training on full COCO reaches the same AP with a long enough schedule (He, Girshick, Dollar, ICCV 2019); VOneNet's fixed V1 front-end improves robustness to perturbations (+18 % relative over base CNNs on a combined adversarial + corruption benchmark) at roughly unchanged clean accuracy - i.e. it helps under shift, not on in-distribution clean data (Dapello et al., NeurIPS 2020).

## Где видно у нас
The project contains a clean natural experiment on the bounded-observer effect: the retina-lamina before the fly removes the fly's night drop (+0.016), while DIS on the same retina output gains +0.001. The fly (734 free parameters, fixed, trained on Sintel) is a bounded observer for whom the retina creates usable information; DIS, a strong variational flow solver, already extracts it. The same pattern at the detector level: frozen YOLO26 on photoreceptor / accumulated frames loses 2-15 pp (tavg3 -2.0 pp at night, photoreceptor -9.5 pp) - for a model trained on raw frames, the processed input lowers V-information (distribution shift), even though Shannon information about objects in a multi-frame average is not lower. The fly as identity signature: +0.14 AP over geometry (the bounded geometry baseline gains usable information), but only +0.006 [+0.001; +0.012] over the fly's own 721-facet input and the fly alone is -0.052 vs its input: conditional V-information of the fly beyond its input is small but positive and doubles at night (+0.010) - exactly the quantity conditional probing measures. DRB: a branch inside a strong, well-trained detector converged to weight 0 in all 896 channels and the frequency mask to neutral 4/4: for V = YOLO neck, the branch carried no extra usable information. Seed spread 0.0144 mAP at 12 epochs and the sign flip at 100 epochs show the classic V-information regime change: a short schedule (bounded learner) and a long schedule (stronger learner) disagree. Rescoring after the detector (5 Hz, 30 Hz tubelets, background-relative motion): ~0 at equal FP: the detector's score already carries the V-usable information of box tracks.

## Решения из литературы
1) Measure, do not assume: conditional probe Delta_V = H_V(Y | base) - H_V(Y | base, module) with V matching the capacity that the real integration will have (a 1x1 conv / 2-layer MLP adapter), on held-out videos (Hewitt et al. 2021; in their NLP case, conditioning on non-contextual embeddings revealed that part-of-speech information is accessible at deeper layers than an unconditioned probe suggested - conditioning changes conclusions). 2) Use MDL (online code length) instead of accuracy for small effects; MDL is reported to be more stable across seeds and probe hyperparameters than accuracy (Voita & Titov 2020). 3) Use PVI per instance (Ethayarajh et al. 2022) to locate the slice where the module adds usable information (night, age 0, occluded) instead of a single global number; their framework explicitly compares slices and input transformations for a fixed model family. 4) Sweep the data budget and probe capacity: preprocessing helps a bounded student and vanishes for a strong one; in the scattering-hybrid line the fixed front-end wins on small CIFAR-10 subsets and STL-10 and ties at full data (Oyallon et al. 2018) [uncertain: exact per-subset numbers not verified]; He et al. 2019 show from-scratch Mask R-CNN on full COCO matching ImageNet-pretrained AP given a longer schedule, while with ~10 % of COCO pretraining still helps [uncertain: exact AP values]. 5) When the target regime is distribution shift (night, glare), a fixed bio front-end can help robustness while not helping clean accuracy (VOneNet +18 % relative on a perturbation benchmark, ImageNet classification only; never shown for a strong detector). 6) CKA (Kornblith 2019) only as a redundancy screen; trust it only with outlier-robust variants and debiased estimators (Davari 2023; debiased CKA estimators, arXiv 2405.01012).

## Что сделать с мухой
1) Treat the fly as a computation that a bounded host cannot do itself and pick the host accordingly: the clearest bounded host in the pipeline is not YOLO26 but the tracker's association/birth heads (q_match, q_birth) and small probes - there the fly already shows usable information (+0.006 over its input, +0.010 at night). 2) For the detector, give the fly a computation YOLO26 truly cannot perform: integration over past frames (YOLO26 is single-frame), with the fly's adaptation and motion channels. Inject it as an extra P3/P4 feature map through a zero-initialised adapter; train the adapter and the neck, keep the backbone frozen in the first stage. 3) Fine-tune the fly itself on BDD (K1) - a fixed Sintel-trained fly is a bounded computation tuned to the wrong statistics (motion error 52.77 px vs 4.87 px for DIS). 4) Keep the retina-lamina stage: it is the one place the project measured usable information created by computation (+0.016 for the fly at night). 5) Never feed a frozen detector with fly-processed frames instead of RGB (G3: -2...-15 pp): for that V the processed input has less usable information.

## Тест до постройки
Budget x capacity conditional probe on existing caches: 1) Candidates and features as in F1: base B = YOLO26 neck features + own score + box geometry at each candidate (night/day/age-0/occluded flags); module F = fly responses over the last k = 1, 3, 10 frames; controls C = DIS flow statistics, frame-difference statistics, mean of aligned frames, shuffled connectome, random features of the same dimension. 2) Grid: probe family {linear, MLP-2 (256), MLP-4 (512)} x training budget {1 %, 5 %, 25 %, 100 % of train videos}. 3) Report Delta_V(F | B) and Delta_V(F | B, C_best) in bits per candidate with video bootstrap, and PVI histograms per slice. 4) Reading: Delta > 0 only at small budget / small capacity => the fly is a prior for bounded learners; it will not help a full YOLO26 training (expect DRB-like collapse), but may help the tracker heads. Delta > 0 that persists at 100 % budget and MLP-4, and beyond C_best => genuine conditional usable information; only then build the detector branch. 5) Redundancy screen: linear CKA(F, B) and CKA(F, C) on the same candidates with outlier trimming; CKA(F, C) near CKA(C, C) means the fly is a re-implementation of the non-biological control. 6) The existing project probe (fly over input +0.006 [+0.001; +0.012], night +0.010) is the first point of this grid; repeat it with B = detector neck instead of raw input.

## Потолок
V-information is bounded above by Shannon information, so the ceiling is the oracle: Delta_V with F replaced by the true label of the next frame (temporal oracle) at the same probe capacity. For the detector, the closable night-specific gap at the threshold is 21.0 - 15.5 = 5.5 pp of night GT; blind objects (4.5 %) are only reachable by a branch inside the detector, not by rescoring. Measured so far: post-hoc candidates' temporal statistics reach ~0 % of that gap (G0b, G0b-30). For identity: the fly reaches +0.006 AP over its input, while geometry stitching gives +0.79 HOTA and appearance/fly beyond geometry are not significant - the identity ceiling in HOTA is small. Plan expectation for retina/dense-fly input channels: 0...+1 pp [uncertain: literature numbers come from weaker bases].

## Где обязано помогать
Night, glare/overexposure (distribution shift, where fixed front-ends are known to help robustness), age-0 objects and occluded objects; rare night classes (rider, bus, motorcycle, bicycle) for mAP. No-harm: day subset and the 70 held-out videos, change within the seed-spread CI; PVI on day instances should not decrease.

## Отрицательный контроль
Random features of the same dimension and the same probe (control task in the Hewitt & Liang sense, separates probe capacity from information); shuffled-connectome fly; time-shuffled frame order; matched non-biological temporal controls (DIS, frame differences, aligned frame mean); conditioning on these controls, not only on raw RGB. For trained models: zero-input branch with the same adapter and the same epochs; seeds injected into the dataloader (rule 6.12).

## Вклад ветви
Track Delta_V of the fused feature vs the base feature at checkpoints (probe re-fit every few epochs on a fixed dev split); adapter weight norm and gate value; ratio of gradient norms branch/main at the fusion point; per-epoch evaluation with the branch zeroed. A collapse of the gate to 0 (as in DRB, 896/896 channels) is a stop signal.

## Минимальный эффект и стоимость
Probe grid: 3 capacities x 4 budgets x ~6 feature sets = 72 probe fits on cached features, minutes each on CPU/GPU; feature extraction of fly responses on val at 1/16 density ~1.2 ms per step (15.8 ms full density) on RTX 5070 - about an hour for val. MDE at the probe level: the existing CI half-width ~0.0055 AP suggests MDE ~0.008 AP (alpha 0.05, power 0.8) with the current number of videos; in bits, take the bootstrap SD of Delta_V and require Delta > 2.8 SD. Detector level (only if the probe passes): sigma = 0.0144 mAP at 12 epochs, MDE 0.033 mAP with 3 seeds per arm (26 h at 4.3 h per run), 0.018 mAP with 10 seeds per arm (86 h).

## Вердикт для проекта
Main explanatory framework together with F1 (priority 1): it explains why the same fly signal is useful for the bounded geometry baseline and the fly itself (retina +0.016) but redundant for YOLO26 and DIS, and why short and long DRB schedules disagree. Practical consequence: run the budget x capacity conditional probe (hours, no training) before any 4.3-hour detector run; direct the fly to (a) bounded consumers (tracker heads) and (b) computations the detector cannot do (multi-frame integration), and fine-tune the fly on BDD. Novelty doubt, briefly: V-information and conditional probing are established; applying them to a connectome front-end is a method choice, not a novelty claim.
