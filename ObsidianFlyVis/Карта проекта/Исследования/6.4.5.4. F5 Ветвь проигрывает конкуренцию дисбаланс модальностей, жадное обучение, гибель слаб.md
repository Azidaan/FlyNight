# 6.4.5.4. F5 Ветвь проигрывает конкуренцию дисбаланс модальностей, жадное обучение, гибель слабой ветви

> OGM-GE (CVPR 2022), Wu et al. 2022, BalanceBenchmark 2025: почему сеть берёт лёгкий источник и бросает слабую ветвь (DRB: вес 0); модуляция градиента, вспомогательные потери на ветвь.

## Механизм
1) Greedy learner (Wu, Jastrzebski, Cho, Geras, ICML 2022): a jointly trained multi-branch network learns first and mostly from whichever input lets the loss fall fastest; once that branch explains the training labels, the shared gradient dL/df (the same softmax residual for both branches, Peng et al. CVPR 2022 eq. 5) becomes small, so the slower branch stops receiving signal and stays under-fitted. Measured as the conditional utilization rate u(m1|m0) = (acc(m0,m1) - acc(m0))/acc(m0,m1) and its cheap training-time proxy, conditional learning speed. 2) Modality competition theory (Huang et al., ICML 2022): with joint training, the network provably learns only a subset of modalities' features - the one that 'wins' early; the loser's features are never learned even though they are informative. 3) Regularization makes it worse: Wu et al. show |d_util| grows with L1 strength for lambda >= 1e-5 on ModelNet40 - strong regularization encourages greediness. A weak branch with a weak data gradient loses to weight decay and collapses to zero / neutral. 4) Not every 'dead branch' is imbalance: if the weak branch's information is a subset of the strong branch's (the fly is a deterministic function of its 721 facets; DIS and geometry already carry motion), its conditional utilization is ~0 for information reasons (data-processing inequality), and no balancing method can create it. BalanceBenchmark (2025) also finds a 'relative balance point' beyond which forcing more balance lowers accuracy, sometimes below baseline. So the diagnostic question is: does the weak branch ALONE (trained to convergence) contain signal the strong branch lacks?

## Где видно у нас
DRB: spatial-branch weight w_lqa = 0 in all 896 channels (plan says it was not connected and got no gradient - a wiring death, not proven competition); frequency mask converged to neutral in 4/4 settings under regularization and moved 0.002 in 12 epochs - matches Wu's 'regularization encourages greediness' (the backbone path is the easy modality); seed spread 0.0144 mAP at 12 epochs swamped all 'gains'; the sign flipped at 100 epochs (+0.027 -> -0.032 small-object night recall) - typical of a branch that is used early and then abandoned as the main path learns. Fly identity signature: fly over geometry +0.14 AP, but over its own facet input only +0.006 [+0.001; +0.012] (night +0.010), fly alone -0.052 vs input - when input and fly compete in one head the input (easy, linear) wins; fly alone being worse than its input says the fly is under-trained for this task (trained on Sintel flow) rather than that the head ignores it. Tracker: geometry-only stitching +0.79 HOTA, appearance and fly beyond geometry insignificant - geometry is the dominant modality; HOTA 58.47 -> 59.27 with DetA flat 58.21 -> 58.17 and FN 15,763 >> IDSw 2,221 - the weak branches act on the small IDSw term only. Motion source: fly flow error 52.77 px vs DIS 4.87 px - here the fly is not 'losing a competition', it is simply ~11x worse; any learned fusion will (correctly) assign it weight ~0 (ΔHOTA ~0).

## Решения из литературы
OGM-GE (gradient of the dominant branch scaled down by 1 - tanh(alpha*rho) where rho = ratio of branch confidence scores, plus Gaussian noise for generalization): CREMA-D concatenation 51.7 -> 61.9 acc (+10.2; audio-only alone 52.5 was better than every vanilla fusion), Kinetics-Sounds 59.8 -> 62.3 (+2.5), VGGSound 49.1 -> 50.6 (+1.5). Alternatives on the same backbone: Modality-Drop 53.3-54.4 / 60.3-61.3, Gradient-Blending 56.8 / 62.2 (CREMA-D / KS). Wu et al. 'guided' re-balancing (extra steps on the slow branch chosen by conditional learning speed): Colored-and-gray MNIST vanilla multi-modal 45.26 -> 91.01 (uni-modal best 99.14), ModelNet40 90.09 -> 91.37, NVGesture-pretrained 83.20 -> 83.82 - huge when one input is a shortcut, ~1 pp otherwise. BalanceBenchmark 2025 (17 methods, same toolkit): KS baseline 65.63 -> MMPareto 74.55; CREMA-D 65.50 -> 79.97; UCF-101 81.80 -> 86.91 (AMCo); FOOD-101 91.65 -> 93.14; CMU-MOSEI 78.99 -> 81.18; VGGSound 48.08 -> 51.58 (UMT). Gains shrink on large/realistic datasets and overly forced balance can fall below baseline; no method gives a good accuracy/compute trade-off; optimization-based methods are most expensive. Detection-specific: RGB-thermal detectors with an auxiliary per-modality head or base/auxiliary detector consistency report large reductions of missing rate under extreme imbalance (-55% MR) [uncertain on base and setting]. Common cure, applicable to any architecture: a per-branch auxiliary loss (uni-modal head on each branch; MMPareto resolves the conflict between the uni-modal and fused gradients), gradient modulation (OGM), or two-stage training (uni-modal pre-training then fusion, UMT).

## Что сделать с мухой
Give the fly its own job before it competes. (1) Train the fly branch ALONE first on a BDD target it is responsible for: for the identity signature (K2) - same-object/different-object contrastive loss on cached tracks, for motion (K1) - next-frame prediction / box transfer on BDD video; the 734 fly params + retina-lamina parameters train, the detector and tracker stay frozen. Success criterion before fusion: fly-alone must stop being worse than its own facet input (now -0.052). (2) Only then fuse with geometry/appearance, with a per-branch auxiliary loss kept on (weight 0.5-1) so the main loss cannot starve it, and OGM-style down-scaling of the geometry branch gradient when its confidence ratio rho > 1. (3) No weight decay on the fly-branch gate and fusion weights. (4) In the detector (K9 adapter on P3) add a fly-only objectness head at P3 as an auxiliary loss so the fly features are shaped for detection even if the main head ignores them. Frozen: detector and tracker; trainable: fly, adapter, fusion weights.

## Тест до постройки
(1) Conditional utilization from already-measured numbers (Wu et al. definition): u(fly|input) = +0.006 / (AP of input+fly) ~ 1% [AP of the combined model to be read from the card]; u(fly|geometry) large (+0.14 AP). If a fly-alone model trained to convergence is still below the input alone (-0.052), the low u(fly|input) is information redundancy, not imbalance - balancing methods will not help; improving the fly (K1/K2 training) will. (2) Learning-speed check on the existing ReID/stitching head training logs: per-branch probe accuracy vs epoch (train a frozen linear probe on each branch's features at every checkpoint); if the fly branch's probe accuracy stops rising once the input branch saturates, it is greedy-learning starvation and OGM/aux loss is the fix. (3) 'Duplicated modality' control from Wu et al.: train the fusion with geometry+geometry'; the distribution of d_util for geometry+fly vs this symmetric control tells whether the asymmetry is real. (4) For the DRB-type detector branch: a 1-epoch run logging ||grad|| per branch; zero grad = wiring, non-zero but shrinking relative to backbone grad = competition.

## Потолок
Balancing recovers the gap between the joint model and 'best uni-modal model + what the weak modality adds alone'. Here: in identity, fly-alone is below its input (-0.052), so the balancing ceiling over 'input + geometry' is about the measured +0.006 AP (night +0.010) - close to 0. In the tracker, appearance and fly beyond geometry are insignificant and IDSw (2,221) is only ~12% of FN+IDSw errors, and the whole stitching effect is +0.79 HOTA; even a perfect identity branch is capped by that IDSw slice. In literature the large gains (+10 pp CREMA-D, +46 pp Colored-MNIST) occur only when the weak modality is strong alone; with a weak-alone branch gains are ~1 pp (ModelNet40 +1.3, NVGesture +0.6). Estimate for the project: <= +0.1..+0.3 HOTA from balancing alone [uncertain]; the larger lever is making the fly good alone first (K1/K2).

## Где обязано помогать
Identity: long occlusion gaps and night re-identifications (where fly over input doubled: +0.010 night vs +0.006 all). Motion: night video where DIS degrades. Detector branch: night small objects. No-harm check: day and short-gap stitching must not lose (geometry-dominant cases), HOTA/IDF1 overall within CI of baseline.

## Отрицательный контроль
Balancing changes optimization, so the control is the same balancing applied to a non-fly second branch: (1) OGM/aux-loss with the raw facet input as the weak branch; (2) OGM with a shuffled-connectome fly; (3) OGM with duplicated geometry (symmetric control, Wu et al.); (4) vanilla fusion without balancing, same epochs. Gain is credited to the fly only if fly+balancing > facets+balancing and > shuffled+balancing, with video-bootstrap CI excluding 0.

## Вклад ветви
Per epoch: OGM discrepancy ratio rho (confidence of branch A / branch B), ratio of gradient norms ||g_fly||/||g_geom|| at the fusion layer, per-branch linear-probe accuracy, fusion weight norms, and drop-branch ablation (zero one branch at eval) at each checkpoint - plot conditional utilization vs epoch to catch death and DRB-type late sign flips.

## Минимальный эффект и стоимость
Identity/stitching heads train on cached tracks (minutes to 2-4 h GPU per run, K2 estimate), bootstrap over videos: the +0.006 [+0.001; +0.012] AP CI half-width ~0.0055 AP is the MDE for one paired comparison; to detect a +0.005 AP balancing effect, need >= 2-3 runs per arm and paired bootstrap. Tracker effect via HOTA: the stitching effect +0.79 HOTA was detectable; MDE ~0.2-0.3 HOTA [uncertain]. Detector branch: 12 epochs ~4.3 h per run, seed spread 0.0144 mAP -> need >= 3 runs/arm (~13 h per arm) for MDE ~0.03 mAP - too coarse for expected effects; do balancing experiments on the identity/motion heads, not on the detector.

## Вердикт для проекта
SECONDARY. Greedy learning/regularization plausibly contributed to DRB (4/4 neutral masks under regularization; late sign flip), but the dominant DRB fact (w=0 in 896 channels) was a dead connection, and for the fly the numbers point to redundancy and a weak (Sintel-trained) fly rather than starvation: fly-alone < its input, motion error 11x DIS. Priority: medium-low as a method; HIGH as a rule: always add a per-branch auxiliary loss, exclude branch gates from weight decay, and run the 'branch alone vs input alone' check before any fusion. Train the fly alone on BDD (K1/K2) first - that is the precondition for any balancing method to have something to balance.
