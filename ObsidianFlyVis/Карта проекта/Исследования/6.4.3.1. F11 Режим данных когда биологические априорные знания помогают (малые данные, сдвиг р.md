# 6.4.3.1. F11 Режим данных когда биологические априорные знания помогают (малые данные, сдвиг распределения) и когда нет

> «Горький урок», VOneNet и критика 2023–2025: биофронт-энды дают устойчивость, а не точность в домене; переформулировка задачи на устойчивость (учимся днём — проверяемся ночью, другие города, погода).

## Механизм
A prior (a fixed or constrained module) helps a learner only when it supplies information the data do not. Two regimes make that true: (a) small data, where the prior replaces samples the learner does not have; (b) test-time distribution shift, where the prior encodes an invariance the training distribution does not demonstrate. In a large, labelled, in-distribution regime the learner can estimate the useful transformation itself, so a fixed prior is at best redundant and usually a constraint that costs accuracy - Sutton's 'bitter lesson' (2019): general methods that scale with data and compute beat built-in human knowledge in the long run. (1) Bio front-ends buy robustness, not in-domain accuracy. VOneNet (Dapello et al., NeurIPS 2020): ResNet50 vs VOneResNet50 clean ImageNet 76.1 vs 74.9, white-box attacks 0.8 vs 29.2, common corruptions 41.0 vs 47.4, perturbation mean 20.9 vs 38.3; removing V1 stochasticity alone costs ~15 points of the perturbation mean, i.e. much of the robustness is noise injection, not V1 fidelity. EVNets (Piper, Oliveira, Marques, NeurIPS 2025): clean 75.4 / 72.9 / 70.4 (ResNet50 / VOne / EV), ImageNet-C mean 38.8 / 40.4 / 41.9, domain shift 33.4 / 37.1 / 38.1; with PRIME augmentation the margin shrinks (36.5 / 42.1 / 42.7). Non-biological control: a wavelet-scattering front-end with Gaussian noise beats VOneBlock robustness (Su & Kempe 2023). (2) 'Accuracy on the line': across 204 ImageNet models and 213 shifts, OOD accuracy is a near-linear function of ID accuracy; architecture and robustness interventions give almost no effective robustness above the line - only training on substantially more (diverse) data does (Taori et al., NeurIPS 2020; Miller et al., ICML 2021) [uncertain: exact model/shift counts]. Geirhos et al. (NeurIPS 2021) likewise find dataset size (1M to 1B) is the main driver of closing the human-machine OOD gap. (3) The same holds for connectome models. Kuklev (arXiv 2609.24565, 2026): a frozen FlyVis loses in-domain to task training (0.857 vs 0.959 one-shot hatch accuracy; 0.619 vs 0.905 wall IoU) but under scan noise and thick strokes the trained nets lose up to 0.188 while the frozen fly loses 0.030, and on 14 real production sheets the 1,876-parameter fly reaches 0.505 AP vs 0.415 for a 200x larger net; rewiring cost 0.271-0.356 accuracy. That is precisely the 'small data + shift' regime. (4) Day-to-night adaptation evidence: with no night labels, 2PCNet (CVPR 2023) lifts BDD100K night AP50 from 41.1 (source-only) to 46.4, but a night-labelled oracle is 46.2 - once labels exist, adaptation machinery adds ~0 (+0.2). ALDI (TMLR 2025) finds no DAOD method beats a fair oracle. Consequence: a fly prior is expected to show its value as a smaller drop under shift (train day / test night, other cities, weather) or as data efficiency, and not as a gain on an in-distribution test where the detector was trained on the same conditions.

## Где видно у нас
The project is in the regime where priors are least useful: YOLO26s trained on 1,400 BDD100K MOT videos including 564 labelled night videos (111,895 labelled night frames, 3.4-4x the unlabelled night pools of the DA papers), and every evaluation is in-distribution (same cities, same mix of day/night, same camera). Measured symptoms: (1) the fixed/pre-detector fly front-end hurts a frozen detector (G3: night recall at equal FP raw 0.7552, photoreceptor -9.45 pp, photoreceptor integration -15.13 pp, tavg3 -1.97 pp; blindness 0.1047 -> 0.2167) - the prior is out of the learner's distribution, not missing information; (2) DRB branches (frequency mask converged to neutral 4 of 4; one branch weight = 0 in all 896 channels) - the network chose to switch off the prior, as expected when data suffice; seed spread 0.0144 mAP at 12 epochs, sign flip at 100 epochs; (3) blindness is equal day and night (4.5% / 4.5%) and car AP50 is at parity (0.817 / 0.816) - for the dominant class there is no domain gap left for an invariance prior to close; the night deficit sits in rare classes (97% of the class-averaged gap in rider, bus, motorcycle, bicycle) - which is a small-data regime inside the big dataset; (4) the fly signature is useful where the project's learned components are data-starved: +0.14 AP over geometry for re-identification, +0.010 over its own input at night vs +0.006 by day (difference night - day not significant, +0.0041 [-0.0032; +0.0117]); retina-lamina removes the fly's own night dip (+0.016) but does nothing for DIS (+0.001) - the prior helps the fly (trained on Sintel, a shifted domain), not a learner already fitted to BDD; (5) the fly as motion source is far worse than a tuned classical method on this data (52.77 px vs 4.87 px DIS error) because it was trained on Sintel (distribution shift working against the fly).

## Решения из литературы
A. Reformulate the claim as robustness/data efficiency and evaluate under a controlled shift (the VOneNet/EVNet/Kuklev pattern): train on condition A, test on condition B, report the drop. Literature magnitudes: VOneNet +6.4 points on corruptions at -1.2 clean; EVNet +4.7 domain-shift at -5.0 clean; Kuklev frozen fly loses 0.030 vs 0.188 under degradation and wins 0.505 vs 0.415 AP on real data with a 200x smaller model. B. Report effective robustness, not raw OOD accuracy (Taori 2020): plot night (or other-city) metric against day metric for all available models; credit an intervention only for its vertical distance above the line fitted to baseline variants. C. Learning curves: train baseline and baseline+prior on 5%, 10%, 25%, 50%, 100% of the data; a useful prior shows a gain that shrinks with data (the crossover point is the regime boundary). Gokce & Schrimpf 2025: small models on few samples align poorly, neural alignment saturates with scale while behaviour keeps improving. D. Use the prior where data are scarce inside the big dataset: rare classes at night (val night has only 143 motorcycle, 737 rider, 867 bicycle boxes; day train 9,246 motorcycle vs 2,710 at night), first appearances, unusual illumination (glare). E. When labels exist, do not expect adaptation machinery to add much: 2PCNet +0.2 AP50 over a night-labelled oracle; DA steps of +0.6 AP are inside the field's +/-1.2 AP reproduction noise. F. Bitter-lesson compliant use of a prior: make it trainable and let data adjust it (K1: fine-tune the 734 fly parameters on BDD; YOLA-style learnable illumination-invariant front-ends report +1.7 to +2.7 against older bases [uncertain: weak bases]).

## Что сделать с мухой
(1) Change the evaluation, not only the model: define a shift protocol on BDD100K MOT using its scene/time/weather tags - train the fly-dependent component (signature head, fly-motion head, or fly-augmented detector head) on day-only videos and test on night; train on one city group and test on another; train clear and test rain/snow. The detector YOLO26s stays as is for the in-domain line; for the shift line, a day-only YOLO26s fine-tune (2 epochs from COCO-pretrained or from best.pt with night removed is not clean - prefer day-only from the COCO checkpoint, 12 epochs) is the baseline. (2) Place the fly where the learner is data-starved: the association/stitching heads trained on 130 videos (small data), rare-class night candidates, and birth events; keep the fly frozen as a prior (Kuklev regime) in one arm and fine-tuned (K1) in another, so the data regime is the only variable. (3) Use the retina-lamina front-end as the invariance prior for the fly only (where it is proven: +0.016 at night) and never as an input transform for a frozen detector (G3). (4) For the detector, the bio prior can enter only as training-time augmentation or a trainable front-end (K4/K5) and must be judged by effective robustness on a held-out condition, not by in-domain night mAP.

## Тест до постройки
(1) Accuracy-on-the-line from existing runs (CPU, under 1 h): collect day and night metrics for every detector variant already trained (3 baseline seeds at 640 and 960, DRB arms, ctl and iou_free probes, G3 frozen variants) from experiments/results/*.csv and runs/*/summary.json; fit night = a + b * day across baseline-like variants; compute each variant's residual with bootstrap CI. If all variants sit on the line, any future fly intervention must clear the residual spread to count. (2) Day-to-night probe transfer on cached crops (2-3 h, no training): for GT objects and threshold-band candidates (conf 0.01-0.25), extract raw gray fovea, receptor-only (721 facets), retina-lamina output, full fly readout (34 types), and the YOLO26 neck feature at the box; fit a logistic probe (TP vs FP, or same/different object for the signature) on day only and test on night; transfer drop = AUC(night | night-trained) - AUC(night | day-trained). Pre-registered: the fly is a useful invariance prior only if its transfer drop is <= 50% of the raw-gray and neck-feature drops and its within-night AUC is not lower by more than 0.005. (3) Data-efficiency curve for the signature head (1-2 h): train base+fly and base+input heads on 5, 10, 20, 40, 130 training videos; a prior is real if the fly gain is largest at 5-10 videos and shrinks with data; if it is flat at +0.006, the fly is a small fixed feature, not a prior.

## Потолок
In-domain (current evaluation): close to zero by the accuracy-on-the-line argument; the measured fly-over-input gain is +0.006 AP for the signature and ~0 HOTA. Under shift: literature effective robustness of bio front-ends is +3 to +6 points on corruption/domain-shift benchmarks at a 1-5 point clean cost (VOneNet, EVNet), and a frozen fly lost 6x less under degradation than trained nets (0.030 vs 0.188) in Kuklev 2026. For the project the plausible ceiling is recovering 10-30% of the day-to-night transfer drop of the component the fly feeds (signature AUC or q_match AP) [uncertain: transfer drop not yet measured]; for the detector itself, night mAP50 change within +/-0.01 of 0.3772 under in-domain evaluation, larger only in a day-trained/night-tested protocol [uncertain].

## Где обязано помогать
Must help: night test videos when the component is trained on day only; other-city and adverse-weather videos when trained on one group; rare night classes (rider, motorcycle, bicycle, bus); training subsets of 5-20 videos. No-harm: in-domain day performance (the clean-accuracy tax of every bio front-end: 0.4-5 points) and in-domain night with full data; report both, and the residual above the accuracy-on-the-line fit.

## Отрицательный контроль
(1) Shuffled-connectome fly and a degree-preserving rewired fly (Dhiman 2026: the flyvis topology advantage can vanish under a degree-preserving null; Kuklev: rewiring cost 0.27-0.36); (2) receptor-only input (721 facets, no connectome) with the same head - the same information without the prior; (3) non-biological invariance prior with the same inputs: DoG/wavelet front-end plus noise (Su & Kempe) and simple per-crop contrast normalisation (Retinex/gamma) - if they match the fly under shift, the robustness is low-pass filtering plus normalisation, not the fly; (4) the same model trained with the target-condition data included (oracle) - the prior is credited only for the part of the gap below the oracle.

## Вклад ветви
For trainable placements: per-epoch norm of the fly-path weights and gradient ratio fly/base; for frozen-fly heads: per-feature-group permutation importance on the shifted test set vs the in-domain set (a prior should matter more under shift); learning-curve slope per data fraction.

## Минимальный эффект и стоимость
Probe and accuracy-on-the-line diagnostics: CPU, 3-5 h total, no GPU. Signature/head learning curves: minutes per fit, 1-2 h total. MDE for paired video-bootstrap AUC differences in the existing heads: the fly-over-input CI half-width is ~0.005 ([+0.001; +0.012]), so MDE ~0.007 AUC/AP at 80% power. Detector under a day-to-night protocol: day-only YOLO26s 12 epochs ~4.3 h per run (fewer images, so less [uncertain]); 3 seeds x 2 arms (with/without fly augmentation) ~26 h; MDE for night mAP50 with SD 0.0031 per arm: 0.0071 at n = 3, 0.0055 at n = 5; the day-to-night drop of a day-only model is expected to be much larger than 0.0965 (the current class-averaged night gap) [uncertain], making a 10% recovery of it detectable.

## Вердикт для проекта
Primary, structural explanation: the project asks a biological prior to improve an in-distribution, label-rich detector, the regime where the bitter lesson and accuracy-on-the-line predict ~0, and that is what was measured (DRB branches switched off, G3 harm, fly-over-input +0.006, DetA unchanged). It does not mean the fly is useless: the regimes where it helped (re-identification heads trained on few videos, retina-lamina fixing the fly's own night dip) are exactly the small-data/shift regimes. Priority: high and cheap - add a shift protocol (day -> night, city -> city) and an accuracy-on-the-line plot before any further fly module; frame the dissertation claim as robustness/data efficiency of the fly component, while keeping in-domain numbers reported honestly.
