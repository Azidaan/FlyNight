*Постановка.* Replace independent weighted candidate BCE by a joint categorical action likelihood over three candidate logits and a fixed zero null logit. Null means no useful candidate, not necessarily physical target absence. Acceptable set includes every candidate already labelled useful. Loss=-log(sum probability of acceptable actions); null is the sole acceptable action if the original useful labels are all zero. Same original positive weight multiplies useful-row loss; fixed optimizer/batches/seeds/1150steps/145950presentations. Objective and its normalization are the intervention; no new parameters.

Reuse18 original BCE models only after full ensemble replay and32-step original loss reproduction. Geometry and R1 use exactly the same intervention. Unknown training labels excluded. Independent BCE can already learn negatives.
