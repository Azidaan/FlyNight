*Действие.* Prepare one target-conditioned proposal readout: choose whether its output should generate a new center or full XYWH box. This single gate determines that action space before any learner. User25Oct explicitly authorized trying the literature-derived variants and a final outcome report.

*Критерии до расчёта.* Center prototype only if observable-eye center oracle mean>=2pp of all5822known rows, lower95%videoCI>0 and increment over original D-threepeak oracle lower>0. Full-box prototype only if observable-eye full-box oracle mean>=2pp, lower>0 and increment over observable-eye center oracle lower>0. All106original recoverable and895remaining rows retained. NULL-only or GT-only reserve does not admit a box/center learner. Outside-eye reserve requires its own observed-input protocol, never immediate full-frame training. These are development gates, not a scientific effect threshold.

*Проверка.* All26raw baseline outputs bit-exact. All1001original incorrect known rows and original C/D/R1 semantic outcomes exact. The remaining895 match old no-useful-D grouping exactly. Original first alternative equals baseline in every arm, full mean restored including on exceptions, hashes immutable. First2videos conservative2*mean*24+45 must fit original600s. Completed videos not repeated.

*Возражение.* Oracle selection uses outcome labels, so it is an optimistic geometry-only diagnostic independent of FlyVis. Actual detection proposals and annotation boxes do not exhaust continuous actions. One-step baseline states omit simultaneous corrections/feedback/low-score association. Missing annotation is not physical absence. Correct high detection may still be unreachable because of competition; this is not all assigned to representation.
