# C9 Активный просмотр микросаккадами (Kuklev 2026; Juusola et al.)

↑ [[07 Исследования/Модернизация мухи и линия B/Модернизация мухи и линия B|Модернизация мухи и линия B]]

## Суть
Active viewing replaces the stabilized foveal crop with a short, prescribed micro-drift of the same crop. Today each per-track eye is re-centered on the predicted box every frame (tracking/foveated_eyes.py, context 2.0), so the object is almost still inside the eye. The motion-sensitive T4/T5 types then respond mostly to prediction error and background, which is the model analogue of perceptual fading. Under active viewing, the frozen FlyVis would receive a few controlled sweeps of the object image (e.g. 4 directions x 2 speeds, about 12 steps of 20 ms), and the signature would be read from the responses the sweeps evoke. The biological motivation is Juusola's work on photoreceptor microsaccades: photomechanical contractions shift and narrow the receptive fields, which gives hyperacuity beyond facet spacing and prevents fading. The direct engineering precedent is Kuklev 2026, who drove a frozen FlyVis over static drawings with prescribed motion and found that a static frame loses accuracy.

## Относимость к проекту
Targets register item N11 (the fly as an identity signature). The signature carries object identity (+0.14 AP over geometry). Over its own 721-facet input, however, it adds only +0.006 AP on test (+0.010 at night), it is worse than that input when used alone (-0.052 AP), and the gain does not reach HOTA. One plausible reason is specific to our setup: the stabilized fovea feeds FlyVis an almost static object image, so the direction-selective machinery that the connectome is good at barely participates. Active viewing tests whether the fly's own processing becomes more informative than its input once it is given motion to process. The night gain (+0.010) and the rewiring effect (+0.044 AP on the fly-alone head) suggest there is circuit-specific signal worth amplifying. It also touches N10 (foveal resolution): sub-facet shifts are the mechanism of hyperacuity. Indirectly it targets IDSw after occlusion through the re-identification and stitching path.

## Пересечение с чужими работами
Kuklev 2026 has already done almost exactly this: a frozen FlyVis driven by prescribed motion, used as a descriptor, with a matched receptors-only baseline, degree-preserving and type/sign-preserving rewiring nulls, and a static-frame ablation. The domain was architectural drawings (static images, no tracking). Juusola et al. provide the biology (photomechanical microsaccades, hyperacuity, stereo, high-frequency synaptic jumping) but no computer-vision descriptor. AMI-EV transfers microsaccades to hardware (a rotating wedge prism on an event camera), with no connectome model. cannot_claim: (a) the idea of probing a frozen FlyVis with microsaccade-like prescribed motion; (b) the static-versus-drift ablation design; (c) the matched receptor-baseline protocol. All three belong to Kuklev 2026. What may remain ours: applying it to per-track foveal eyes in driving MOT video (moving camera, 30 Hz), re-driving from the cached eye input, stratifying by illumination, and linking the result to stitching HOTA/IDF1. Negative outcomes must not be presented as novelty.

## Результаты у авторов
Kuklev 2026 (static drawings, synthetic motion only, no camera motion). Probe: a 64x64 window resized to the model eye and translated in 4 directions at 2 speeds (3 and 6 frame px per step), giving 8 trajectories of 12 frames at a 20 ms step, of which the first 4 are settling. Descriptor: 57 non-input types x {mean, temporal SD, spatial variation} x 8 = 1,368 dims, compared by cosine. Clean synthetic hatch matching: frozen fly 0.857 accuracy, receptors-only 0.891, task-trained 5,888-parameter CNN 0.959. Held-out test: fly 0.835, receptors 0.894, trained networks 0.971-0.980. Real production sheets (14): fly (1,876 params) 0.505 AP versus a 365,921-param CNN 0.415 AP. The biological model degrades less under realistic noise. Rewiring (dev) costs: degree-preserving -0.318, type-pair/sign-preserving -0.271, Erdos-Renyi -0.356 accuracy. Positive control, silencing R7/R8: -0.092. Motion ablation: static frame instead of drift -0.089 (paired) on hatch matching; single static frame versus 4-direction sweep -0.062 IoU and -0.050 AP. Juusola lab: microsaccades give hyperacute resolution beyond the interommatidial limit and mirror-symmetric binocular sampling for hyperacute stereo (electrophysiology, high-speed imaging, behaviour, models). The 2026 Nat. Commun. paper reports about 2,500 bits/s photoreceptor sampling and about 4,100 bits/s synaptic transmission, bandwidth extended to about 1,000 Hz during saccades, and behavioural responses in about 13-20 ms. AMI-EV: wedge prism at 720 RPM, 0.5-1.0 deg deflection, 1.5-2.0 px compensation error, DVXplorer 640x480. It reports longer corner-tracking lifetime than a standard event camera and better human-pose IoU/PDJ, and it is more robust than frame cameras under HDR illumination (the size of that advantage is reported loosely, about 50%) [uncertain: AMI-EV and Nat. Commun. figures come from abstracts or secondary summaries, not full-text tables].

## Уровень измерения
Kuklev: descriptor level (matching accuracy, segmentation AP/IoU), no tracking. Juusola: neural and behavioural level. AMI-EV: feature-track lifetime and pose IoU/PDJ. None of them measures association pairs in MOT or HOTA/IDF1 after TrackEval.

## Согласованный контроль
Kuklev: yes on both counts. The receptors-only descriptor built from the same photoreceptor samples beats the fly on held-out data (0.894 vs 0.835), so the fly's gain is robustness to noise and on real sheets, not raw accuracy. Graph nulls: degree-preserving rewiring -0.318, type-pair/sign-preserving -0.271, Erdos-Renyi -0.356, which means exact wiring matters strongly in his task. There is also a positive control (R7/R8 silencing -0.092). Whether the receptors-only baseline received the same drift, and so whether the static-versus-drift gap also holds for receptors, is not clear from the text we retrieved [uncertain]. Juusola and AMI-EV: no information-matched control and no graph null (not applicable).

## Разрез по условиям
Kuklev: clean versus realistic noise only (the fly degrades less). No night, overexposure, occlusion or density breakdown in any of the sources. For us, a dark/light split (as in [[fly_matched_controls_v1]]) would have to be added.

## Стоимость у нас
A cheap offline test is possible without video. runs/fly_signatures stores first_hexin/last_hexin (721 facets after retina-lamina, mean over K=5 frames) for 209,486 lives in 140 videos. Frozen FlyVis can be re-driven from these images with prescribed sub-facet shifts on the hex lattice (interpolated; 4 directions x 2 speeds x 12 steps at dt 0.02 after the usual 1 s fade-in), with no video decoding, retina or tracker. Per life we would store 34 output types x {mean, temporal SD, spatial SD} x 8 trajectories. Matched controls, all built from the same cached input: (i) the same statistics of the swept input sequence itself (active input-only), (ii) static input with no sweep, (iii) the same sweep through the rewired connectome (the rewire() hook in tracking/connectome_null.py already exists). The ReID ladder and video bootstrap in scripts/eval_fly_reid.py take CPU minutes. For scale, the original signature build took 18.0 GPU-process-hours summed over 140 videos (389 s for a 1,213-frame video). The offline probe has no per-frame dependency and can use much larger batches; we estimate 5-20 GPU-hours summed [uncertain: not benchmarked], less if restricted to lives that appear in the train/dev/test pairs. An online version (sweeps inside the tracker at the first/last K frames of each life) needs a full signature rebuild of at least about 18 GPU-hours summed. Launch per plan section 9 (Start-Process, Idle priority).

## Ожидаемый эффект
Fly-alone AP is likely to rise. Kuklev's static-versus-drift gap is 0.05-0.09 on his tasks, and our fly-alone head is 0.582 test versus 0.625 for input-alone. The increment of the fly over its input is expected to stay small. It is +0.006 AP now; Kuklev's receptors still beat the fly on held-out data, and the matched control gets the same sweep. The realistic range for (input_active + fly_active) - (input_active) is 0 to +0.01 AP on test [uncertain: extrapolated from project numbers and Kuklev, not measured], possibly larger at night, where processing helped most (+0.010). HOTA: no change is expected. Pair AP barely predicts stitching HOTA (geometry-only 59.283 versus appearance 59.347, CI through zero), so a fly effect at the pair level of about 0.01 AP will not reach HOTA. Main value: a clean answer to whether the fly processes motion better than its input when given motion, including a rewiring null.

## Риски
(1) The offline probe sweeps a K-frame mean image, which is blurred and lacks the real temporal context. The input-only control shares this artifact, but the absolute numbers will not transfer to the online eye. (2) Changing motion and statistics together violates the one-condition rule, so the design must be 2x2 (static/drift x mean-only/full statistics). (3) Many features (34 x 3 x 8 = 816 per life) raise the risk of overfitting and multiple testing. Choose regularization on dev only, and report test and val7 once. (4) Hex-lattice interpolation and a 20 ms step may leave the regime FlyVis was trained in (the integration dt is capped at 0.02). (5) GPU contention with any running training (use Idle priority). (6) Sign transfer from short or subset runs to full runs is forbidden. (7) Overlap with Kuklev 2026 must go into cannot_claim. The detector stays frozen and is not affected.
