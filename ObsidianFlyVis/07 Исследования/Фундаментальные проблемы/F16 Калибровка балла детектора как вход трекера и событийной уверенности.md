# F16 Калибровка балла детектора как вход трекера и событийной уверенности

↑ [[07 Исследования/Фундаментальные проблемы/Фундаментальные проблемы|Фундаментальные проблемы]]

> Multivariate Confidence Calibration 2020, ConfTrack WACV 2024, BoostTrack 2024: некалиброванный балл ночью/днём; калибровка до добавления признаков мухи.

## Механизм
1) What calibration is and what it cannot do. A detector score s is calibrated if, among boxes with score s, a fraction s are true positives at a given IoU. Kuppers et al. (CVPRW 2020) showed that detector miscalibration is not only global but depends on box position and scale (D-ECE as a function of cx, cy, w, h), and that a multivariate calibration map (histogram binning, logistic or beta calibration over [score, cx, cy, w, h]) removes most of it. The key property for this project: ANY strictly monotone map applied to all boxes with one global function leaves the ranking unchanged, so per-class AP is unchanged and, for a tracker with fixed thresholds (ByteTrack high 0.5-0.6 / low 0.1 / birth threshold), it is exactly equivalent to moving the threshold. Global calibration therefore cannot add a single true positive at equal FP. Calibration changes operating points only when it is CONDITIONAL: a different map per class, per illumination regime (night/day/glare), per box size, per object age. Then the relative order of boxes across conditions changes and a single threshold becomes a different threshold per condition.
2) Why the score is miscalibrated across conditions. Training targets in Ultralytics are IoU-weighted (target confidence = IoU of the best predicted box, ultralytics/utils/tal.py:140-142), so a box that is localised worse at night gets a lower target score even if the class evidence is correct; the score mixes 'is it an object' and 'how good is the box'. Under domain shift calibration degrades further: Munir et al. (NeurIPS 2022) report D-ECE of FCOS rising from in-domain to out-of-domain and post-hoc temperature scaling fitted in the source domain making it worse on Cityscapes->BDD100K (D-ECE 19.5 -> 27.4), because a single temperature fitted on one domain does not transfer. Kuzucu et al. (ECCV 2024) show that properly fitted post-hoc calibrators (Platt, isotonic regression) beat train-time calibration losses (Cal-DETR by >7 D-ECE on COCO), i.e. calibration is cheap and is a post-processing problem, not a training problem.
3) Why it matters for a tracker and for event heads. (a) ByteTrack/XYWHByteTrack use the raw score as a hard switch between first and second association and birth; an under-confident night score sends true objects to the second stage or blocks birth. (b) Trackers that use the score as a soft variable - ConfTrack (WACV 2024) scales the Kalman observation noise with confidence and penalises low-confidence objects in the cost; NSA-Kalman (GIAOTracker 2021) uses R_k = (1 - c_k) R; Hybrid-SORT (AAAI 2024) tracks confidence as a Kalman state - implicitly assume the score is a probability with the same meaning in all conditions. If night scores are systematically lower for the same IoU, the filter trusts night observations less than it should. (c) For learned event heads (q_det, q_match, q_birth, q_alive) the raw score enters as a feature. If the score is miscalibrated per condition, the head will use ANY other feature that encodes the condition (brightness, contrast, noise level) to correct the score. A biological feature that correlates with illumination (fly photoreceptor/lamina outputs certainly do) can then get credit for being a 'night indicator' that fixes calibration, not for carrying new object information. This is the methodological reason to calibrate BEFORE adding fly features: the baseline head must already contain conf plus a conditional calibration (class x illumination x size), otherwise the fly gain is confounded with a recalibration gain.
4) Boosting the score with track context (BoostTrack 2024, BoostTrack++) is a form of conditional recalibration (condition = 'overlaps a live tracklet'). Its gain shrinks to near zero on strong baselines (see solutions), which is consistent with this project's finding that the detector score already contains the temporal information available from box tracks.

## Где видно у нас
Conditional miscalibration is measured, the ranking ceiling is also measured, and they point in different directions. (1) Card [confidence_calibration↗](obsidian://open?vault=ObsidianFlyVis&file=02%20%D0%AD%D0%BA%D1%81%D0%BF%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%B5%D0%BD%D1%82%D1%8B%2Fconfidence_calibration) (experiments/results/confidence_calibration.csv, plan section 2.5): inside cells class x size x crowding x score bin, the night precision is higher than the day precision at the same score: +0.0355 averaged over all bins and +0.0980 in the working band 0.1-0.5 (night scores are under-confident exactly where the ByteTrack thresholds sit); the effect is class-heterogeneous (car and pedestrian under-confident, bus over-confident by -0.20...-0.40) and persists at 960 input (+0.0911, confidence_calibration_960.csv). For size bucket 1 the night-day precision gap in bins 0.3-0.4 and 0.4-0.5 is +0.141 and +0.128. (2) Threshold losses are 21.0 % of objects at night versus 15.5 % by day while blindness (no candidate at all) is 4.5 % in both, so most of the night gap is below-threshold candidates - exactly the regime where a conditional calibration would act. (3) But the ranking is saturated: [event_heads_v1↗](obsidian://open?vault=ObsidianFlyVis&file=02%20%D0%AD%D0%BA%D1%81%D0%BF%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%B5%D0%BD%D1%82%D1%8B%2Fevent_heads_v1) q_det head (conf + geometry + context) versus monotone control of conf: AP 0.9190 -> 0.9197, delta -0.0026 [-0.0162; +0.0071] (runs/event_heads/report.json); gates G0b (5 Hz tubelet rescoring, night +1.6 % [-0.6; +3.9] at equal FP), G0b-30 (30 Hz, tubelet + motion, -0.1 % dev+test) and G2 (retraining without IoU-multiplied target, night -0.15 pp, mAP50 -0.0006) are all null. The score's Spearman correlation with box IoU is 0.731 / 0.732 day / night, so the score-IoU relation itself is not broken at night; the miscalibration is a shift of the score-to-precision map, not noise. (4) Calibration-dependent event heads: q_birth head improves AP 0.5480 -> 0.6931 but its ECE is worse than the control (0.109 vs 0.061) - the head ranks better but is less calibrated, so any downstream use as a probability (birth threshold, fusion with fly features) needs a separate calibration step. q_alive gains mainly in Brier (0.1401 -> 0.0825) - a calibration gain, not a ranking gain. (5) The fly decoder itself needed a calibration coefficient (g 0.398 -> 0.243 after the retina-lamina front-end, plan line on stage A), showing that fly outputs change scale with illumination. (6) The motion-reliability head with FlyVis has ECE 0.0221 versus 0.0212 without it on test (motion_reliability_v1): adding the fly slightly degraded calibration. (7) Final tracker: DetA 58.21 -> 58.17 (unchanged) and FN 15 763 >> IDSw 2 221; nothing that only re-orders association can move DetA, while conditional thresholds can in principle move FN.

## Решения из литературы
A. Post-hoc conditional calibration (cheap, strongest evidence). Kuppers 2020: Faster R-CNN ResNet-50 at IoU 0.75 on COCO, confidence-only D-ECE 26.1 % -> 1.0 % with histogram binning; position-aware example: global ECE 7.3 % -> 1.1 % with global logistic calibration but position-conditional D-ECE only 11.2 % -> 8.7 %, whereas dependent multivariate logistic calibration brings it to 3.3 % - i.e. a global map fixes the average but not the conditional error. Kuzucu ECCV 2024: isotonic regression / Platt scaling fitted per class on a held-out split beat train-time methods (D-DETR + isotonic beats Cal-DETR by >7 D-ECE on COCO); they also stress that calibration must be evaluated at the operating threshold actually used and that LaECE should account for IoU. Under domain shift, TCD (NeurIPS 2022) FCOS Cityscapes->BDD100K: D-ECE 19.5 (baseline) / 27.4 (temperature scaling fitted on source) / 12.4 (TCD train-time loss), AP50 19.5 / 19.3 / 22.0; Faster R-CNN: 16.3 / 13.7 / 9.6 at AP50 23.3 / 23.3 / 23.5. Lesson: fit the calibrator on data from the target condition (here: night videos are labelled, so fit per condition directly); a source-fitted single temperature can hurt. B. Train-time calibration losses (BPC CVPR 2023, MCCL CVPR 2023, Cal-DETR NeurIPS 2023) give AP changes of roughly 0 to +1 and D-ECE reductions of a few points; per Kuzucu 2024 they are dominated by good post-hoc calibrators [uncertain for YOLO-family detectors, none tested on YOLO26]. C. Using the calibrated score inside the tracker. ConfTrack (WACV 2024): confidence-adaptive Kalman observation noise + low-confidence penalisation + cascaded matching; MOT17 test HOTA 65.4, IDF1 81.2, MOTA 80.0; MOT20 test HOTA 64.8, IDF1 80.2 (versus ByteTrack MOT17 63.1 / 77.3 / 80.3; MOT20 61.3 / 75.2 / 77.8) - but ConfTrack changes several components at once, so the gain cannot be attributed to calibration alone [uncertain: per-component ablation numbers not verified]. NSA Kalman (GIAOTracker, StrongSORT) is the standard 'R scaled by (1 - c)' trick; StrongSORT reports NSA as one of several small additive modules (+0.1 to +0.5 IDF1-HOTA on MOT17 val) [uncertain]. Hybrid-SORT models confidence as a Kalman state (TCM): +4.0 HOTA on DanceTrack val over its OC-SORT-like base, with the Kalman variant of confidence modelling +2.9 HOTA in the first association, linear prediction -1.1 HOTA. D. Track-context confidence boosting. BoostTrack (MVA 2024) detection-likelihood (DLO) boost: on MOT20 val with a plain SORT baseline HOTA 56.65 -> 58.58 (+1.93) but IDs +151 and IDSW +132; on the strong BoostTrack+ baseline MOT17 val HOTA 72.17 -> 72.41 (+0.24) and MOT20 val 62.59 -> 62.58 (0.0) with IDs 1852 -> 2019 (Table 4, arXiv:2408.13003). BoostTrack++ soft boost + varying threshold: MOT17 test HOTA 66.4 -> 66.6, MOT20 test 66.2 -> 66.4. Net message: confidence boosting gives ~+2 HOTA over weak trackers and ~0 to +0.2 over strong ones - the same pattern this project measured with tubelet rescoring. E. Separate the meanings of the score (UA-MOT style, and this project's q_det/q_match/q_birth/q_alive): objectness probability, localisation quality (IoU-aware heads: VarifocalNet, GFL quality score; YOLO26 already fuses IoU into the target), association probability. Calibrate each one separately and only then combine.

## Что сделать с мухой
1) Do not use the fly to fix the score. The rescoring gates (G0b, G0b-30, q_det head) show the score's ranking is saturated; calibration only moves thresholds. A fly feature can at best act as an illumination-aware recalibration, which a 3-parameter Platt map per (class x night/day/glare x size) does for free. 2) Make the calibration stage explicit and frozen BEFORE the fly: calibrated_conf = isotonic_or_Platt(conf | class, illumination regime, size bucket, age bucket), fitted on the train videos of the event dataset (event_dataset_v1), frozen, and fed to XYWHByteTrack thresholds and to every event head as the baseline feature. The fly is then evaluated as 'calibrated_conf + geometry + DIS + fly' against 'calibrated_conf + geometry + DIS + illumination scalars (mean, std, percentile luminance of the crop and of the frame)'. Only the residual over the illumination-scalar control can be credited to the fly. 3) Where the fly can legitimately enter score-related decisions: (a) q_alive and q_birth, which are not detector-score problems (q_birth ranking changes, Spearman with conf 0.088); there the fly state of the foveated eye could signal 'something is still there' after detector dropout - but [flyvis_event_features_v1↗](obsidian://open?vault=ObsidianFlyVis&file=01%20%D0%9C%D1%83%D1%85%D0%B0%2F%D0%AD%D0%BA%D1%81%D0%BF%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%B5%D0%BD%D1%82%D1%8B%2Fflyvis_event_features_v1) already shows no gain (q_alive +fly T4T5 delta AP -0.0021 [-0.0086; +0.0088]); (b) Kalman observation noise: replace ConfTrack's R(conf) by R(calibrated_conf, fly motion residual), i.e. the fly modulates trust in the observation rather than its existence; train nothing in the fly, fit two scalars. 4) If the fly is ever trained end-to-end (F18), its outputs must be recalibrated after every training (the decoder coefficient already changed 0.398 -> 0.243 after the retina-lamina front-end), and calibration (ECE/Brier) must be reported next to AP for each head with and without fly.

## Тест до постройки
All on existing caches, CPU only, minutes. D1 (ceiling of conditional thresholding): on the val detection caches used by gates G0a/G0b, fit isotonic calibration per (class x night/day x size bucket) on half of the videos, apply on the other half, and compute recall of objects at equal total FP versus the global threshold 0.25 (same equal-FP protocol as G0b), with video bootstrap; repeat with an oracle per-condition threshold (choose the threshold per condition on the evaluation half itself) to get the upper bound. If the oracle per-condition threshold gives < +1 % of threshold-lost night objects at equal FP, calibration cannot move DetA and the topic closes. D2 (tracker sensitivity): run XYWHByteTrack on the frozen 7-video bench with track_high/track_low/new_track thresholds replaced by per-condition thresholds derived from D1 (no retraining; the [bench_freeze_v1↗](obsidian://open?vault=ObsidianFlyVis&file=02%20%D0%AD%D0%BA%D1%81%D0%BF%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%B5%D0%BD%D1%82%D1%8B%2Fbench_freeze_v1) cache makes it deterministic); this is a tracker run on cached detections, not detector inference. D3 (confound check for fly credit): in the existing ladders (fly_matched_controls_v1, [flyvis_retina_stageB_v1↗](obsidian://open?vault=ObsidianFlyVis&file=01%20%D0%9C%D1%83%D1%85%D0%B0%2F%D0%AD%D0%BA%D1%81%D0%BF%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%B5%D0%BD%D1%82%D1%8B%2Fflyvis_retina_stageB_v1)) add a control 'geometry + illumination scalars' and 'geometry + calibrated_conf'; if the dark-video fly gain (+0.0102 [+0.0032; +0.0175] over the eye input; retina-lamina +0.016 over geometry) shrinks to the CI of zero when illumination scalars are present, the fly's night benefit was a calibration/illumination proxy. D4: report ECE and Brier for every existing event head with and without fly - if the fly improves AP but worsens ECE (as in [motion_reliability_v1↗](obsidian://open?vault=ObsidianFlyVis&file=01%20%D0%9C%D1%83%D1%85%D0%B0%2F%D0%AD%D0%BA%D1%81%D0%BF%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%B5%D0%BD%D1%82%D1%8B%2Fmotion_reliability_v1): 0.0221 vs 0.0212), the head is overfitting the condition.

## Потолок
Global monotone calibration: exactly 0 % change in AP, recall at equal FP, DetA and HOTA (mathematical invariance). Conditional calibration: bounded above by the oracle per-condition threshold of D1. Available indirect bounds: the best learned rescoring head reached only +1.6 % [-0.6; +3.9] of night threshold-lost objects at equal FP, and the q_det head delta AP was -0.0026 [-0.0162; +0.0071]; a calibration map is a strict subset of what those heads could learn, so the expected ceiling is about 0-2 % of threshold-lost night objects, i.e. at most ~0.4 % of all night GT objects (2 % x 21 %), worth roughly 0-0.2 DetA [uncertain: not measured, estimate from the heads]. In the tracker literature the corresponding gain on strong baselines is 0 to +0.24 HOTA (BoostTrack DLO on BoostTrack+, MOT17/MOT20 val). The real value of calibration here is not metric gain but validity: correct attribution of fly gains and usable probabilities for q_birth/q_alive thresholds.

## Где обязано помогать
Must help: night (threshold losses 21.0 %), working score band 0.1-0.5, size bucket 1 (largest measured night-day precision gap, +0.13...+0.14), age 0 objects (birth), rare classes where miscalibration is largest (bus over-confident by 0.20-0.40; rider, motorcycle, bicycle carry 97 % of the night mAP gap). No-harm check: day subset and car class (car mAP night/day 0.817 / 0.816 - nothing to fix), FP count and false births per video must stay within the bootstrap CI of the global-threshold baseline, and IDSw must not rise (BoostTrack's DLO boost raised IDs by 8-12 % on MOT20 val, so ID count is the expected side effect).

## Отрицательный контроль
1) Global monotone calibration (same isotonic map for all conditions): must give exactly zero change; if it does not, the evaluation pipeline has a bug. 2) Shuffled-condition calibration: per-condition maps assigned to random conditions - any gain from conditional calibration must exceed it. 3) For fly credit: 'calibrated_conf + illumination scalars (crop/frame luminance mean, std, 5th/95th percentile, JPEG blockiness)' as the matched control with the same number of features as the fly readout; and the existing shuffled-connectome and own-input (721-facet hex input) controls, all evaluated AFTER calibration. 4) Calibration fitted on train videos only, evaluated on held-out videos (no fitting on the evaluation split, otherwise isotonic regression memorises).

## Вклад ветви
No training branch: calibration is post-hoc. Monitor (a) ECE/LaECE and Brier per condition before/after calibration, (b) the coefficient of the calibrated_conf feature and of the fly features in the logistic heads - if adding calibration makes the fly coefficients drop toward zero, the fly was carrying calibration; (c) for any jointly trained fly (F18), recompute the calibration map every few epochs and track the per-condition precision gap at scores 0.1-0.5.

## Минимальный эффект и стоимость
Cost: D1 and D3-D4 are CPU-only on existing caches (tens of minutes); D2 is a tracker pass over 7 cached videos (minutes). No GPU, no detector training. MDE: from the existing event-head bootstraps the 95 % CI half-width for delta AP at the q_det level is about 0.012 and for the reid ladders about 0.005-0.006 (e.g. [+0.0007; +0.0118]); with 70 held-out videos the MDE for recall at equal FP is about 2-3 % of threshold-lost objects (G0b CI half-width ~2.2-2.3 %). Hence the expected conditional-calibration ceiling (0-2 %) is at or below the MDE: a single run will most likely be inconclusive, which itself is the decision rule - if D1's oracle bound is below the MDE, do not pursue calibration as a metric lever; keep it only as a mandatory preprocessing step for fair fly attribution.

## Вердикт для проекта
Secondary as a cause of the failures, but primary as a methodological guard. It does not explain the null results of rescoring or DRB: the score's ranking is saturated (q_det head delta AP -0.0026, rescoring heads ~0), and global calibration is mathematically incapable of changing DetA. It does explain part of the night threshold loss (night precision +0.098 higher at equal score in 0.1-0.5), which only conditional thresholds can exploit, with an expected ceiling around 0-0.2 DetA. Most important for the fly: every fly gain measured in night-heavy subsets (retina-lamina +0.016, dark-video fly over eye input +0.0102) is at risk of being an illumination/calibration proxy until it is re-measured on top of a calibrated score and illumination-scalar controls. Priority: high for D3/D4 (cheap, protects attribution), medium for D1 (one CPU evaluation, decides whether conditional thresholds go into XYWHByteTrack), low for any trained calibration method.
