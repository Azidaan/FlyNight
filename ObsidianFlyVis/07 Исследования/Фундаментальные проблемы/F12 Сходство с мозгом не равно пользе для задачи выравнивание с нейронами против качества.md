# F12 Сходство с мозгом не равно пользе для задачи: выравнивание с нейронами против качества

↑ [[07 Исследования/Фундаментальные проблемы/Фундаментальные проблемы|Фундаментальные проблемы]]

> Brain-Score против качества на ImageNet, зависимость выводов от меры сходства (bioRxiv 2024); что значит для FlyVis как модели нейронов, а не инженерного блока.

## Механизм
Neural alignment and task performance are two different objectives that coincide only over part of the model space. (1) Decoupling at high performance. Brain-Score (Schrimpf et al., bioRxiv 2018; Neuron 2020): below ~70% ImageNet top-1, accuracy and Brain-Score correlate strongly (r ~ 0.92); above 70% the correlation weakens, and the most brain-like models were mid-accuracy (DenseNet-169, CORnet-S, ResNet-101). Linsley et al. (NeurIPS 2023): the newest high-accuracy DNNs are worse IT models, because they rely on different features than IT, and this worsens with accuracy; Linsley, Feng, Serre (arXiv 2504.16940, 2025): alignment is plateauing or falling as models reach human-level accuracy. Gokce & Schrimpf (ICML 2025, 600+ models): behavioural alignment keeps scaling, neural alignment saturates. (2) The verdict 'which model is most brain-like' depends on the measure. Soni, Srivastava, Maechler, Kording, Khosla (bioRxiv 2024.08.07.607035): across 9 similarity measures, models best under one measure are often worst under another and prior conclusions can flip or dissolve; mean Kendall tau between model rankings 0.477 within a dataset and 0.364 across datasets; measures split into lenient (linear predictivity, RSA) and strict (pairwise/soft matching) clusters. (3) Degeneracy in connectome-constrained models. Lappalainen et al. (Nature 2024; 45,669 neurons, 1,513,231 connections, 734 free parameters, ensemble of 50 models trained on optic flow) found that better task performance predicts T4/T5 direction selectivity better (r = 0.60, P = 2.6e-6), but contrast-preference predictions do not correlate with task performance, random-parameter models with the connectome already predict ON/OFF preference, and the ensemble contains distinct T4c solutions - task performance alone does not uniquely determine the circuit. Beiran & Litwin-Kumar (Nat. Neurosci. 2025): connectivity plus task output leaves a degenerate space of gains/biases with different activity; recordings from about D neurons (the dynamics' dimensionality) are needed to break it. Zhou & Hasler (bioRxiv 2026) [uncertain: preprint, not peer reviewed]: in flyvis, behavioural fidelity is achievable without biological fidelity; RSA vs biological T4/T5 r = 0.930 for connectome-constrained vs 0.603 for random wiring at matched behaviour. (4) Therefore FlyVis is validated as a model of neurons (its tuning matches 26 studies), not as an engineering block: its 734 parameters were chosen to make Sintel optic flow decodable from biologically plausible units, and an unconstrained CNN reaches lower flow error. Its value for a downstream task must be shown by task metrics under equal-information controls; neural plausibility neither guarantees nor excludes utility.

## Где видно у нас
The project's numbers reproduce the decoupling. (1) The fly is a good neuron model but a poor flow engine on BDD: flow error 52.77 px vs 4.87 px for DIS, ΔHOTA ≈ 0 as motion source. (2) Biological wiring matters for the fly but not for the task beyond its input: the true connectome beats the shuffled one in re-identification (0.582 vs 0.522, +0.044 [0.027; 0.059]), yet over its own 721-facet input the fly adds only +0.006 [+0.001; +0.012] (+0.010 night), and alone it is worse than the input (-0.052); over the input the shuffled-vs-true difference is not significant. This is the pattern 'topology shapes the representation (alignment) but the information for the task is already in the input (utility)'. (3) The retina-lamina front-end, the most biologically motivated addition, fixes the fly's own night dip (+0.016) but does nothing for DIS (+0.001) and does not reach DetA. (4) Final tracker: HOTA 58.47 -> 59.27 from geometry-only stitching (+0.79); appearance and fly over geometry not significant; DetA 58.21 -> 58.17. (5) The project used a single ensemble member (flow/0000/000) with an uncalibrated decoder (registry item H12): with r = 0.60 between task error and DSI and distinct solution clusters in the ensemble, member choice can change both biology and utility, and this has not been measured.

## Решения из литературы
A. Separate the two claims and test each with its own metric: neural fidelity (tuning, DSI, RSA/CKA to recorded responses) and task utility (AP, HOTA, DetA) - never infer one from the other. Linsley 2023 'neural harmonizer' shows they can be co-optimised but only by an explicit alignment loss. B. Report alignment with several measures (lenient and strict: linear predictivity, RSA, CKA, soft matching) and show conclusions are stable (Soni et al. 2024); rank agreement in the field is only tau ~0.36-0.48. C. Treat the 50-model FlyVis ensemble as a population: choose members by downstream utility under a held-out check, or average them; Lappalainen report task error-DSI r = 0.60, so selection by flow error also partially selects biology. D. Break degeneracy with task-relevant constraints rather than more biology: fine-tune the 734 parameters on the downstream objective (K1/K2) with a regulariser that keeps the known tuning (DSI of T4/T5, ON/OFF polarity) within the ensemble range - utility is optimised, biology is preserved as a constraint, and the drift of fidelity metrics is reported (the knockout-training idea of Cowley 2024 for keeping unit identity). E. Use connectome topology as the hypothesis under test with proper nulls (shuffled, degree-preserving rewired, random-parameter with connectome), as in Kuklev 2026 (rewiring -0.27 to -0.36 accuracy) and Dhiman 2026 (advantage vanishes under a degree-preserving null).

## Что сделать с мухой
(1) Stop expecting the frozen Sintel-trained fly to be a good engineering motion or detection block on BDD: it is a neuron model; to make it a component, train it for the component's objective. Concretely K1: unfreeze the 734 parameters + retina-lamina, self-supervise on BDD train videos (next-frame prediction or box-transfer error vs DIS), keep the decoder small, freeze YOLO26 and the tracker. K2: train the signature (same/different object) with gradients through the 734 parameters. (2) Add a fidelity guard to every fine-tune: after training, recompute T4/T5 DSI and preferred direction, ON/OFF polarity of L1-L5/Mi/Tm cells on the flyvis standard stimuli (moving edges, flashes) and the RSA to the original ensemble; report utility gain together with fidelity drift. This turns the dissertation's subject into 'how far can a connectome model be pushed toward a task while staying a fly'. (3) Use the 50 ensemble members: evaluate the frozen signature/motion task for all 50 (cheap with cached eyes) - utility spread across members is an upper bound on what 'picking a better fly' can give, and its correlation with task error and DSI tells whether biology predicts utility here. (4) Read out the right cells: utility is in the input-proximal lamina channels at night and in T4/T5 for motion; the broad 34-type readout dilutes; probe per cell type.

## Тест до постройки
(1) Ensemble utility vs fidelity scan (no training, 2-4 h GPU on cached foveal inputs): for each of the 50 flyvis ensemble members compute (a) its published task error, (b) DSI correlation / ON-OFF accuracy on flyvis standard stimuli, (c) project utility: signature AP over input on val (fly_matched_controls protocol) and flow error vs DIS on dev+test night/day. Spearman correlation utility vs (a) and vs (b) with bootstrap CI. Decision: if utility correlates with task error but not with fidelity, optimise the task (K1/K2) and treat biology as a constraint; if utility range across members is < 0.005 AP, member choice and fine biology are irrelevant to the project's metric. (2) Redundancy test with CKA/linear probes (1-2 h): linear CKA and ridge R^2 between fly readouts (per cell type) and (i) the 721-facet input, (ii) YOLO26 neck features at the box, (iii) DIS flow inside the box, on 5-10k crops. High R^2 from input to fly (> 0.8) means the fly is a re-encoding of its input - consistent with +0.006 - and no amount of biological fidelity will add task information; the residual (fly not predicted from input) is the only possible source of utility; probe it alone for the task. (3) Multi-measure check: compute true-vs-shuffled connectome differences with RSA, CKA and soft-matching; if the sign of 'true is more distinct' depends on the measure, do not use it as an argument.

## Потолок
Biological fidelity sets no ceiling on task utility; the measured ceiling of the frozen fly as a signature is +0.006 AP over its input (+0.010 at night) and ~0 HOTA. For a task-trained fly the ceiling is bounded by the information in its 721-facet input sequence: the frozen fly is 0.052 AP below its own input, so a perfect task-trained fly at least closes that 0.052 gap and could add the input's +0.14-level identity value over geometry [uncertain]; at the tracker level the stitching ceiling is small (geometry +0.79 HOTA, appearance over geometry not significant). For motion, the ceiling of K1 is reaching DIS (4.87 px) from 52.77 px, i.e. 100% of the flow gap, but even DIS-quality motion gave ΔHOTA ≈ 0 in this tracker, so the HOTA ceiling of a better fly motion is ~0 unless the motion is used for birth/occlusion [uncertain].

## Где обязано помогать
Must help: night tracks and occlusion re-entries (where appearance and geometry are ambiguous); the 'fly residual' subset of crops where the fly readout is not predictable from its input. No-harm: day association AP, IDSw, and the fly's neural fidelity metrics (DSI, ON/OFF) must stay within the ensemble's range after any fine-tune.

## Отрицательный контроль
(1) Shuffled connectome and degree-preserving rewired connectome trained identically (fine-tune too); (2) random-parameter fly with the true connectome (Lappalainen: predicts contrast preference but not direction selectivity); (3) parameter-matched non-biological recurrent conv net on the same 721-facet hex input (same parameter count 734 + decoder) trained on the same objective - utility must exceed it to be attributed to biology; (4) receptor-only input with the same head. Agreement control: the gain must appear under at least two fidelity/similarity measures and two task metrics (AP and HOTA/DetA).

## Вклад ветви
During fine-tuning of the 734 parameters: per-epoch L2 drift of each parameter group (time constants, synaptic strengths per cell-type pair) from the Sintel checkpoint, task loss, and fidelity metrics (DSI of T4/T5, ON/OFF sign) computed every N steps; a utility gain with collapsed DSI means the network became a generic RNN, not a better fly.

## Минимальный эффект и стоимость
Ensemble scan: 50 members x cached foveal inputs; the per-eye fly costs ~150-390 ms per frame per process [uncertain], so restrict to val7 + a 20-video dev subset: ~2-4 h GPU. CKA/probes: CPU, 1-2 h. MDE for the signature AP with paired video bootstrap: CI half-width ~0.005 for fly-over-input, so MDE ~0.007 AP; to detect a utility-fidelity correlation of rho = 0.4 across 50 members at 80% power needs n ~ 47, so the full ensemble is just sufficient. K1/K2 fine-tunes: 2-5 h GPU per arm (pilot plan: 1-2 h on 20 train videos), 3 arms (true, shuffled, parameter-matched RNN) x 2 seeds ~ 12-30 h.

## Вердикт для проекта
Secondary but conceptually central: it does not by itself cause the failed experiments (that is the data regime, F11, and module placement), but it explains why 'a validated model of fly neurons' was expected to be a good engineering block and was not: FlyVis was optimised for Sintel flow and validated on neuron tuning, and both properties are weakly tied to BDD tracking utility (52.77 vs 4.87 px; +0.006 AP over input). Priority: medium-high and cheap - run the 50-member utility-vs-fidelity scan and the input-redundancy CKA before K1/K2; then fine-tune the fly for the task with a fidelity guard, which gives the dissertation a defensible claim (utility gained per unit of fidelity lost) without arguing that brain similarity implies usefulness.
