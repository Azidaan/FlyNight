# F1 Нет новой информации: неравенство обработки данных и избыточность модуля

↑ [[07 Исследования/Фундаментальные проблемы/Фундаментальные проблемы|Фундаментальные проблемы]]

> Почему фиксированная обработка тех же пикселей (FlyVis, DIS, тубелеты, сетчатка) не добавляет информации обученному детектору/трекеру; как это измерить заранее (условная взаимная информация, пробы).

## Механизм
1) Data processing inequality (DPI, Cover & Thomas, Elements of Information Theory, ch. 2): for any fixed function f of the pixels X, I(Y; f(X)) <= I(Y; X). A fixed FlyVis pass, DIS flow, a tubelet statistic or a retina model computed from the same frames cannot add Shannon information about the label Y that the frames do not already contain. 2) The relevant quantity for a module bolted onto a trained detector is not I(Y; f(X)) but the conditional information I(Y; f(X) | Z), where Z is what the detector already computes (neck features, its own score). If the detector is a high-capacity network trained on enough data, Z is close to a sufficient statistic of X for Y on the training distribution, so I(Y; f(X) | Z) ~ 0: the module is redundant even if f(X) alone is informative (that is why 'fly beats geometry' and 'fly over its own input ~0' can both be true). 3) DPI holds for unlimited observers. For computationally bounded learners Xu et al. (ICLR 2020) show usable (V-) information CAN be created by computation; this is the only legitimate route by which a fixed module helps on the same pixels: it makes information easier to extract for a model that otherwise cannot (small capacity, little data, wrong inductive bias, distribution shift). A YOLO26s trained on 1400 videos is not data-starved for the dominant class (car, 83 % of night boxes), so this route is narrow. 4) Real new information comes only from (a) new measurements (another sensor, more frames/time that a single-frame detector never sees, higher resolution), (b) new supervision/labels, or (c) a prior that encodes knowledge absent from the training set (the connectome is evolution's prior on motion, trained on Sintel, not on BDD objects). 5) Optimisation side of the same coin: when a branch is redundant with the main path, gradient descent drives its gate to zero or lets the dominant path absorb the signal (modality competition, Wang, Tran & Feiszli CVPR 2020; Peng et al. OGM-GE CVPR 2022). A dead branch is the optimiser's verdict that the branch adds no usable information at that cost. 6) Fixed preprocessing can even subtract information (DPI is an inequality): restoration/enhancement trained for human-perceived quality destroys cues the detector used and shifts its input distribution (Pei et al. ECCV 2018 dehazing does not help CNN classification; enhancement-before-detection on ExDark often fails to beat raw dim images, LIME-Eval 2024). 7) Measuring conditional MI directly in high dimension is unreliable: any distribution-free lower-bound estimator is limited to O(log N) nats (McAllester & Stratos, AISTATS 2020) and variational estimators have high variance (Song & Ermon, ICLR 2020). The practical substitute is conditional probing of usable information (Hewitt et al., EMNLP 2021) or MDL probing (Voita & Titov, EMNLP 2020), with control tasks (Hewitt & Liang, EMNLP 2019).

## Где видно у нас
Every negative result in the project fits 'no new usable information beyond what the detector/tracker already has': (1) Fly as identity signature: +0.14 AP over geometry, but only +0.006 [+0.001; +0.012] over its own 721-facet input, and fly alone is worse than its input (-0.052 AP) - a textbook DPI picture: f(input) loses information versus input, and almost nothing is gained conditionally. (2) Shuffled connectome: -0.044 AP for the fly alone, but over the input the difference is not significant - topology matters for what f extracts, not for what the system lacks. (3) Retina-lamina before the fly removes the fly's night drop (+0.016), while DIS on the same retina output gains +0.001: the retina makes information more accessible to a weak, fixed observer (the fly), not to a strong one (DIS) - exactly the bounded-observer exception of Xu et al. (4) DRB in the YOLO neck: one branch weight = 0 in all 896 channels, frequency mask converged to neutral in 4/4 runs, seed spread 0.0144 mAP at 12 epochs, sign flipped at 100 epochs, +17 % time: the optimiser rejected a branch computed from the same feature map. (5) Rescoring weak candidates by 5 Hz and 30 Hz tubelets with background-relative motion: ~0 at equal FP (G0b night head +1.6 % [-0.6; +3.9], G0b-30 dev+test -0.1 %); IoU-free confidence target: -0.15 pp at night (G2). The detector score already contains box precision, time and box motion. (6) Final tracker: HOTA 58.47 -> 59.27 while DetA 58.21 -> 58.17 (unchanged), FN 15 763 >> IDSw 2 221; geometry-only stitching +0.79 HOTA, appearance and fly on top not significant: post-hoc modules can re-link but cannot create detections the detector never emitted (blind 4.5 % at night and by day). (7) Frozen YOLO26 on photoreceptor / temporally accumulated frames: -2...-15 pp, blindness grows: fixed processing removed cues and shifted the input. (8) Fly as motion source: error 52.77 px vs 4.87 px for DIS, Delta HOTA ~ 0: a fixed Sintel-trained model is a worse estimator of a quantity the tracker already gets from DIS. The only place where something not-yet-used exists: the threshold losses (21.0 % at night vs 15.5 % by day) and the multi-frame signal a single-frame detector never sees - but G0b/G0b-30 show that temporal statistics of candidate boxes do not separate them, so the remaining information, if any, lies in pixels over time, not in box tracks.

## Решения из литературы
A) Stop feeding the module the same information; feed it information the host lacks. In the literature, gains against a strong base appear when a genuinely new measurement is fused: RGB+thermal halfway fusion on KAIST, miss rate 36.99 % vs 48.59 % for color-only Faster R-CNN (night: 35.49 % vs 64.39 %) (Liu et al. BMVC 2016); RGB+events on DSEC-Det, image-only 33.8 mAP -> hybrid 37.2 mAP, and 44.2 mAP for inter-frame detection, +6.7 mAP over image-based extrapolation (Gehrig & Scaramuzza, Nature 2024) [uncertain: exact table setting]; CFT on FLIR, two-stream YOLOv5 37.4 -> 40.2 mAP (RGB-only 31.8) (Fang et al. 2021). For a single RGB camera the only new measurement available is time: frames before t that the single-frame YOLO26 never sees. B) Make the module trainable for the target so it is not a fixed f: task-driven enhancement beats fixed enhancement - FeatEnHancer (ICCV 2023) +5.7 mAP on ExDark, +1.5 on DARK FACE, +5.1 mIoU on ACDC night when the enhancer is trained by the detection loss, whereas fixed pre-enhancement before YOLOv3 on ExDark gives no gain or a loss (LIME-Eval 2024). Caveat: these bases are weaker than YOLO26 trained on in-domain night data; project plan D8 notes YOLA-type gains of +1.7...+2.7 only against old bases. C) Measure conditional usable information before building: conditional probing (Hewitt et al. 2021) estimates I_V(Y; fly | base) as the drop in held-out cross-entropy of a probe on [base, fly] vs [base]; MDL probing (Voita & Titov 2020) is more stable at small effects; control tasks/random features of equal dimension (Hewitt & Liang 2019) separate information from probe capacity. D) If a module is kept, fight redundancy-driven collapse explicitly: gradient blending (Wang et al. 2020, Kinetics A+RGB 71.4 -> recovers above RGB-only 72.6 [uncertain: exact G-Blend number]), OGM-GE (CREMA-D concat 51.7 -> 61.9 %, VGGSound 49.1 -> 50.6 %), auxiliary loss on the branch alone, modality dropout. These fix optimisation, not information: if I(Y; f | Z) = 0 they cannot help.

## Что сделать с мухой
1) Give the fly what YOLO26 lacks: a history of 3-30 frames at 30 Hz, processed by the fly's temporal pathways (photoreceptor/lamina adaptation, T4/T5 motion, T2/T3 small-object channels), delivered to the detector as an extra feature map at P3/P4 - not as a replacement of the RGB input and not as post-hoc rescoring of the detector's own candidates (both closed by G3 and rule 6.14). 2) Do not keep the fly fixed: fine-tune its 734 parameters plus the retina on BDD100K video (candidate K1: self-supervised next-frame / box-transfer objective first, then end-to-end with the detector at a low learning rate). A fixed Sintel-trained fly is, by DPI, a lossy function of frames whose information the detector already extracts better. 3) Freeze: YOLO26 backbone for the probe stage; unfreeze the neck plus a zero-initialised 1x1 adapter in the training stage. 4) Keep the matched non-biological controls of identical shape: the same adapter fed with frame differences / DIS flow / mean of 3-5 homography-aligned frames, and the shuffled-connectome fly. The fly is credited only for information that remains after these controls (conditional on them), not after raw RGB alone. 5) Report the identity-signature result honestly as 'redundant with its input (+0.006)', and the retina result as a bounded-observer effect (+0.016 for the fly, +0.001 for DIS).

## Тест до постройки
Conditional V-information probe on existing caches, no detector training (hours of CPU/GPU): 1) Units: candidate locations from YOLO26 on val (all candidates above 0.01 plus GT-matched anchors), split by video; targets Y = 'true object at this location' and 'missed at the 0.25 threshold'. 2) Base features Z: YOLO26 neck features (P3/P4 pooled in the box, plus own score and box), cached once. 3) Candidate features F: fly responses (T4/T5, T2/T3, lamina) pooled in the same box over the last k frames; also controls: shuffled connectome, DIS flow statistics, frame-difference statistics, random projection of RGB of the same dimension. 4) Probes: logistic regression and a 2-layer MLP; report Delta = CE(Y | Z) - CE(Y | Z, F) in bits per candidate on held-out videos, with video bootstrap CI, separately for night, day, object age 0, occluded. 5) Decision rule written before running: build only if Delta(fly) > 0 with CI excluding 0 on night or age 0, AND Delta(fly) - Delta(best matched control) > 0 with CI excluding 0. The project already has a near-instance of this test: fly over its input +0.006 [+0.001; +0.012] AP - small but positive, i.e. the effect size to expect. 6) Redundancy screen (cheap, not a decision): linear CKA between F and Z on the same candidates; CKA close to 1 means redundant; low CKA does not mean useful (Davari et al. ICLR 2023 show CKA is sensitive to outliers and manipulable). 7) Oracle check: replace F by the true label shifted to the next frame (temporal oracle) to measure the maximal Delta any temporal feature could bring at this probe capacity.

## Потолок
Information ceiling for any same-pixel module added after the detector: zero for objects the detector never proposes (blind 4.5 % of GT at night and by day), and bounded by the threshold losses (21.0 % of night GT lost at the 0.25 threshold vs 15.5 % by day) for rescoring. Measured fraction of that ceiling reached by the best post-hoc signals: ~0 (G0b night head +1.6 % [-0.6; +3.9], G0b-30 -0.1 %); i.e. 0 % of the gap. For tracking, DetA did not move (58.21 -> 58.17, -0.07 %), HOTA +1.4 % (58.47 -> 59.27) came entirely from geometry stitching (+0.79 HOTA); fly and appearance beyond geometry: 0 within CI. Upper bound for a temporal fly branch inside the detector: if it recovered all night threshold losses minus the day level (21.0 - 15.5 = 5.5 pp of night GT) that would be the closable night-specific gap; literature for new-sensor fusion suggests recovering 20-40 % of such gaps, for same-camera temporal fusion less [uncertain]. Plan expectation for K4/K9 (retina or dense fly as input channel): 0...+1 pp.

## Где обязано помогать
Night and age-0 (newborn) objects at equal FP, and partially occluded objects - the subsets where the detector's single-frame evidence is weakest and multi-frame evidence is new. Rare night classes (rider, bus, motorcycle, bicycle carry 97 % of the night mAP gap) for mAP; cars for DetA/FN (83 % of night boxes; car mAP night/day 0.817/0.816 means no night gap for cars in mAP, so DetA gains must come from recall at threshold). No-harm checks: day subset, overexposure subset, and the 70 held-out videos; requirement: day change within the seed-spread CI and FP at the operating point unchanged.

## Отрицательный контроль
1) Shuffled-connectome fly with the same parameter count and the same training (tests whether topology, not capacity, carries the gain; the project already found -0.044 AP for the fly alone but no difference over the input). 2) Matched non-biological temporal control into the same adapter: frame differences, DIS flow, mean of 3-5 homography-aligned frames. The fly is credited only for Delta beyond the best of these. 3) Time-shuffled input (frames in wrong order) - kills genuine temporal information while keeping pixel statistics. 4) Zero-input branch (adapter fed with constants) - controls for the extra capacity and extra training of the neck. 5) Seed control with seed actually injected into the dataloader generator (rule 6.12), otherwise variance is not measured.

## Вклад ветви
Log per epoch: L2 norm of the adapter/gate weights (DRB died to exactly 0 in 896 channels - make that a stop signal); ratio ||grad_branch|| / ||grad_main|| at the fusion point (OGM-GE style discrepancy ratio); Delta loss when the branch is zeroed at evaluation (branch ablation every epoch on a fixed dev subset); conditional probe Delta on the fused features at checkpoints.

## Минимальный эффект и стоимость
Detector training comparisons: seed spread sigma = 0.0144 mAP at 12 epochs (DRB). Two-arm comparison with n seeds per arm, alpha 0.05, power 0.8: MDE ~ 2.8 * sigma * sqrt(2/n) = 0.033 mAP (n = 3), 0.026 (n = 5), 0.018 (n = 10). Cost at 12 epochs ~ 4.3 h per run on RTX 5070 12 GB: n = 3 per arm = 6 runs = 26 h; n = 5 = 43 h; n = 10 = 86 h. Any expected gain of 0...+1 pp is below the n = 3 MDE, so detector-level claims need either n >= 10 or the cheaper probe first. Probe-level comparisons on caches: the existing fly-over-input result has CI half-width ~0.0055 AP, i.e. MDE ~ 0.008 AP with the current number of videos; cost minutes to an hour per probe once features are cached; fly feature extraction ~1.2 ms per step at 1/16 density, 15.8 ms at full density on RTX 5070 (plan 7.5).

## Вердикт для проекта
Main explanation (priority 1). Almost every failed idea - DRB, fly as motion, fly as signature over its input, retina for DIS, tubelet/motion rescoring, frozen detector on processed frames - is a module computing a fixed function of information the detector or tracker already uses. The way forward for the fly is: (1) give it information the host lacks (multi-frame history at the pixel level), (2) make it trainable on BDD (K1), (3) prove conditional usable information with the probe above before any 4.3-hour training, and (4) credit the fly only for what remains after matched non-biological temporal controls. Novelty doubt, briefly: conditional probing and DPI arguments are standard; the contribution can only be their application to a connectome model, which goes to cannot_claim if the probe is null.
