# D11 Заслонённые и мелкие объекты в детекции вождения: что даёт больше всего на сильной базе

↑ [[07 Исследования/Детектор с мухой/Детектор с мухой|Детектор с мухой]]

> Приёмы для мелких и заслонённых объектов в YOLO и DETR 2023–2026: обучение на высоком разрешении, P2-голова, STAL в YOLO26, SAHI и тайлинг, амодальная детекция и учитывающая заслонение детекция; сколько дают на BDD100K — это сильные контрольные плечи без мухи, против которых должна выиграть муха.

## Суть
On a strong modern base the levers with reproducible gains for small objects are, in order: (1) training and inference resolution (official YOLO26s ablation on COCO: 640 -> 1280 raises AP from ~46 to ~50-51 and AP_S from ~27.6 to ~36); (2) a stride-4 P2 head (gains of +1.8 mAP50-95 / +4.6 mAP50 on BDD100K reported only on nano-size bases); (3) tiling/SAHI at inference and slicing-aided fine-tuning (+5 to +7 AP inference-only, +12.7 to +14.5 AP with fine-tuning, but on aerial images where objects are tiny relative to the frame); (4) small-target-aware label assignment, which YOLO26 already contains (STAL: +0.6 AP_S, +0.2 AP). For occlusion, BDD100K MOT boxes are modal (visible extent), so amodal detection (TAO-Amodal) does not match the evaluation; occlusion modules in YOLO variants are reported on weak bases with large, poorly controlled gains; the realistic levers are temporal (video aggregation, tracker persistence) and confidence calibration. These are the no-fly control arms that a fly detector must beat; the strongest and cheapest is simply training at the 960 resolution that the project already uses at inference.

## Место мухи
Two placements, both after YOLO, because the fly's 721-facet fovea cannot add resolution to a full 960 frame. (a) Second-look rescoring of low-confidence candidates (the SAHI idea turned into a fovea): for every YOLO candidate in the band [0.01, operating threshold) that is small (< 32x32 px at 960 [threshold to pre-register]) or overlaps another box (occlusion proxy), place a fly eye centred on the box with field of view ~2-3x box size, run retina-lamina -> FlyVis for 5-10 frames of the 30 Hz stream preceding the frame, and feed the type-wise readout (34 types x 19 macrocolumns, as in build_fly_signatures.py) together with the YOLO score/class/geometry into a small rescoring head. What from the fly: retina-lamina (contrast normalisation of a dim, tiny patch), and T4/T5 direction-selective outputs that respond to local motion relative to background - a partially occluded car or a distant pedestrian moving against the ego-flow gives motion evidence that a single-frame detector lacks. (b) Motion-defined proposal source at birth: T4/T5 local motion contrast over the whole frame (coarse grid of eyes) proposes regions where something moves inconsistently with ego-motion, which are then re-scored by YOLO at higher resolution. Placement (b) is costlier and closer to the STMD literature. Why meaningful: the measured losses for small and occluded objects pass largely through the threshold (51% of the occlusion gap goes through score under-estimation), so a second opinion on existing candidates is the right lever; the fly is used where it has a documented own signal (identity +0.14 AP over geometry, connectome topology +0.044 AP) and where temporal integration at 30 Hz matters.

## Какую потерю закрывает
SMALL objects (only 56.7% found) - partly THRESHOLD (candidate exists with low score) and partly BLINDNESS for the tiniest; resolution-type arms (train@960, P2, SAHI) target blindness and localisation, the fly second-look targets the threshold part. OCCLUSION at night (night penalty 5.4x larger; project: 70.8% of objects occluded, 27.34 pp gap to clean, 51% of it through confidence under-estimation) - threshold part only; the other 49% (blindness, box inaccuracy) is not curable by rescoring. BIRTH (age 0: 31% night vs 68% at age 10+, 2/3 of the gap through threshold): newly appearing objects are often small/distant or entering from occlusion, so the same rescoring head can be evaluated on the age-0 stratum. The project already measured that doubling resolution returns only +0.025 of small-object recall (not half of the losses), so resolution alone has a measured ceiling here.

## Пересечение с чужими работами
Second-stage zoom/re-inference on candidate crops is SAHI and many cascade/zoom-in detectors; P2 heads and high resolution are standard (Ultralytics ships P2 configs for YOLOv8 [uncertain for YOLO26]); STAL is in YOLO26 itself; occlusion-feature modules (RSO-YOLO FECM, RE-YOLOv5, repulsion losses) are common; amodal detection/tracking (TAO-Amodal). Insect-inspired small-target detection exists (STMD models; the project's C8 note: STMD are tuned to dark targets at 240 Hz while night targets are headlights). FlyVis as frozen descriptor: Kuklev 2026. Motion-based proposals for small moving objects from frame differences/optical flow are classic. cannot_claim: second-look rescoring as a method, P2/high-resolution/tiling gains, motion-cue small-object detection by insect models, amodal completion. Possibly claimable only after measurement: connectome-constrained fovea as a candidate re-scorer that beats a same-input (721 raw facets) control and a YOLO re-inference control on BDD100K small/occluded/age-0 strata [uncertain].

## Результаты у авторов
YOLO26 official (COCO, YOLO11s base for STAL/ProgLoss ablations): TAL 46.6 AP / 29.0 AP_S -> STAL (s_ref = 16, boxes smaller than stride 8 enlarged only for anchor filtering) 46.8 / 29.6 (+0.2 AP, +0.6 AP_S); ProgLoss (one-to-many -> one-to-one weights 0.8/0.2 -> 0.1/0.9) 46.4 -> 46.7 E2E AP; DFL removal on YOLO26s +0.3 AP at 640 and +1.3 AP at 1280; YOLO26s at 640 ~46.0-46.3 AP, 27.3-27.9 AP_S vs at 1280 ~49.8-51.1 AP, 35.9-36.0 AP_S (resolution effect includes training at 1280). SAHI (VisDrone/xView, FCOS/VFNet/TOOD): sliced inference +6.8/+5.1/+5.3 AP, with slicing-aided fine-tuning +12.7/+13.4/+14.5 AP. RSO-YOLO (YOLOv12 nano-class base, 2.6M params, 640, 300 epochs, 10 000-image BDD100K subset): mAP50 55.2 -> 65.9; P2 head alone +4.6 mAP50 and +1.8 mAP50-95 on BDD100K; SODA10M 58.5 -> 66.5; FLIR 61.2 -> 68.4; FPS 120 -> 108. DGSS-YOLOv8s: +2.4 mAP50 on BDD100K, +4.6 on KITTI, AP_small up to +9.7 on KITTI. Blog (YOLOv8s on BDD100K): imgsz 1024 raised mAP50-95 0.232 -> 0.328 and traffic-light 0.159 -> 0.250 [uncertain: schedules and baseline not controlled]. TAO-Amodal: simple fine-tuning raises amodal detection and tracking of occluded objects by 3.3% and 2.1%; about 2x on people vs modal baselines. YOLOV (ImageNet VID, YOLOX base): 87.5 AP50 at > 30 FPS on a 2080Ti (still-image YOLOX baseline gain about +7-8 AP50 [uncertain]). Frame rate / camera motion: COCO, VisDrone, xView, SODA10M are still images; BDD100K results are frame-level detection on 720p frames from moving cars; none uses the 30 Hz stream except video detectors (ImageNet VID).

## Проверка против сильной базы
Only the resolution effect and STAL are measured on a strong, current base (Ultralytics YOLO26/YOLO11s, full COCO schedules) - but STAL is tiny (+0.6 AP_S) and already in our model. Resolution gains are robust and reproduced across the YOLO literature. P2 and occlusion modules on BDD100K come from MDPI papers with nano bases, 10k-image subsets, single runs, no seeds, and many simultaneous changes (RSO-YOLO changes neck, head and adds FECM at once; the +10.7 mAP50 jump is implausibly large for a strong base). SAHI gains are on aerial imagery; for 1280x720 driving frames already inferred at 960 the headroom is much smaller, which the project's own doubling test (+0.025) confirms. Amodal results are evaluated with amodal ground truth and would lose IoU against BDD100K modal boxes. No same-information control (e.g. the same crop re-inferred by the same detector) exists for any rescoring method; this must be built here.

## Разрез по условиям
Size: AP_S in YOLO26 ablations; SAHI small-object AP; DGSS AP_small on KITTI. Occlusion: TAO-Amodal occlusion levels; CityPersons heavy occlusion in RE-YOLOv5 (MR-2 -9.65 [secondary source]); RSO-YOLO claims occlusion but reports no occlusion-level table [uncertain]. Night: none of the small/occlusion papers splits BDD100K by time of day. First appearance: none. BDD100K-specific results exist (RSO-YOLO, DGSS-YOLOv8s, MHD-Net, blog) but only as overall mAP/class AP. The project itself has the needed strata (occluded/truncated attributes, size, night via BDD timeofday, age since birth).

## Дешёвая проверка
Gate A - resolution ceiling without training (~30-60 min GPU): run weights/baseline_seed42/best.pt at imgsz 960 (reference), 1280 and 1536 on the 200 val videos (det_cache style, conf 0.01) and with a 2x2 SAHI-style tiling at 960 per tile (overlap 0.2, merge by class-wise NMS/WBF since YOLO26 is NMS-free); report found-at-threshold for small, occluded, night and age-0 strata. This defines the inference-only control arms. Gate B - second-look rescoring (2-4 h, mostly fly compute): take candidates from runs/det_cache_ctl_960 in the band [0.01, threshold) on dev/test videos for small/occluded/age-0 strata, label each as TP/FP against GT (IoU >= 0.5), and fit nested heads with video-level cross-validation: H0 = YOLO score + class + geometry (reference AUC ~0.90); H1 = H0 + YOLO re-inference score on a 2x upscaled crop (SAHI control); H2 = H0 + raw 721-facet eye input after retina-lamina (input control); H3 = H0 + fly readout; H4 = H0 + fly with shuffled connectome; H5 = H1 + H3. Pre-registered pass: H3 - max(H1, H2) >= +0.005 AUC with video-bootstrap 95% CI > 0 on the night small+occluded band, and recall at the operating precision +1 pp or more; and H5 - H1 > 0 with CI > 0 (the fly adds over the SAHI control). Gate C - cheapest strong training arm (~3 h): fine-tune best.pt for 2 epochs at imgsz 960 vs 2 epochs at 640 (same schedule, 2 seeds each). If train@960 gives more than the fly second-look, the fly must be evaluated on top of the 960-trained model.

## Стоимость у нас
Train at 960: ~2.25x pixels -> 12 epochs ~9-10 h on the RTX 5070 with reduced batch [uncertain], 100 epochs ~80 h - the expensive part; fine-tune from best.pt at 960 for 3-5 epochs ~2.5-4 h. P2 head: +~20-30% GFLOPs [uncertain], 12 epochs ~5.5 h at 640, requires a YOLO26-P2 yaml (a P2 variant is not part of the released YOLO26 configs [uncertain]). SAHI 2x2 at 960: ~4-5x inference time (~unsuitable online, acceptable as offline control). Fly second-look: eyes on untracked candidates; measured cost of signatures was 75 ms/frame on one video and 340-390 ms/frame when 7 processes shared 12 GB, so covering the low-confidence band on 70 dev/test videos is several hours; online use would cost roughly 2-5x the detector's inference [uncertain].

## Ожидаемый эффект
Train@960 (matching inference): the largest no-fly gain, plausibly +0.01 to +0.03 night mAP50 and several pp of small-object recall, given the COCO AP_S +8 from 640 -> 1280 and that the project already harvested +3.1 MOTA at night by inferring at 960 [uncertain magnitude for BDD100K]. P2: +0.005 to +0.02 mAP50 on top [uncertain, bases in literature are weak]. SAHI tiling: small on 720p driving frames (project doubling test +0.025 small recall) at a large compute cost. Fly second-look: by analogy with the project's re-ID ladder (fly over its own input +0.006 AP) and the failed scene rescoring (AUC 0.9025 -> 0.9005), expect +0.002 to +0.006 AUC on the low-confidence band, i.e. +0.5-1.5 pp of small/occluded recall at fixed precision, DetA <= +0.3, FN -1 to -3%; probably not enough to beat train@960, but possibly additive to it.

## Риски
Comparisons that differ in more than one condition (resolution + head + schedule) are the norm in the BDD100K literature and must not be used as expected gains. Seed noise 0.0144 mAP at 12 epochs exceeds most expected deltas; the DRB sign flip at 100 epochs shows that short fine-tunes can mislead - gates are filters, not claims. High-resolution training on 12 GB may force smaller batch, changing optimisation (another condition). SAHI merging with an NMS-free model reintroduces NMS and duplicate handling, which can hurt MOTA/HOTA through double detections. Rescoring heads overfit at video level; use video-level splits only. Amodal training would reduce IoU against modal BDD boxes. A fly head that only re-learns box size/score is a geometry effect - controls H2/H4 are mandatory.
