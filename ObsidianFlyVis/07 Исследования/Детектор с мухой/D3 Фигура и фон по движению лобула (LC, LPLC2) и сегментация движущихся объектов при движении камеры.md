# D3 Фигура и фон по движению: лобула (LC, LPLC2) и сегментация движущихся объектов при движении камеры

↑ [[07 Исследования/Детектор с мухой/Детектор с мухой|Детектор с мухой]]

> Как муха отделяет объект от фона по относительному движению (figure-ground, LC-нейроны, LPLC2 — надвигание); вычислительные модели; сегментация и открытие движущихся объектов в видео вождения 2023–2026 (motion segmentation, компенсация собственного движения); может ли карта «движется не как фон» давать предложения рамок или поднимать уверенность при рождении объекта.

## Суть
The fly separates an object from the background by relative motion: wide-field motion of the whole panorama (caused by self-motion) inhibits object detectors, while local motion that differs from the surround excites them. In the lobula this is done by visual projection neurons: LC11 responds to a small (down to ~2.2 deg) object moving in any direction and is suppressed by larger objects and by panoramic motion (centre-surround inhibition); LPLC2 pools all four T4/T5 directions along four cross-shaped dendritic arms with radial excitation and inward (inhibitory, via lobula-plate LPi interneurons) opponency, so it fires only to expansion from its centre (looming) and not to translation, contraction or luminance change. In parallel the fly's tangential cells (HS/VS) estimate self-rotation and both motor and visual self-motion signals reduce LC gain during saccades. The machine-vision counterpart is moving-object segmentation under a moving camera: separate the flow into a rigid ego-motion part (camera + depth) and an independent residual, and use the residual mask as object evidence; 2023-2026 driving work (Dynamo-Depth, MOD-UV, MR-DINOSAUR, Object Concepts from Motion) uses this mainly to discover objects without labels, not to beat a supervised detector. For this project the question is narrow: can a 'moves unlike the background' score (centre-surround flow contrast, epipolar residual, looming/divergence contrast) add information that a single-frame YOLO26s does not have, so that real low-confidence candidates (threshold zone, newborn objects) are raised, or so that blind objects get a proposal.

## Место мухи
Role: re-scorer of existing low-confidence candidates and (secondary) source of proposals for blind objects - not a new detector branch first. What to take from the fly and why: (1) FlyVis stops at T4/T5, Tm, TmY - the project already verified at runtime that the model has no LC/LPLC neurons (plan, entry on [flyvis_internal_readout_v1↗](obsidian://open?vault=ObsidianFlyVis&file=01%20%D0%9C%D1%83%D1%85%D0%B0%2F%D0%AD%D0%BA%D1%81%D0%BF%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%B5%D0%BD%D1%82%D1%8B%2Fflyvis_internal_readout_v1)). So the 'lobula' must be built on top of FlyVis outputs: (a) an LPLC2-like looming unit per candidate: four arms read T4a-d/T5a-d along the cardinal directions from the candidate centre, excitation for outward motion, subtraction of inward motion (LPi opponency), output squared/rectified; this is a radial-expansion (divergence) detector that ignores translation; (b) an LC11/FD-like figure unit: local direction energy inside the box minus the same energy pooled over a wide surround (and minus a whole-frame HS/VS-like self-motion estimate obtained by wide-field integration of T4/T5), rectified - i.e. relative motion, not motion; (c) the retina-lamina front-end before the fly, which is already shown to remove the fly's night failure (flyvis_retina_stageB_v1) - without it the fly is out of distribution at night. Connectivity priors for (a)/(b) can be taken from FlyWire/Nern 2025 (which T4/T5 layers and LPi feed LPLC2; which Tm/Li feed LC11), with only a handful of trainable gains (biology prior, Zhou et al. 2022 show that even shallow trained networks recover LPLC2-like opponency). (2) Where it plugs into the detector: the detector already outputs candidates at conf 0.01; for every candidate in the threshold zone (conf from 0.01 to the stand threshold) compute the figure-ground and looming scores at its box and feed them, together with conf/class/geometry/brightness, to a tiny rescoring head (q_det for the threshold zone, and q_birth when the candidate has no track). This is the cheapest place, it keeps the frozen YOLO26s intact, and it directly targets the measured loss (21% night threshold loss, newborn objects). Only if this works should the map be pushed into the detector as a zero-initialised side input (overlaps topic D2/D4). (3) Proposals: connected blobs of high figure-ground score that overlap no candidate even at conf 0.01 are proposals for the 4.5% blind objects; at most a small gain. Why it is meaningful for the fly specifically: the fly's T4/T5 already deliver contrast-normalised ON/OFF direction maps at every column, and LPLC2/LC11-like pooling is exactly what lobula neurons do downstream of them; the step is a biologically motivated completion of FlyVis rather than an arbitrary head. Important constraint from the project's own negatives: the tiled 2x3 readout of T4/T5 energies with inside/ring contrast (column pitch 13.3 px) gave nothing for q_match, q_alive and q_birth (flyvis_internal_readout_v1, [flyvis_event_features_v1↗](obsidian://open?vault=ObsidianFlyVis&file=01%20%D0%9C%D1%83%D1%85%D0%B0%2F%D0%AD%D0%BA%D1%81%D0%BF%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%B5%D0%BD%D1%82%D1%8B%2Fflyvis_event_features_v1)), and the q_det event head with DIS overlap features gave nothing (event_heads_v1: AP 0.9190 -> 0.9199). Therefore: do not repeat a tiled inside/ring T4/T5 energy readout at tracker birth events; the new test differs in (i) target (TP vs FP among threshold-zone detector candidates, out of sample on val), (ii) explicit ego-motion model (relative motion after removing the camera component, epipolar residual, divergence contrast) and (iii) DIS-first: an information ceiling with DIS must be positive before the fly variant is built; the fly variant then must match or beat the DIS variant (or add to it with CI > 0), otherwise the fly is not the right source for this function.

## Какую потерю закрывает
Primary: THRESHOLD (candidate exists, confidence too low; 21.0% night vs 15.5% day, night under-confidence +0.098 in the 0.1-0.5 band) - motion is information a single-frame detector does not have, unlike class/geometry/scene features whose rescoring failed (AUC 0.9025 -> 0.9005); a real moving object in a dark frame still produces coherent relative motion, while a false candidate on a static texture or noise does not. Secondary: BIRTH (age 0 found 31% vs 68% at age 10+, two thirds via the threshold) - objects that enter the field of view from the side (overtaking, crossing pedestrians, cyclists) move very differently from the expanding background, so the figure-ground score is strongest exactly at entry; LPLC2-like looming contrast targets approaching objects. Weak/limited: SMALL (56.7% found) - distant small objects have sub-pixel parallax at 30 Hz and occupy at most one point of the existing sparse DIS grid (32 px pitch at full resolution), so dense flow at a larger frame gap is required and the signal is still weak; NIGHT OCCLUSION - partially occluded objects have a broken motion boundary, little expected. BLINDNESS (4.5%) - only via motion proposals; ceiling small because blind objects are mostly tiny/dark ones where flow fails too. OVEREXPOSURE - flow on saturated regions is unreliable; not targeted. Structural limit: objects that do not move relative to the world and are far away move like the background (MOD-UV: motion segmentation recalls 0.0% of small static objects; KITTI MOD has 5997 static vs 2383 moving vehicles), and a lead car moving in the same direction is degenerate for the epipolar test (it is caught only by the 'nearly static in the image while the background expands' contrast).

## Пересечение с чужими работами
Insect-inspired figure-ground, looming and small-target models applied to real video are an established line: LPLC2 models with attention for multi-target looming on 44 real UAV clips (Liu and Fu 2025), LGMD/LPLC2 collision models (Fu, Yue and colleagues), STMD models with feedback or LPTC-based ego-motion decoupling for small targets in cluttered moving backgrounds (Wang, Peng, Yue et al. 2019-2026). Connectome/LC models as part of a learned pipeline exist in neuroscience: Cowley et al. 2024 (23-unit LC bottleneck trained on behaviour), Zhou et al. 2022 (trained LPLC2-like networks), NeuroMechFly v2 (2024) reuses a connectome-constrained visual model for following another fly - the closest use of a FlyVis-like model for object pursuit. Machine-vision moving-object segmentation and motion-supervised object discovery in driving are mature (Dynamo-Depth, MOD-UV, MR-DINOSAUR, SegAnyMo, Object Concepts from Motion 2026; FuseMODNet/MODNet earlier). I found no work that builds lobula units (LPLC2/LC11-like) on top of FlyVis T4/T5 outputs and uses them to rescore threshold-zone candidates of a modern detector on BDD100K [uncertain: absence of evidence is not evidence; not a novelty argument]. cannot_claim: first bio-inspired looming or figure-ground detector; first motion-based objectness or moving-object proposals in driving; that motion cues improve detection in general (shown before, with weaker baselines); novelty because no publication was found. What could be claimed only if gates pass: a measured, controlled finding that a connectome-derived lobula completion of FlyVis supplies information about threshold-zone candidates beyond the detector and beyond the same function computed from DIS.

## Результаты у авторов
Biology (no detection metrics): LC11 responds to dark objects as small as 2.2 deg moving in any direction and is inhibited by larger objects and panoramic motion; blocking inhibition abolishes small-object selectivity (Keles and Frye 2017). LPLC2 receives input from all T4/T5 subtypes, responds to outward motion from its RF centre and not to contraction, wide-field translation or luminance change; strongly prefers dark looming (Klapoetke 2017). Escape is initiated by a fraction of the LC4/LPLC2 population (2025 connectome analysis, bioRxiv). LC gain is reduced during saccades by motor and visual self-motion signals (Turner et al. 2022). Machine vision, driving, moving camera: Dynamo-Depth (NeurIPS 2023): unsupervised motion segmentation F1 71.8% on Waymo Open, 42.8% on nuScenes overall - 57.2% day-clear vs 21.1% in other conditions (night/rain); moving-object depth AbsRel 0.194 vs 0.599 for Lite-Mono on Waymo. MOD-UV (ECCV 2024, Mask R-CNN R50, pseudo-labels from Dynamo-Depth motion masks, no human labels): Waymo box AP 12.6 vs supervised Mask R-CNN 25.7 (AP at IoU 0.5:0.95); nuScenes zero-shot AP 10.7; KITTI AP 18.0 vs HASSOD 7.5 [uncertain: exact baseline cells as extracted]; stage ablation - motion-only pseudo-labels AP 4.2 -> after Moving2Mobile 5.6 -> Large2Small 7.1 -> final 10.9; motion segmentation recalls 0.0% of small static objects, self-training recovers ~9.1% static medium and ~25.8% static large. MR-DINOSAUR (ICCVW 2025): KITTI F1@50 35.0 vs 23.2 for DIOD, TRI-PD 48.1 vs 41.5; KITTI AP50 59.7, AR50 24.7; pseudo-labels only from quasi-static frames (camera nearly still) - 13% of KITTI frames, 14% of TRI-PD, i.e. even 2025 methods avoid ego-motion rather than solve it. FuseMODNet (ICCVW 2019, simulated Dark-KITTI): moving-object segmentation mIoU 74.24% with RGB+flow, 75.3% with RGB+LiDAR flow; +10.1% relative on Dark-KITTI and +4.25% on KITTI over its own baseline (weak 2019 segmentation base). MODNet (2017): two-stream RGB+flow +21.5% relative mAP on KITTI MOD over its single-stream baseline. UAV analogue with a YOLO base (arXiv 2605.22605, 2026): homography global motion compensation + dual-interval differencing injected into YOLOv8n FPN: VisDrone-VID mAP50 23.3 -> 27.4, mAP50-95 10.4 -> 12.1; short-interval only 24.4, long-interval only 22.4; 12.28 ms motion extraction + 13.87 ms network on Jetson Orin Nano. LPLC2 attention ensemble (Liu and Fu 2025): synthetic 33 Hz stimuli and 44 real UAV clips, qualitative traces, ~7-frame lock-on latency, no precision/recall. Frame rates: Waymo/nuScenes/KITTI 10-12 Hz camera frames in these papers [uncertain], moving ego camera in all driving works.

## Проверка против сильной базы
No. None of the driving moving-object works compares against a modern supervised detector (YOLO26/RT-DETR/D-FINE class) with the same training budget: MOD-UV is unsupervised and reaches about half of a supervised Mask R-CNN R50 (AP 12.6 vs 25.7) - motion is a substitute for labels, not an addition on top of them; MODNet/FuseMODNet measure moving-object segmentation against their own weak 2017-2019 baselines; the UAV motion-guided YOLO uses YOLOv8n (mAP50 23.3) where any extra cue helps. 'Same information without the bio-model' controls are absent in the bio-inspired line (LPLC2/STMD models are compared with other bio models, not with an optical-flow residual). No independent reproductions of a motion-figure-ground gain on top of a strong detector in driving were found [uncertain]. Inside the project, the closest checks are negative: q_det head with DIS temporal-overlap features (event_heads_v1, AP 0.9190 -> 0.9199) and the post-hoc confidence correction (AUC 0.9025 -> 0.9005); but neither used relative (ego-compensated) motion, so the question is open. The required control here is explicit: the same figure-ground/looming functions computed from DIS (no fly), plus shuffled-feature and 'flow magnitude only' controls.

## Разрез по условиям
Partial and not on BDD100K. Dynamo-Depth reports nuScenes day-clear vs other conditions (night/rain): motion segmentation F1 57.2 vs 21.1 - the motion cue itself degrades strongly at night. FuseMODNet reports simulated low light (Dark-KITTI). MOD-UV reports small/medium/large and static vs moving strata (motion recalls 0% of small static objects). LC11 biology: small objects (2.2 deg) and wide-field suppression. No paper reports first-appearance (age 0), occlusion or overexposure strata, and none uses BDD100K MOT.

## Дешёвая проверка
Gate G0 - information ceiling with DIS, no fly, no training (CPU, about 2-3 h, Start-Process Idle). Data: val 200 videos, 5 Hz labelled frames (data/bdd100k/images/track/val, already local) and the existing detection cache runs/det_cache_ctl_960/val (conf 0.01, imgsz 960, same model as the stand) - out of sample for the detector, same population as [miss_ladder↗](obsidian://open?vault=ObsidianFlyVis&file=02%20%D0%AD%D0%BA%D1%81%D0%BF%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%B5%D0%BD%D1%82%D1%8B%2Fmiss_ladder). Do not use runs/train_cache for G0: those 200 videos are from the detector's training split, so threshold-zone statistics are biased. Flow: dense DIS (OpenCV, MEDIUM, at 1/2 resolution) between the labelled frame t and the previous labelled frame t-1 (0.2 s gap - larger parallax for distant objects; record that 5 Hz flow fails for very near fast objects), computed on the fly; the sparse dis_quarter grid (880 points, 32 px pitch) is too coarse for small boxes. Ego-motion model per frame: robust fundamental-matrix/FOE fit (RANSAC on flow outside all candidates with conf >= 0.25) plus a robust homography for the far field. Per candidate box (all detections with 0.01 <= conf < stand threshold) compute: (1) centre-surround relative motion |median flow inside - median flow in a ring of 1.5-2x box size| / (ring flow magnitude + eps) - LC11/FD-like; (2) epipolar residual (median Sampson distance of inside flow vectors to the frame's epipolar geometry, depth-independent) - 'moves unlike a static world'; (3) divergence contrast inside vs ring (LPLC2-like looming); (4) flow coverage/texture validity. Labels: candidate TP if it is the greedy IoU >= 0.5 match of a GT box of the same class (the THRESHOLD category of [miss_ladder↗](obsidian://open?vault=ObsidianFlyVis&file=02%20%D0%AD%D0%BA%D1%81%D0%BF%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%B5%D0%BD%D1%82%D1%8B%2Fmiss_ladder)), otherwise FP; crowd/ignore boxes excluded; age per GT from the existing birth labels. Models: logistic regression and a small HGB, base = conf + class one-hot + geometry + image brightness (the features of the failed correction) vs base + motion (1)-(4); 5-fold split by video; paired bootstrap over videos (1000). Controls: (a) base + raw flow magnitude only (no figure-ground) - shows whether the relative part matters; (b) motion features shuffled across candidates within the same frame. Pre-registered pass criteria (write into the card before running): ΔAP of TP-vs-FP in the threshold zone >= +0.010 with 95% CI lower bound > 0 on all val, and > 0 on night; at the stand threshold, recovered threshold-zone TPs >= 3% of night threshold losses with added FP <= 5% of the stand's FP; motion > control (a) with CI > 0. Report strata: night/day, age 0 vs 10+, small (< 32x32), frame-edge entry, class. Secondary G0b (same run): fraction of BLIND GT objects (no candidate at conf 0.01) covered by a figure-ground blob (IoU >= 0.3) vs the rate of such blobs on background; pass only if precision of blobs >= 0.2 [uncertain: threshold chosen ad hoc]. If G0 fails -> record completed_negative and stop the direction for detection (do not build lobula units for the detector). Gate G1 - only if G0 passes: does the fly deliver the same function? On 30 Hz data (val7 videos plus runs/train_cache dev/test 70 videos with the in-sample caveat), run FlyVis with the retina-lamina front-end on foveal eyes centred on the top-K threshold-zone candidates per frame (K ~ 20) and compute the LPLC2-like and LC11-like units from T4/T5 (built as described in fly_role, fixed weights from connectome priors, at most a few scalar gains fitted on train videos); compare with the identical functions from DIS on the same candidates. Pass: fly variant ΔAP >= DIS variant - 0.002, or fly adds over DIS with CI > 0; plus fly > shuffled-connectome control. Explicitly not a repeat of tiled 2x3 inside/ring readouts for tracker events (already negative).

## Стоимость у нас
G0: dense DIS on ~40k val labelled frame pairs at 1/2 resolution is roughly 15-40 min CPU, feature extraction and bootstrap ~1 h; total 2-3 h CPU at Idle priority, no GPU, no detector training [uncertain: DIS timing at 1/2 resolution not measured here]. G1: FlyVis foveal eyes on ~20 candidates per frame at 30 Hz over ~77 videos; the existing fovea cache took ~120 s per video together with other passes and the tiled activity cache ~19 ms/frame on GPU, so G1 is about 3-6 h GPU [uncertain]. If both gates pass, the cheapest full check needs no detector training: a rescoring head over the frozen stand (threshold-zone q_det + q_birth) evaluated with the frozen tracker on full val (HOTA/DetA/FN) - a few hours including TrackEval. Pushing the map into YOLO26s as a zero-initialised input: flow/fly maps for 1400 training videos at labelled frames (DIS ~1-2 h CPU; fly maps need 30 Hz decoding of 1281 aligned videos, extrapolating train_cache throughput ~1-2 days [uncertain]) + 12 epochs x 3 seeds ≈ 13 h (fine-tune from best.pt) + a 100-epoch confirmation ≈ 35 h. Inference cost: dense DIS at 1/2 resolution ~10-25 ms/frame CPU [uncertain]; foveal fly on K candidates is the main cost (tens of ms per frame on GPU).

## Ожидаемый эффект
Most likely small or null; a positive result is plausible only for the moving-object part of the threshold zone. Reasoning: (1) the information is genuinely new to the detector (unlike the failed class/geometry/scene correction), which is why the gate is worth running; (2) but a large share of BDD objects is static or moves with the ego vehicle (KITTI MOD: ~28% of vehicles moving), distant small objects have little parallax, and flow-based motion segmentation collapses at night (Dynamo-Depth F1 57.2 day-clear -> 21.1 other) - exactly where the threshold loss is largest; (3) the project's q_det head with DIS overlap features was flat (0.9190 -> 0.9199). Rough ceiling if G0 passes: motion-discordant objects form perhaps 20-35% of night threshold losses [uncertain]; recovering a third of them at acceptable FP would move night found-rate by ~1.5-2 pp of GT (from 69.2%), cut tracker FN by a few hundred of 15,763 (2-5%) and add ~+0.2-0.6 DetA [uncertain]; the largest relative gain would be at age 0 for frame-edge entries (overtaking vehicles, crossing pedestrians). Night mAP50 change through post-hoc rescoring is expected below the seed noise of a retrained detector (0.0031-0.0144), so mAP is not the right primary metric - use threshold-zone AP and tracker DetA/FN. Prior probability that G0 passes the pre-registered criteria: ~25-35% [uncertain]; that the fly variant then matches DIS: lower, given DIS beats decoded fly flow by an order of magnitude (4.87 vs 52.77 px) and fly features were neutral in four earlier gates.

## Риски
1) Ego-motion is the core difficulty: homography/epipolar models leave parallax residuals on near static objects and are degenerate for co-moving lead cars; 2025 methods still sidestep it by using only quasi-static frames (MR-DINOSAUR: ~13% of KITTI frames). 2) Night: flow and motion segmentation degrade strongly (Dynamo-Depth 57.2 -> 21.1 F1), so the cue may vanish where it is needed; the retina-lamina front-end helps the fly but did not help DIS (dis_retina_control_v1). 3) Resolution/time: 32 px sparse DIS grid and 30 Hz sub-pixel parallax for small far objects; 5 Hz flow fails for near fast objects. 4) Static objects are invisible to motion (MOD-UV 0% recall on small static) - expect gains only for movers. 5) Correlation with conf: moving, large, near objects already have high conf; the gate must show gain within the threshold zone and against the 'flow magnitude only' control, otherwise it is just a size/proximity proxy. 6) Biased data: runs/train_cache videos are in the detector's training split - use only for the fly-vs-DIS comparison, never for the ceiling. 7) Repeating failed tests: tiled inside/ring T4/T5 readouts at tracker events are already negative - do not re-run them under a new name. 8) DRB lessons if it reaches detector training: seed spread 0.0144 mAP at 12 epochs, sign flip at 100 epochs, a branch without gradient (check gradient norms of the zero-initialised input), +17% time; compare runs differing by one condition only, 3+ seeds, CIs. 9) Non-comparable literature: unsupervised discovery AP or segmentation mIoU are not detection gains over a supervised base; UAV gains are on YOLOv8n. 10) Licences: FlyVis MIT, OpenCV DIS Apache-2.0; FlyWire connectome data have their own terms (attribution, possibly non-commercial) [uncertain]; MOD-UV/Dynamo-Depth/SegAnyMo code licences not checked [uncertain].
