# D4 Новая модальность в обученном YOLO без вреда: адаптеры с нулевой инициализацией и слияние RGB-T

↑ [[07 Исследования/Детектор с мухой/Детектор с мухой|Детектор с мухой]]

> Адаптеры с нулевой инициализацией (ControlNet zero conv), ранняя, средняя и поздняя фузия дополнительной модальности в YOLO-детекторах; многоспектральная ночная детекция (LLVIP, FLIR, M3FD, KAIST) — какой уровень слияния работает и с каким приростом против сильной одномодальной базы; применимость к каналу мухи как псевдомодальности.

## Суть
Two ideas. (1) Zero-initialized adapters: freeze a trained network and attach a trainable side branch for the new input, joined through a 1x1 convolution whose weights and bias start at zero. At step 0 the model is exactly the original detector, so the new input can only be learned in, never 'shock' the pretrained features (ControlNet, ICCV 2023). YOLOv11-RGBT (2025) moved this into YOLO as 'multispectral controllable fine-tuning' (MCF): train the stronger single-modality detector, freeze it, inject the second modality through Zero Conv2d at mid level. (2) Fusion level for RGB-T night detection: early (channel concat at input), mid (feature concat / attention in backbone or neck), late (box/score ensembling, e.g. ProbEn). The robust finding across 2022-2025 YOLO-class results is that naive mid-fusion of two freshly trained branches often does NOT beat the best single modality (modality imbalance), while frozen-base + zero-init injection or late score fusion gives small but consistent gains (+0.3 to +0.9 AP over the strongest single modality for MCF; +3.2 AP50 for a three-detector ProbEn ensemble on FLIR). For the fly, the important caveat is that FlyVis is a pseudo-modality computed from the same RGB stream; its only genuinely new information is temporal (several past frames), not new photons as with thermal.

## Место мухи
Role: a pseudo-modality side branch (adapter) injected into the frozen trained YOLO26s best.pt through zero-initialized 1x1 convolutions, MCF-style, plus a late-fusion variant as a fallback. Concretely: (a) Run FlyVis convolutionally on the full frame rather than per track: because parameters are shared per cell type, the network is a recurrent convolution on a hexagonal lattice and can be tiled over the image at a coarse column spacing matched to the P3 grid (stride 8: 80x45 columns at 640, 120x68 at 960) [uncertain: whether flyvis code supports arbitrary lattice extent without re-implementation]. Feed it the retina-lamina front-end that already removes the fly's night failure. (b) Read out a small, interpretable set of channels: T4a-d and T5a-d (ON/OFF direction-selective motion, 8 maps) for the motion/birth signal, and L1/L2/L3 or Mi1/Tm3 (adapted contrast, 3-6 maps) for night contrast. (c) Resample to the P3 (and optionally P4) grid, pass through a light trainable stem (e.g. 2 conv layers, 32-64 channels), then a Zero Conv2d added residually to the frozen P3 feature: F_P3' = F_P3 + Z(stem(fly)). At initialisation the detector output equals the baseline exactly, which is the 'no harm' guarantee. Freeze backbone, neck and head of YOLO26s (or unfreeze only the head in a second stage). (d) Late-fusion fallback (ProbEn-like): keep the detector untouched and use fly evidence per candidate box from the existing conf>=0.01 cache to re-score candidates (logistic or tiny MLP on [detector score, fly features inside the box and ring]). Why it is sensible: the dominant loss is the THRESHOLD loss (candidate exists, confidence too low, more so at night), and age-0 objects lose 2/3 of their gap through the threshold; a frozen single-frame detector has no temporal evidence, while T4/T5 encode motion relative to the background from several frames, which is exactly what a newly appearing or dimly lit moving object adds. Caveat to state openly: unlike thermal, the fly adds no new photons; the gain can only come from temporal integration and the fly's nonlinear adaptation, so the decisive control is the same frames through a non-biological module (frame difference, DIS flow magnitude/direction, a conv-GRU of equal size, and the topology-shuffled fly).

## Какую потерю закрывает
Primary: THRESHOLD loss (21.0% night vs 15.5% day; night confidence deficit +0.098). A zero-conv residual on P3 mainly shifts logits of existing candidates; in the RGB-T literature the gains of MCF are in mAP50-95 rather than in new recalls (FLIR YOLOv11s: AP50 77.35 -> 77.38-77.76, AP 43.44 -> 43.71-44.20), consistent with a confidence/localisation refinement mechanism. Secondary: BIRTH (age 0: 31% vs 68% found), because T4/T5 respond to motion onset that a single frame lacks; but ego-motion of the BDD camera produces dense background motion, so the gain is expected mostly for objects moving relative to the flow field (crossing pedestrians, overtaking cars), not for static objects revealed by ego-motion. Weak or none: BLINDNESS (4.5%, equal day and night) - the RGB-T literature shows recall gains from new photons (thermal), which the fly cannot provide; only early/mid fusion that changes proposal maps could touch it. Small objects (56.7% found): a P3-level injection is the right scale, but FlyVis column spacing (one ommatidium ~ several pixels after tiling) limits sub-column objects [uncertain]. Night occlusion (5.4x penalty): no direct mechanism. Overexposure: lamina adaptation could help contrast in saturated regions, unverified.

## Пересечение с чужими работами
Zero-initialized injection of an extra input into a frozen detector is not new: ControlNet (2023) introduced zero convolution; YOLOv11-RGBT MCF (2025) applies exactly this to YOLO with infrared; a 2025 adverse-weather paper side-loads a CNN into a frozen YOLO11n via zero-conv; UniRGB-IR (2024) uses adapters into a frozen RGB foundation model. Late score fusion (ProbEn, 2022) and illumination-derived pseudo-channels from the same RGB (YOLA, NeurIPS 2024) are also published. Bio-inspired motion front-ends (Hassenstein-Reichardt EMDs, STMD models) exist for small-target detection but, in the searches made, as standalone models rather than as a channel injected into a modern YOLO. No published work was found that uses the connectome-constrained FlyVis model (or any connectome-constrained optic-lobe model) as a full-frame input channel/adapter of a trained detector [uncertain: search not exhaustive, arXiv 2026 not fully covered]. cannot_claim: the zero-init adapter, mid-level fusion, the 'no harm at init' property and late score fusion are borrowed; one cannot claim novelty of the fusion mechanism. Possible claim, only if gates pass: the fly (connectome-constrained recurrent model with the retina-lamina front-end) as a pseudo-modality whose contribution exceeds an equal-information non-biological control.

## Результаты у авторов
YOLOv11-RGBT (arXiv 2506.14696, COCO-pretrained YOLOv11, single run per setting, no seed variance reported): FLIR (aligned, car-mounted thermal+RGB video frames, mixed day/night): YOLOv11s RGB-only AP50 66.19 / AP 33.29; IR-only 77.35 / 43.44; three-node mid-fusion 76.64 / 41.85 (worse than IR-only); P3 mid-fusion 79.09 / 42.62; MCF (frozen IR model + Zero Conv2d RGB) 77.38-77.76 / 43.71-44.20 depending on optimizer; YOLOv11x MCF best 83.48 / 47.61 vs IR-only 80.27 / 44.43. LLVIP (static surveillance cameras, night pedestrians): YOLOv11s RGB 89.84 / 53.29, IR 97.55 / 67.58, mid-fusion 96.84 / 66.02 (worse than IR), P3 97.39 / 67.99, MCF 96.74 / 68.45 (+0.87 AP over IR); YOLOv11x IR 97.41 / 69.93 vs MCF 97.06 / 70.26 (+0.33 AP, -0.35 AP50). M3FD (car-mounted, 6 classes): YOLOv11s RGB-only 84.67 / 58.51 vs best fusion (mid) 84.91 / 58.47, early 83.85 / 57.75, late 84.63 / 58.02, score fusion 83.14 / 56.99 - i.e. against a strong RGB baseline, fusion gives ~0 and some levels hurt; YOLOv11m early fusion 87.06 / 61.29 beat mid 86.71 / 60.67. ProbEn (ECCV 2022, Faster R-CNN family, FLIR, AP50): thermal-only 79.24, early fusion 78.80, mid fusion 80.53, NMS-style max+argmax late fusion 83.14, ProbEn3 (thermal + early + mid detectors, v-avg boxes) 83.76 (night 85.56); KAIST LAMR prior art 6.48 -> ProbEn++ 5.14. Note the ProbEn gain partly is an ensemble effect of three detectors. UniRGB-IR (frozen ViT + adapters): LLVIP mAP50 96.1 / mAP 63.2, FLIR-aligned 81.4 / 44.1 (not directly comparable baseline). YOLA (pseudo-channel derived from RGB, illumination-invariant module): +2.3 mAP on ExDark and +4.8 on UG2+ DARK FACE with TOOD (older baseline, still images). Adverse-weather zero-conv side-load: +3.8 to +9.5 mAP50 on NVD over a frozen YOLO11n (weak frozen baseline) [uncertain: exact setup].

## Проверка против сильной базы
Partly. YOLOv11-RGBT compares against same-size YOLOv11 single-modality models trained with the same recipe, which is a reasonably strong and fair baseline, but there is no seed variance, so +0.3 to +0.9 AP from MCF is within the size of typical seed noise for small detectors (our DRB seed spread was 0.0144 mAP at 12 epochs) and not statistically established. The striking negative fact is robust across their tables: freshly trained two-branch mid-fusion is often worse than the best single modality (FLIR, LLVIP) and ~equal on M3FD - i.e. naive fusion does not survive a strong single-modality baseline. ProbEn's gain is large but confounded with ensembling three detectors (no equal-compute ensemble of three thermal-only detectors as control). No paper found that controls for 'same information without the model' for a pseudo-modality derived from RGB; YOLA-type works compare to the bare detector, not to an equal-capacity generic layer. No independent reproductions of MCF found [uncertain]. Thermal/IR results do not transfer in sign to a pseudo-modality: thermal adds independent photons, the fly does not.

## Разрез по условиям
ProbEn reports FLIR day/night split (ProbEn3 night AP50 85.56 vs day 82.21; thermal-only night 82.90 vs day 75.35) and KAIST day/night LAMR. LLVIP is night-only. YOLOv11-RGBT gives no condition split. No small-object, occlusion or first-appearance breakdown in any of the fusion works found; no BDD100K results (BDD100K has no thermal channel). YOLA reports only low-light datasets overall.

## Дешёвая проверка
Gate 0 (no training of the detector, hours): candidate re-scoring on the existing cache runs/det_cache_ctl_960 (baseline seed42, 960, all 200 val videos, conf>=0.01). Restrict to val7 or a 20-40 video night-heavy subset where fly signals can be computed in reasonable time (foveal fly ~150-390 ms/frame per process per the plan). For every cached candidate, compute fly features from the full-frame or candidate-centred fly run over the previous 6-10 frames at 30 Hz: mean and max of T4a-d/T5a-d activity in the box minus a surrounding ring (relative motion), plus lamina contrast. Fit, with video-level cross-validation, logistic regression on (i) detector score only, (ii) score + fly features, (iii) score + equal-information controls: frame difference energy, DIS flow magnitude/direction residual vs ring, (iv) score + topology-shuffled fly. Pre-registered pass criteria: (1) AUC(ii) - AUC(i) >= +0.005 with video-bootstrap 95% CI excluding 0 (the earlier class/geometry/scene correction moved AUC 0.9025 -> 0.9005, so the bar is 'new information', not calibration); (2) AUC(ii) - max(AUC(iii)) >= +0.003, CI excluding 0; (3) at the operating threshold, recall of night age-0 objects at fixed FP count rises by >= +3 percentage points; (4) shuffled fly loses >= half of the gain. If (1) passes but (2) fails, the motion signal is real but not fly-specific - then use DIS/frame difference as the channel and drop the fly claim. Gate 1 (one short training, <= 1 day): only if Gate 0 passes, train the zero-conv P3 adapter on a frozen best.pt for 3 epochs on a subset, 3 seeds, and check that the zero-conv weight norm grows and the ratio ||Z(stem(fly))|| / ||F_P3|| is > 1e-2 (branch actually used) and night mAP50 does not fall below 0.3772 - 0.0031.

## Стоимость у нас
Fly features: full-frame FlyVis is the dominant cost. Per-track foveal fly (721 facets) already runs at ~150-390 ms/frame per process; a full-frame tiling at P3 resolution has ~3,600-8,200 columns, i.e. ~5-11x one eye, so offline precomputation over BDD100K MOT train (1,400 videos x ~200 labelled frames at 5 Hz, each needing ~6-10 preceding 30 Hz frames) is on the order of days on the RTX 5070 [uncertain: estimate, depends on batching of the hex convolution]; storage ~10-30 GB at float16 for 12-16 channels on the P3 grid [uncertain]. A cheaper path is a subset (e.g. 300 videos) or a GPU re-implementation of the fly as a plain hex-conv RNN. Training: with the YOLO26s backbone/neck/head frozen and only the stem + zero-conv trainable, a 12-epoch run should cost less than the 4.3 h full run (backward still passes through neck/head but no weight updates) [uncertain]; 3 seeds are required, ~10-13 h. Unfreezing the head after stage 1: +12 epochs, ~4.3 h per seed. 100-epoch confirmation (~35 h) only if the 12-epoch effect exceeds 2x the seed spread. Inference: +1 small conv stem at P3 is negligible; the fly itself adds tens to hundreds of ms per frame unless re-implemented efficiently. Gate 0 re-scoring: a few hours on val7/subset using cached detections.

## Ожидаемый эффект
Modest. Literature priors: with a truly new modality, zero-init MCF over a strong single-modality YOLO gives +0.3 to +0.9 AP (mAP50-95) and ~0 to +0.4 AP50; naive fusion gives ~0 or negative. A pseudo-modality from the same RGB should give less than thermal. Derived-from-RGB channels (YOLA) give +2.3 mAP on ExDark but against an older baseline. For us: night mAP50 change of 0 to +0.01 (vs control 0.3772 +- 0.0031) is the realistic range [uncertain]; if it appears it should be visible mainly as fewer THRESHOLD losses (a few percentage points of the 21.0% night threshold loss) and higher age-0 night recall, not as lower blindness (4.5%). Translated to the tracker, since DetA was flat at ~58.2 and FN = 15,763, even a 3-5% FN reduction would be +0.3 to +0.6 DetA [uncertain]. A larger gain would require the motion signal to rescue birth frames, which Gate 0 directly measures.

## Риски
1) Lazy branch / no gradient (the DRB lesson): with a frozen strong base, the optimiser can keep the zero-conv near zero because the RGB path already explains the loss (modality imbalance, OGM-GE). Monitor zero-conv weight norm and contribution ratio per epoch; consider gradient modulation or night-only sample weighting. 2) Seed noise: DRB spread 0.0144 mAP at 12 epochs exceeds the expected effect; freezing the base reduces but does not remove variance, so >= 3 seeds and CI are mandatory; do not transfer a 12-epoch sign to 100 epochs (DRB sign flipped). 3) Time overhead: DRB cost +17%; full-frame fly can cost far more at inference - report latency honestly. 4) Incomparable comparisons: a frozen best.pt + adapter vs a fully retrained YOLO differ in more than one factor; the fair comparison is frozen best.pt + adapter(fly) vs frozen best.pt + adapter(control input of equal channels/params) vs frozen best.pt fine-tuned for the same steps with no extra input. 5) Information-equivalence: frame-difference or DIS may explain all of the gain (already seen: the retina helps the fly but not DIS); then the fly claim must be dropped. 6) Ego-motion: BDD camera motion floods T4/T5 with background motion; relative motion (box minus ring) features are needed. 7) Licences: Ultralytics and YOLOv11-RGBT are AGPL-3.0 (code derived from them inherits AGPL), ControlNet Apache-2.0, flyvis MIT, YOLA without a licence file (do not copy code).
